# Avalor Artwork — Wave 8: Kingdom, Memory, and Wonder

**Prepared:** 13 November 2026. Checked directly against the current 132-article public encyclopedia. **All ten entries exist and had no `image:` assignment at selection.** The ten Wave 7 history images were already assigned to their articles; current completed GitHub Pages publishing runs were successful.

## Why these ten

Wave 7 illustrated the Misfits' campaign. Wave 8 expands the identity of the Archives: the united kingdom and the lands beyond it; the long shadow of the Black Tower; the correction of the Betrayer legend; the everyday faith, magic and people of Avalor. The intent is breadth and historical meaning rather than supplying generic images to short entries.

## Art direction and publication rules

- **Kingdoms & Places:** 16:9 landscape (~1920 × 1080), displayed above the manuscript in the wide article plate and eligible for automatic Locations Gallery indexing.
- **History & Chronicles / Faith, Magic & Cosmology:** 2:3 vertical (~1200 × 1800), displayed in the article sidebar. These do not automatically appear in Locations Gallery.
- **Overall style:** Realistic painterly fantasy, naturalistic materials, fine atmospheric detail, plausible architecture, and rich but restrained color. Text, logos and new heraldry should not be baked into artwork.
- **Evidence handling:** Aethrion has no securely located ruins or eyewitness account. The Blood Sickness has no verified symptom list or transmission mechanism. The Emerald lacks an approved exact gem design. Treat these images as **artist's interpretations**, not archival proof.
- **Public-safety boundary:** The images should not reveal future stories, previously hidden motives, the identity of powers behind the Beyond, or other Keeper-only information. The existence of an encyclopedia entry does not authorize adding revelations absent from its public historical account.

## 1. The Kingdom of Avalor — Many Peoples, One Realm

- **Source article:** https://yoshi3309.github.io/avalor-wiki/articles/kingdom-of-avalor/
- **Article category:** Kingdoms & Places
- **Image at selection:** None
- **Recommended composition:** 16:9 landscape
- **What the image establishes:** A wide view of the shared realm, distinct from the Welcome banner and Renada illustration

### Copy-ready image generation prompt

Create an expansive 16:9 landscape illustration for the encyclopedia article THE KINGDOM OF AVALOR, in the established realistic painterly fantasy style of a finely produced illustrated royal history. From a believable rural crossroads, show a small mixed-ancestry group of ordinary travelers, merchants and families passing peacefully between cultivated fields, an ancient forest edge and a distant river road; the great mountains rise far off under a pale morning sky. A modest stone waymarker may stand at the crossroads, but without readable lettering, royal insignia or fabricated geographical names. The emotional focus is the northern union founded in 1 AF: communities whose earlier histories remain distinct, bound by shared roads, institutions and mutual responsibility rather than a homogeneous people. Subtle evidence of different crafts and travel traditions should feel natural, never stereotyped. Give the whole image an inviting sense of space, dignity and lived experience, with warm sunlight, slate-blue distance, moss greens and realistic stone textures. Do not depict the four sheriffdoms as adjacent surveyed regions, invent royal borders, relocate actual landmarks, duplicate a precise view of Renada, invent a coronation or insert identifiable named characters. No text. This is a representative painter's scene, not an authenticated geographic panorama.

### Copy-ready image generation prompt

Create a vertical 2:3 painterly fantasy historical illustration titled in concept AETHRION: THE CIVILIZATION WITHOUT A MAP, but do NOT put any words in the image. This is an enigmatic scholarly image about an ancient society known only through uncertain traditions of extraordinary learning, an attempt to master mortality and sudden disappearance. In the warm half-light of a royal research chamber, arrange several fragile and conflicting old manuscript fragments on an examination table beneath a small glass lens. Perhaps a slight reflection hints at impossible architecture, but never display identifiable ruins, a confirmed city, ancient technology, a divine judge or a portal as factual evidence. The Royal Commission has no authenticated location, chronology, eyewitness account or ruins for Aethrion; this must be a study of historical uncertainty, not a literal reconstruction. Rich charcoal, parchment gold and blue-gray palette, elegant fine brushwork, philosophical unease, generous vertical composition. No legible text, celestial figure, catastrophe spectacle, named scholar portraits, official seals or invented artifacts.

## 4. The Betrayer's Legend — A History Written Wrong

- **Source article:** https://yoshi3309.github.io/avalor-wiki/articles/betrayers-legend/
- **Article category:** History & Chronicles
- **Image at selection:** None
- **Recommended composition:** 2:3 vertical
- **What the image establishes:** How an old story vilified Jaznaar, without depicting him as actually guilty

### Copy-ready image generation prompt

Create a vertical 2:3 realistic painterly archival illustration about THE BETRAYER'S LEGEND, the false story that cast Jaznaar as a traitor and celebrated Aldus Greystone III as a savior. On an old scholar's desk, show a weathered illustrated chronicle with an intentionally indistinct painted figure in a dark cloak, flanked by other copied historical pages and a newer evidence packet awaiting examination. Let a strong shaft of daylight fall across the newer records, contrasting with the deeply shadowed, older story. The visual idea is that generations of repetition once gave a deliberate falsehood an air of authority. Do not portray the accused drow as actually committing treason, label an entire ancestry dangerous, invent a specific portrait of Jaznaar or Aldus, depict a confirmed Black Tower scene, or introduce a fictional written confession. The founders themselves knew Jaznaar was innocent, while the hostile legend took hold in later generations; do not suggest the original Six believed it. Keep all writing undecipherable. No text, named heraldry or invented royal proclamation. Rich old-master brushwork, historically thoughtful atmosphere, restrained charcoal and amber colors.

In a shadowed stone workroom, focus upon an abandoned scholar-healer's table with a cracked glass vessel, surgical or alchemical instruments of plausible medieval craftsmanship, scattered unreadable research papers and a dark red-black glimmer spilling over the edge of a shallow basin. ### Copy-ready image generation prompt

Create a solemn, hopeful vertical 2:3 realistic painterly royal historical illustration of THE VINDICATION OF JAZNAAR in Avalor, after the Misfits' defeat of Cid. Depict a quiet moment in a stone archival hall rather than an invented royal ceremony: on a table, a carefully removed weathered memorial plaque rests beside a newer, uninscribed replacement tablet and a sealed stack of historical records, while warm daylight breaks through a high window. The visual idea is that old evidence was preserved even as the kingdom publicly corrected an unjust accusation. In Renada's Rynhold Park the statue of Aldus remained, its old inscription was removed, and the original plaque was preserved at the Great Library; this scene does not need to depict the exact Park sculpture or its unconfirmed lettering. No unapproved likenesses of Gradvum, Cid or Jaznaar; no invented monument design, legible proclamation, text, heraldry or dramatic trial. Quiet gravity, aged stone, parchment, soft gold light and meticulous painterly realism.

### Copy-ready image generation prompt

Create a dignified vertical 2:3 realistic painterly fantasy illustration depicting the many peoples of Avalor through everyday civic life. In a lively but believable market square in a town of the northern realm, show a mixed community of humans, elves, drow, dwarves, dragonborn, firbolgs and other fantasy ancestries as neighbors, merchants, students, artisans, families and travelers. Do not create specific royal buildings, territorial claims, invented insignia, named figures, readably lettered market signs or a coronation. An illustrative social portrait of a realm, not a literal census or costume guide.

1. Render each image using the corresponding article and this prompt. Confirm any character design or named architectural detail against existing approved imagery.
2. Save a filename such as `aethrion-unrecorded-history.png`; submit it through Pages CMS and assign it to the named article, using truthful alt text and an interpretive caption.
3. Avoid presenting invented archaeology, terrain relationships, emblems, magic rules or ritual practices as new canon. Revisions should be reviewed before publication.
4. Expect two location artwork assignments to join the Locations Gallery automatically; the other eight display in their article sidebars.
5. Check the latest GitHub Actions result after each group of uploads. A UI 'fetch error' does not prove the image failed to reach GitHub; inspect the actual repository and publishing checks before retrying.
