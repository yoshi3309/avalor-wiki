# Avalor Artwork — Wave 5: Ten Source-Linked Illustrations

Reviewed 11 November 2026 against published articles. All ten selected article records had no assigned image when reviewed. The previous Wave 4 includes unillustrated entries still awaiting artwork; this is the NEXT selection, not a claim that Wave 4 has already been completed. The first three Wave 4 locations, Port Orange, Tarnapolus and Stems Port, had received assigned images. Osmostown, East Port, Lord Osmo's Tower, The Aegis, Ringtree Clan, Cathedral Paladins and Heureux's Bard Academy were still awaiting image assignments at the time of this review.

Use 16:9 landscape for Kingdoms & Places and 2:3 vertical for the History & Chronicles and Organizations & Factions entries, because the latter categories retain the portrait-style article sidebar. All illustrations are interpretive, not purported documentary evidence; avoid invented official heraldry, names, rituals, quotations, maps or hidden lore.

## 1. Tinsel

- Wiki article: https://yoshi3309.github.io/avalor-wiki/articles/tinsel/
- Composition: 16:9 landscape
- Historical focus: A peaceful trading port defined by its former rivalry with Cove City and the kraken trade.

**Image-generation prompt**

Create a richly detailed 16:9 realistic painterly fantasy landscape of Tinsel, an established northern Avalorian port on the King's Road and a commercial partner of neighboring Cove City. From a waterfront viewpoint, show a working harbor with weathered stone quays, commercial sailing ships, fish and goods being unloaded, merchants and crews engaged in ordinary trade, and the coast stretching into the distance. Its centuries-long feud with Cove City ended with the unification of Avalor, so the image should convey the prosperity of peaceful maritime cooperation rather than an active war. Small hints of a specialized seafaring industry may suggest the established kraken hunting trade, but DO NOT depict a kraken or invent specific hunting equipment, official uniforms or harborside monuments. Natural sea light, warm stone, muted sailcloth, fine brushwork. No invented banners, readable inscriptions, modern ships or exaggerated fantasy magic.

## 2. River City

- Wiki article: https://yoshi3309.github.io/avalor-wiki/articles/river-city/
- Composition: 16:9 landscape
- Historical focus: Modest but growing fishing harbor feeding the capital and the Royal Heartlands.

**Image-generation prompt**

Create a 16:9 realistic painterly fantasy illustration of River City, a smaller but steadily growing fishing port in Avalor, known for supplying fresh fish to Renada and the Royal Heartlands. The image should celebrate everyday work rather than royal spectacle: modest timber-and-stone docks, fishing boats returning with their catch, a few local workers sorting baskets of fresh fish, market carts, and simple waterfront homes. Show a practical harbor and a community whose prosperity comes from patient labor, with gentle early-morning sunlight on the water, natural textures and atmospheric depth. The city should not look like an immense naval base, a wealthy freeport, or an invented capital. Avoid identifiable named figures, large military vessels, new historical monuments, logos, legible signs or modern machinery. Human-scale, warm, detailed archival landscape painting.

Dramatic overcast light, human-scale emotion, historically grounded armor and a sober painterly finish. Do not assign recognizable appearances to Rynhold, Osmo, Bradgem or other named figures; do not show a specific formation at Heroes Hill, portray Osmo as magical or undead, invent official national heraldry or include written text. Restrained warm daylight, expressive but subtle gestures, wood and stone architecture, historical gravitas. ## 5. The Sundering of the Six

- Wiki article: https://yoshi3309.github.io/avalor-wiki/articles/sundering-of-the-six/
- Composition: 2:3 vertical
- Historical focus: The fracture of the founding fellowship over Aldus's dangerous experiments.

**Image-generation prompt**

Create a vertical 2:3 realistic painterly fantasy illustration expressing the Sundering of the Six, the tragic rupture in Avalor's founding fellowship after the other five companions learned of Aldus Greystone III's forbidden experiments. Rather than attempting to recreate a precise undocumented confrontation, depict an empty stone chamber at the base of a dark mountain tower, with six extinguished or fading candlelights arranged across a table, abandoned travel equipment, and cold light separating the objects. The room should communicate divided loyalty, grief, difficult moral choices and a fellowship that cannot return to what it was. Deep slate, charcoal and muted amber tones, restrained realistic painting, compelling negative space and symbolic composition. Do not depict the ritual itself, reveal secret magic, invent quotations, show a forged portrait of any of the Six, or imply Jaznaar betrayed his friends. No text, runes or invented heraldry.

## 7. The Hunters Guild

- Composition: 2:3 vertical
- Historical focus: Faelynn's hunters protecting people across the dangerous reaches of the Dead Marshes.

**Image-generation prompt**

Create a vertical 2:3 realistic painterly fantasy illustration of the Hunters Guild of Avalor, founded by Faelynn Naïlo and based at Wayward Sun deep in the Dead Marshes. Show an anonymous small team of skilled scouts and rangers guiding a weary civilian traveler along a narrow, uncertain marshland path between reeds and dark channels, their equipment practical: cloaks, bows, cord, boots and travel packs. Their emphasis is guidance, protection and knowledge of difficult country, not indiscriminate monster hunting. In the distance, subtle wooden walkways may hint at a sheltered refuge without inventing its architecture. Morning mist, muted green and brown colors, natural light, documentary attention to material culture. Do not depict Faelynn or any named Misfit with an invented likeness, show an unknown monster, invent guild insignia, include military banners, write signs or assert a specific historical rescue mission.

- Composition: 2:3 vertical
- Historical focus: A 34-sovereign dynasty remembered through its archival register, not an invented portrait gallery.

**Image-generation prompt**

Create a vertical 2:3 realistic painterly fantasy illustration evoking House Frozenroar, Avalor's royal dynasty from Rynhold Frozenroar to the present king. In a grand but grounded royal archive or ceremonial hall, depict a long processional arrangement of aged royal ledgers, empty stone niches, family history scrolls, and subtle sculptural lion motifs in worn stone. A succession of candlelit archways recedes into depth, suggesting generations of rulers without showing unapproved faces, crowns, specific armor or invented genealogical positions. The documented royal register contains thirty-four successive sovereigns; do not depict or numerically imply a different count. Avoid an invented family tree, recognizable portraits, heraldic flags with new symbols, readable names or specific crown regalia.
