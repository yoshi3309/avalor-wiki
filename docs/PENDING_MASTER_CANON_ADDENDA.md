## Confirmed addition: Kel'Maris, the Lion's Shadow

- **Name:** Kel'Maris.
- **Species:** High elf; first elf to become Lord Marshal.
- **Age:** 85 in the supplied backstory (the exact date at which this age was recorded is not specified).
- **Family and origin:** First daughter of a prominent elven family from the village of Ilyanamel.
- **Martial history:** Training across multiple weapons from early childhood; her preferred two-weapon fighting style paired a longsword with a shortsword and became known as the **Kel Scythe**, particularly devastating to shield formations.
- **Avalor Games:** Placed among the top five swordsmen many times; won the championship twice.
- **Relationship to the crown:** Sent to Renada at King Gerran's order to teach the young Prince Gradvum swordsmanship. Became his friend, joined Lion's Bane, quickly reached second in command beneath Lord Marshal **Kurt Reyginald**, and served as close personal guard to the king. Her constant presence earned the epithet **Lion's Shadow**.
- **Office:** Succeeded Kurt Reyginald upon his retirement; was already Lord Marshal at Gradvum's accession. Commissioned the Misfits against the first unnamed necromancer and later the cult of Gol'Goroth.
- **Stanleyville:** Survived the earlier Hydra temple disaster. Later the Stalker pierced her chest and killed her while she accompanied the Misfits in Stanleyville.
- **Funeral:** Body returned to Renada, state funeral, three-day lying in state at the cathedral, private cremation according to her wishes, ashes scattered in the King's Forest.
- **Succession:** Office left vacant until Gradvum appointed Lavitz after the defeat of Cid.

### Literal creator-submitted account (retained for master-document reconciliation)

> Lord Marshall of the Lions Bane. Young by elven standards, Kel'Maris was the first of her kind to become the Lord Marshall of the Lions Bane. A spry High elf at the young age of 85, Kel'Maris has been around for many years of the Frozenroar reign. Hailing from the elven village of Ilyanamel, Kel'Maris travelled to Renada by order of King Gerran in order to be the sword instructor for to his son, a young Gradvum who was set to inherit the throne. Kel'Maris became great friends through the years with Gradvum and she readily agreed to join the Lions Bane, the King's personal guard. She quickly rose through the ranks and became second in command, directly below the Lord Marshall Kurt Reyginald. She was assigned as the personal guard to the King, always within ear shot. Due to her continuous presence, Kel'Maris gaining the nickname "Lions Shadow". She succeeded Kurt upon his retirement as the Lord Marshall and held that position when Gradvum took the throne.

> Kel'maris was the first daughter of a prominent Elven family. Starting at a very young age, she trained in various forms of weaponry. Her most skilled form was a dual wielding form using a long sword and a short sword. The form became known as the Kel Scythe for its devastating ability to break a shield formation. When she moved to the capital to be Gradvum's teacher, she entered the Avalor games every year, placing as one of the top five swordsman in the kingdom many times. Twice she had beat out every combatant and won it all.

> As Lord Marshall, Kel'Maris accompanied the group that will become known as the Misfits on their hunt for the immortal called Cid. While at Stanleyville, Kel'maris fell when the creature pierced her chest. Captain Funkin was able to safely bring back her body to Renada. She was honored with a state funeral and her body was gracefully displayed at the Grand Cathedral for three days. Her body was burned in a private ceremony in accordance to her wishes, her ashes scattered across the Kings Forest.

## Open continuity issue KEL-001: Captain Funkin and the return of the body

**Account A (previous canon, published wiki and four reference PDFs):** Captain Funkin died at the Hydra temple, before the later events in Stanleyville. Kel'Maris and Anna survived the temple.  
**Account B (new creator-supplied story):** Captain Funkin safely recovered Kel'Maris's body after the Stalker killed her at Stanleyville and returned it to Renada.  
**Conflict:** The same individual cannot have died earlier and personally performed that recovery afterward without a new explanatory event. No such event has been established.  
**Current public editorial treatment:** State that Kel'Maris's body was returned to Renada without naming the bearer. Keep both original facts in this pending addendum. **Status: OPEN - needs creator resolution.**
