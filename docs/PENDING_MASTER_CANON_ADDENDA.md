## Confirmed addition: Kel'Maris, the Lion's Shadow

- **Name:** Kel'Maris.
- **Species:** High elf; first elf to become Lord Marshal.
- **Age:** 85 in the supplied backstory (the exact date at which this age was recorded is not specified).
- **Family and origin:** First daughter of a prominent elven family from the village of Ilyanamel.
- **Martial history:** Training across multiple weapons from early childhood; her preferred two-weapon fighting style paired a longsword with a shortsword and became known as the **Kel Scythe**, particularly devastating to shield formations.
- **Avalor Games:** Placed among the top five swordsmen many times; won the championship twice.
- **Relationship to the crown:** Sent to Renada at King Gerran's order to teach the young Prince Gradvum swordsmanship. Became his friend, joined Lion's Bane, quickly reached second in command beneath Lord Marshal **Kurt Reyginald**, and served as close personal guard to the king. Her constant presence earned the epithet **Lion's Shadow**.
- **Office:** Succeeded Kurt Reyginald upon his retirement; was already Lord Marshal at Gradvum's accession. Commissioned the Misfits against the first unnamed necromancer and later the cult of Gol'Goroth.
- **Stanleyville:** The Stalker pierced Kel'Maris through the chest and killed her **before the Hydra temple disaster**. Captain Funkin survived the Stalker encounter and carried her body back to Renada. He subsequently died at the Hydra temple alongside Inspector Sihnion and the guards.
- **Funeral:** Body returned to Renada, state funeral, three-day lying in state at the cathedral, private cremation according to her wishes, ashes scattered in the King's Forest.
- **Succession:** Office left vacant until Gradvum appointed Lavitz after the defeat of Cid.

## Confirmed addition - JAARIS-002: The Master of Broken Wills (November 12, 2026)

**Status: APPROVED CANON - publication permitted.**

**Creator direction:** Jaaris's fundamental motivation is **control and domination**. The three recommended elaborations were expressly approved: (1) gifted self-made scholar, (2) gaining leadership of the hostile fey through magical bargains, compulsions and intimidation, and (3) consuming Tristan and Kristofer's life energy himself through a necromantic ritual. Unlike important recurring figures, short-lived villains and side characters should normally receive only **a couple of meaningful paragraphs**, not encyclopedic biographies of every year.

### Origin and philosophy
Jaaris is a dark-haired tiefling with gray skin marked by necrotic corruption. Born among mortal settlements near the Feywild, he was a magically gifted, self-taught scholar whose primary ambition was absolute obedience. He explored charms, oaths and compulsions before deepening his necromancy, drawn by the possibility that the dead need never disobey. His repeated necrotic experiments marked his body. He believed freedom to refuse was a fundamental defect in the living.

### The hostile fey
As an outsider rather than a fey elf, Jaaris obtained power over one hostile faction of fey elves by offering binding magic, then consolidating authority with coercive bargains, compulsions and fear. This is a particular faction, not all fey or the Feywild. Some followed from shared cruelty, others because they feared defying him.

### Sven, Tristan and Kristofer
Sven had made a family with Tristan and Kristofer. Under Jaaris's orders, the hostile watchers deliberately withdrew near the end of their fourth year together so Sven would lower his guard. When Sven left to seek a birthday gift for Kristofer, the fey burned their home and captured the family. Jaaris personally consumed Tristan and Kristofer's life energy in the ritual while Sven, magically bound, was forced to witness it. He also attempted to take Sven's soul, but some **unidentified force or circumstance stopped him**; do not invent the intervention's identity. Jaaris acted from contempt for freely chosen love. **He carried out this crime independently of Cid**, before entering the Blood King's service.

### Cid, Hydra and Fo'Gurn
After the tragedy, Jaaris became Cid's servant because their ambitions aligned: Cid sought to overcome mortality; Jaaris wanted to overcome free will. He was not the unnamed necromancer defeated during the first royal commission. After the Stalker killed Kel'Maris, Jaaris was among Cid's servants at the later ruined temple, where he formed a Hydra from the failed ritual's sacrificial mass. Fo'Gurn truly died buying the company's escape. Jaaris subsequently restored him by necromancy and compelled him as a weapon against Lavitz and the Misfits, using the paladin's bonds as a test of absolute control.

### Defeat and remaining limits
At the Shadowfell fortress, Lavitz lowered his defenses rather than destroy Fo'Gurn. **Fo'Gurn chose to protect Lavitz and broke Jaaris's hold before Jaaris died.** Jaaris's subsequent death caused the unnatural existence supporting Fo'Gurn to fail; it did not create his moral freedom. How Jaaris acquired each particular spell, any personal association with Karina beyond service to Cid, and the precise force that prevented Sven's soul from being consumed remain **UNESTABLISHED**.

### Preservation and publication
- **Wiki:** expanded `content/articles/jaaris.md` and related Sven/Cid accounts.
- **Master Canon Bible:** add independent fey crime, character philosophy, post-tragedy Cid allegiance, Hydra/Fo'Gurn events.
- **Living Chronicle:** integrate into Sven's family tragedy and later Shadowfell conflict; preserve temporal boundaries.
- **Compendium Volume I:** new character-biography expansion and necromancy/agency implications where appropriate.
- **Compendium Volume II:** do not invent a named settlement or mapped territory near the Feywild; geographic identification remains general.

He constructed eight distorted memory episodes and seductive ideal lives. ## Addendum 007 — The Unbroken Frozenroar Line and Varied Royal Fates (November 12, 2026)

**Status: KEEPER APPROVED; SUPERSEDES earlier provisional date and partial-lineage assumptions.**

### Approved calendar

The **Misfits' original campaign took place during 2000 AF**, with approximately six months between Moonville Manor and Cid's final defeat. **Months 1–6** are useful narrative pacing markers only; calendar month names and precise dates remain unassigned.

- Month 1: Moonville Manor, first royal commission.
- Month 2: Gol'Goroth, Noki's sheep confrontation, search for Lowkee's Keeper; Anna joins.
- Month 3: Stanleyville — Jack's bodily death and the Stalker killing Kel'Maris; Funkin returns the body. **Later** in Month 3, the Hydra temple — Captain Funkin, Inspector Sihnion, guards, and Fo'Gurn die.
- Month 4: Rotten Husk liberation, Jaznaar's revelation and death after the refuge meeting.
- Month 5: Tournament, Melmiri's kidnapping, Ros Furband's captivity, real Jinlin confrontation (defeated, not confirmed dead).
- Month 6: Zauks killed by Amara; Shadowfell — Fo'Gurn breaks Jaaris's control while Jaaris lives, Jaaris dies, Fo'Gurn's borrowed existence fails, Cid is taken into the Beyond; homecoming and public vindication.

**Tristan and Kristofer died in 1997 AF** at Jaaris's hands, before Sven joined the Misfits. The post-Cid present is **2020 AF**, with no deaths among the established named cast in the intervening twenty years.

### Approved royal succession

**House Frozenroar is one unbroken descent line of 34 rulers from Rynhold to Gradvum.** Succession is familial, not invariably parent-child. Abbreviated pedigrees should not falsely imply undocumented links are absent from the family.

### First new Hunters Guild biography: Heyden of the Goldenfoot

The creator provided a long, detailed personal history. Its wiki adaptation is `content/articles/heyden-of-the-goldenfoot.md` (BIO.061), a standalone article because the adventures and character development justify the space. Do **not** classify him as a minor NPC.

**Identity and family:** Heyden Goldenfoot, called Heyden of the Goldenfoot, is a **32-year-old male Leonin, chaotic good**, with amber-gold eyes, tawny-gold mane with darker undertones, and an unusually large, quiet-moving physique (265 pounds). He is the fourth prince of **Labos**, son of **King Geoff and Queen Viviliv**. His older brothers are **Jeothor** (crown heir), **Verbos** (destined for Lord General), and **Seals** (monastic tradition). He grew up with opportunity to travel because his brothers inherited the roles usually expected of royal sons. He dislikes ceremony, arrogance and the substitution of wealth for competence; he loves the thrill and skill of the hunt more than taking a life.

**Chult:** Heyden tracked an **Allosaurus** with a hunting party in Chult. It cut a deep scar down the **right side of his face into the mane**; he killed the beast by closing with it and tearing at its throat. His scar is regarded as a trophy.

**Weyland Forest / Valiant:** He pursued the **Great Elk** for almost **two years** under guidance from the **Woodland Elves of Valiant**. He killed the elk after a long apprenticeship in patience, concealment and reading the forest. The elves accepted him, tattooed their **sigil on his left thigh** and gave him a **Revenant Double-Bladed Scimitar**, a valued possession. The hunt's deeper importance was being accepted for his earned abilities, not his ancestry or title.

**Gnomish village and Gomo:** After helping a village and its local gnomish hunters kill a **Giant Constrictor Snake**, Heyden met a **halfling hunter named Gomo** at a tavern. Gomo told him of dangerous magically twisted creatures in Avalor's **Dead Marshes**, gave him a **Hunters Guild coin**, and recommended **Silver of Moonlight** as a contact.

**Avalor and the guild:** He joined his parents' diplomatic delegation to Avalor, went through official events in the capital, then traveled into the Dead Marshes. Gomo's **coin—not the Goldenfoot family name—gained him entry to the guild**. **Silver of Moonlight**, a female guild hunter, welcomed him, taught him customs and became his friend. He remained as extraordinary contracts accumulated.

**Wood of the Fey / Shadow:** Fey-woods experience imparted uncanny movement, climbing and hiding techniques to this large Leonin. Other guild members dubbed him **Shadow** because he could disappear among branches and foliage even under observation. The names and precise nature of the fey teachers remain unrevealed in the creator's history.

**Chosen family:** Only months after his arrival, **several unnamed hunters** died on the job. Their absence caused Heyden a new, deeper grief. The guild mourned them with the saying, **"No one misses a dead man's party."** He realized that finding a family worth staying with meant having someone to lose. These deaths concern unnamed hunters and **do not contradict the rule that no established named cast died in the twenty-year post-Cid interval**.

**Appearance and tools:** Weathered leather hunting attire, sturdy boots, dark cloak, hidden Goldenfoot signet, Gomo's coin, gifted scimitar, longbow, specialty arrows, hunting knives, traps, ropes and survival gear. He is proud of both family and guild, despite avoiding courts. His personal rank within the guild is not formalized beyond being a member and a hunter called Shadow.

**Unestablished and intentionally unfilled:** Heyden's exact date of birth, years of his major hunts, who the fey instructors were, outcomes after the supplied narrative, Silver's surname and Gomo's formal rank, and any direct connection to the Black Diamond or Blackwater Bay. The user-supplied story includes unrelated promotional fragments (e.g., camping gear and safari calls-to-action); these are artifacts of pasted text, **not Avalor canon**.
