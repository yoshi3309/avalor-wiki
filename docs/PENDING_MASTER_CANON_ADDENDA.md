## Confirmed addition: Kel'Maris, the Lion's Shadow

- **Name:** Kel'Maris.
- **Species:** High elf; first elf to become Lord Marshal.
- **Age:** 85 in the supplied backstory (the exact date at which this age was recorded is not specified).
- **Family and origin:** First daughter of a prominent elven family from the village of Ilyanamel.
- **Martial history:** Training across multiple weapons from early childhood; her preferred two-weapon fighting style paired a longsword with a shortsword and became known as the **Kel Scythe**, particularly devastating to shield formations.
- **Avalor Games:** Placed among the top five swordsmen many times; won the championship twice.
- **Relationship to the crown:** Sent to Renada at King Gerran's order to teach the young Prince Gradvum swordsmanship. Became his friend, joined Lion's Bane, quickly reached second in command beneath Lord Marshal **Kurt Reyginald**, and served as close personal guard to the king. Her constant presence earned the epithet **Lion's Shadow**.
- **Office:** Succeeded Kurt Reyginald upon his retirement; was already Lord Marshal at Gradvum's accession. Commissioned the Misfits against the first unnamed necromancer and later the cult of Gol'Goroth.
- **Stanleyville:** The Stalker pierced Kel'Maris through the chest and killed her **before the Hydra temple disaster**. Captain Funkin survived the Stalker encounter and carried her body back to Renada. He subsequently died at the Hydra temple alongside Inspector Sihnion and the guards.
- **Funeral:** Body returned to Renada, state funeral, three-day lying in state at the cathedral, private cremation according to her wishes, ashes scattered in the King's Forest.
- **Succession:** Office left vacant until Gradvum appointed Lavitz after the defeat of Cid.

### Literal creator-submitted account (retained for master-document reconciliation)

> Lord Marshall of the Lions Bane. Young by elven standards, Kel'Maris was the first of her kind to become the Lord Marshall of the Lions Bane. A spry High elf at the young age of 85, Kel'Maris has been around for many years of the Frozenroar reign. Hailing from the elven village of Ilyanamel, Kel'Maris travelled to Renada by order of King Gerran in order to be the sword instructor for to his son, a young Gradvum who was set to inherit the throne. Kel'Maris became great friends through the years with Gradvum and she readily agreed to join the Lions Bane, the King's personal guard. She quickly rose through the ranks and became second in command, directly below the Lord Marshall Kurt Reyginald. She was assigned as the personal guard to the King, always within ear shot. Due to her continuous presence, Kel'Maris gaining the nickname "Lions Shadow". She succeeded Kurt upon his retirement as the Lord Marshall and held that position when Gradvum took the throne.

> Kel'maris was the first daughter of a prominent Elven family. Starting at a very young age, she trained in various forms of weaponry. Her most skilled form was a dual wielding form using a long sword and a short sword. The form became known as the Kel Scythe for its devastating ability to break a shield formation. When she moved to the capital to be Gradvum's teacher, she entered the Avalor games every year, placing as one of the top five swordsman in the kingdom many times. Twice she had beat out every combatant and won it all.

While at Stanleyville, Kel'maris fell when the creature pierced her chest. Captain Funkin was able to safely bring back her body to Renada. She was honored with a state funeral and her body was gracefully displayed at the Grand Cathedral for three days. Her body was burned in a private ceremony in accordance to her wishes, her ashes scattered across the Kings Forest.

## Resolved continuity issue KEL-001: Captain Funkin and the event order

**Original conflict:** The earlier compiled chronology erroneously put the Hydra temple before the Stalker. That made the source account that Captain Funkin returned Kel'Maris's body appear impossible, because Funkin was recorded as dying at the temple.

**Creator correction, November 12, 2026:** **The Stalker occurred before the Hydra.** Kel'Maris was pierced and killed in Stanleyville. **Captain Funkin returned her body safely to Renada**, where she received her funeral. **Only afterward** did Funkin die at the ruined temple during the Hydra catastrophe, alongside Inspector Sihnion and guards. Fo'Gurn also died there buying the Misfits time to escape. Anna survived the temple; Kel'Maris was already dead and therefore was not a captive or survivor of the Hydra episode.

**Status: RESOLVED - creator-approved canon.** The original submitted history above is preserved verbatim. Prior contradictory public chronology has been superseded, not deleted from the audit trail.

## Confirmed addition - JAARIS-002: The Master of Broken Wills (November 12, 2026)

**Status: APPROVED CANON - publication permitted.**

**Creator direction:** Jaaris's fundamental motivation is **control and domination**. The three recommended elaborations were expressly approved: (1) gifted self-made scholar, (2) gaining leadership of the hostile fey through magical bargains, compulsions and intimidation, and (3) consuming Tristan and Kristofer's life energy himself through a necromantic ritual. Unlike important recurring figures, short-lived villains and side characters should normally receive only **a couple of meaningful paragraphs**, not encyclopedic biographies of every year.

### Origin and philosophy
Jaaris is a dark-haired tiefling with gray skin marked by necrotic corruption. Born among mortal settlements near the Feywild, he was a magically gifted, self-taught scholar whose primary ambition was absolute obedience. He explored charms, oaths and compulsions before deepening his necromancy, drawn by the possibility that the dead need never disobey. His repeated necrotic experiments marked his body. He believed freedom to refuse was a fundamental defect in the living.

### The hostile fey
As an outsider rather than a fey elf, Jaaris obtained power over one hostile faction of fey elves by offering binding magic, then consolidating authority with coercive bargains, compulsions and fear. This is a particular faction, not all fey or the Feywild. Some followed from shared cruelty, others because they feared defying him.

### Sven, Tristan and Kristofer
Sven had made a family with Tristan and Kristofer. Under Jaaris's orders, the hostile watchers deliberately withdrew near the end of their fourth year together so Sven would lower his guard. When Sven left to seek a birthday gift for Kristofer, the fey burned their home and captured the family. Jaaris personally consumed Tristan and Kristofer's life energy in the ritual while Sven, magically bound, was forced to witness it. He also attempted to take Sven's soul, but some **unidentified force or circumstance stopped him**; do not invent the intervention's identity. Jaaris acted from contempt for freely chosen love. **He carried out this crime independently of Cid**, before entering the Blood King's service.

### Cid, Hydra and Fo'Gurn
After the tragedy, Jaaris became Cid's servant because their ambitions aligned: Cid sought to overcome mortality; Jaaris wanted to overcome free will. He was not the unnamed necromancer defeated during the first royal commission. After the Stalker killed Kel'Maris, Jaaris was among Cid's servants at the later ruined temple, where he formed a Hydra from the failed ritual's sacrificial mass. Fo'Gurn truly died buying the company's escape. Jaaris subsequently restored him by necromancy and compelled him as a weapon against Lavitz and the Misfits, using the paladin's bonds as a test of absolute control.

### Defeat and remaining limits
At the Shadowfell fortress, Lavitz lowered his defenses rather than destroy Fo'Gurn. **Fo'Gurn chose to protect Lavitz and broke Jaaris's hold before Jaaris died.** Jaaris's subsequent death caused the unnatural existence supporting Fo'Gurn to fail; it did not create his moral freedom. How Jaaris acquired each particular spell, any personal association with Karina beyond service to Cid, and the precise force that prevented Sven's soul from being consumed remain **UNESTABLISHED**.

### Preservation and publication
- **Wiki:** expanded `content/articles/jaaris.md` and related Sven/Cid accounts.
- **Master Canon Bible:** add independent fey crime, character philosophy, post-tragedy Cid allegiance, Hydra/Fo'Gurn events.
- **Living Chronicle:** integrate into Sven's family tragedy and later Shadowfell conflict; preserve temporal boundaries.
- **Compendium Volume I:** new character-biography expansion and necromancy/agency implications where appropriate.
- **Compendium Volume II:** do not invent a named settlement or mapped territory near the Feywild; geographic identification remains general.
- **Short supporting NPC rule:** most deceased villains, brief-role NPCs and side characters need two meaningful paragraphs rather than full encyclopedic histories; lengthy treatments are reserved for major story-shaping characters.


## Addendum 003 - Compact supporting-character archive (November 12, 2026)

**Status: PUBLICATION APPROVED FOR ESTABLISHED EVENTS.** This batch is an editorial consolidation and short-biography expansion, not approval to invent hidden histories, motivations, dates or encounters. Seven standalone biographies were added and three existing biographies revised for proportionate depth.

### The three existing accounts refined

- **Zauks** (`content/articles/zauks.md`): A mind flayer among Cid's servants at the ruined temple, later the captor who imprisoned the Misfits physically in cocoons after their real defeat of Jinlin. He constructed eight distorted memory episodes and seductive ideal lives. Amara killed him after arriving as an owl, outside the illusions. **Unresolved:** private motivation, origins, full psychic mechanisms, independent link to the Ringtree mind flayer. A thematic emphasis on manipulating grief is an interpretation of his recorded actions, not a newly established secret origin.
- **Noki / Noke** (`content/articles/noki-the-sheep.md`): The original undisfigured mage became a sheep during his confrontation with the Misfits; disfigurement occurred only at that encounter's end. He survived despite their belief he had died and afterward became Noke, an intelligent, scarred, physically powerful servant of the Den Mother who can use spells and a massive possibly dragon-associated wooden club. **Unresolved:** cause of disfigurement, means of survival, relationship to Den Mother, club's precise origin. His wounded pride is an editorial interpretation, not an established private confession.
- **Ros Furband** (`content/articles/ros-furband.md`): A hired pirate captain with a large galleon and more than forty pirates in some accounts. Seized the Misfits after the tournament, forced them to labor under antimagic restraints, and delivered them toward Jinlin while Melmiri remained captive. His commercial role is established; the identity of the person paying him, fee, later fate, and Beldir's unusual presence remain unresolved. **Do not identify Ros as secretly loyal to Cid or the Wrath without approval.**

### Seven concise new biographies

- **Karina** (`content/articles/karina.md`; BIO.052): Vampiric centaur in Cid's retinue, murderer of Tel'Lora's mother while seeking her spellbook and ice-blue crystal. Killed by the Misfits. Her defeat is possibly associated with the Rotten Husk episode, **not securely scene-documented**. Do not invent how she died.
- **Black Agnes Mudwallow** (`content/articles/black-agnes-mudwallow.md`; BIO.053): Night hag among Cid's servants at the ruined temple. Defeated and killed by the Misfits during the original campaign. Final encounter, timing and opponent remain **unestablished**.
- **Jinlin Vechon** (`content/articles/jinlin-vechon.md`; BIO.054): Dragonborn cursed with unwanted vampirism by Aldus's Blood Sickness experiments nearly two thousand years earlier, founder of the Wrath of Innocent, driven by revenge against Cid. His followers harmed innocents; the Misfits **defeated him in a real battle** after Ros's captivity and before Zauks. **Do not claim Jinlin was killed unless confirmed.**
- **Captain Funkin** (`content/articles/captain-funkin.md`; BIO.055): Renadan captain who returned Kel'Maris's body after her Stalker death, then died in the **later** ruined temple disaster. Stalker-before-Hydra is the controlling chronology.
- **Inspector Sihnion** (`content/articles/inspector-sihnion.md`; BIO.056): Inspector present at the later temple disaster, died with Captain Funkin and trapped guards. Specific investigative assignment and earlier life remain unestablished.
- **Kurt Reyginald** (`content/articles/kurt-reyginald.md`; BIO.057): Former Lord Marshal of Lion's Bane under King Gerran. Kel'Maris served as his second in command and succeeded him on retirement, before Gradvum's accession. His personal history requires no invented account.
- **King Gerran Frozenroar** (`content/articles/king-gerran-frozenroar.md`; BIO.058): Sovereign 1946-1982 AF, father and predecessor to Gradvum. Called Kel'Maris from Ilyanamel to teach his son swordsmanship; appointed Admiral Beldir Willowrun Sentinel of the Seas on the Aegis. Further diplomatic or military stories remain unestablished.
