# Avalor Artwork — Wave 7: The Misfits' Campaign in Ten Illustrations

**Prepared:** 12 November 2026. All ten linked entries exist in the current PUBLIC wiki and had no assigned article image when inspected. Wave 6 had 9 of 10 images assigned; `threefold-devotion` was still awaiting art.

**Series idea:** A unified set of ten visually connected historical plates moving from Moonville Manor to the Misfits' return to Renada. This is an editorial ordering of visual subjects, not a newly asserted calendar of the campaign.

**Style and layout:** 2:3 vertical illustration (ideally 1200 x 1800 or larger) with consistent realistic painterly fantasy brushwork, believable medieval materials, atmospheric lighting, and restrained magic. All selected articles are in `History & Chronicles`, which currently uses a sidebar portrait-shaped media frame rather than the full-width location-image treatment.

**Continuity and confidentiality:** This is a public artwork plan. Some current public article drafts contain material marked restricted in Keeper-only references; the images must NOT amplify such disclosures. In particular, avoid Anna's nature, the Emerald's full purpose, Fo'Gurn's necromantic restoration and final rest, Sven's personal divine choice, and future Godkiller material. Even where current text includes these details, the artwork should not depict them. The Keeper should separately review player-facing article visibility. Favor interpretive and symbolic compositions over claimed factual reconstructions.

## 1. Moonville Manor — Before the Crown Called

- **Existing wiki article:** https://yoshi3309.github.io/avalor-wiki/articles/moonville-manor-incident/
- **Image assignment at selection:** None
- **Image shape:** 2:3 vertical
- **Narrative role:** The living-house mystery that first brought the companions together

### Image-generation prompt

Create a tall 2:3 realistic painterly high-fantasy illustration of MOONVILLE MANOR, the living house from the earliest known adventure of the Misfits in Avalor. The focal scene is an impossible old manor interior: a grand but weathered timber-and-stone corridor with doors that appear to lead back into themselves, inconsistent stair geometry, a candlelit room visibly out of alignment with the passage, subtly bending floorboards and a disquieting sense that the house itself is aware. Place a few small anonymous adventurer silhouettes at the corridor's end to convey uncertainty and mutual reliance, but do not assign faces or costumes to Lavitz, Fo'Gurn, Tel'Lora, Sven or Amara without their approved character references. Mood: Gothic wonder, eerie but grounded, amber candlelight against deep blue shadows; the mystery of a house that cannot be trusted. This occurred BEFORE the first royal commission. Do not portray a mimic attack, the mind flayer Zauks, a hidden controller of the manor, a prophecy, legible writing, invented house heraldry or a specific room-by-room floor plan. No text.

## 2. Gol'Goroth — The Second Commission

- **Existing wiki article:** https://yoshi3309.github.io/avalor-wiki/articles/golgoroth-second-commission/
- **Image assignment at selection:** None
- **Image shape:** 2:3 vertical
- **Narrative role:** The cult investigation during which Heureux became a Misfit

### Image-generation prompt

Create a vertical 2:3 realistic painterly fantasy illustration representing the Misfits' SECOND royal commission, an investigation of a cult devoted to Gol'Goroth. Show an old stone approach to a neglected sanctuary at nightfall, several anonymous investigators moving cautiously through fallen masonry while the light from a hooded lantern glances off weathered columns. A traveling musical instrument strapped to one pack quietly evokes Heureux, the blue-furred firbolg bard who joined the fellowship during this commission, without inventing his exact appearance or the moment of his arrival. The atmosphere should be one of danger, discovery and an expanding circle of friendship, not merely a demonic ritual scene. The exact cult rites and membership are not securely preserved, so do not invent a specific Gol'Goroth effigy, infernal glyph, sacrifice, named cult leader, spell effect or Cid-directed conspiracy. A practical ruined space and forbidding atmosphere are illustrative, not claims of precisely known architecture. Restrained candlelight, painterly realism, stone texture, no legible writing or invented heraldry.

## 3. The Sheep Incident

- **Existing wiki article:** https://yoshi3309.github.io/avalor-wiki/articles/noki-sheep-incident/
- **Image assignment at selection:** None
- **Image shape:** 2:3 vertical
- **Narrative role:** A strange comic episode whose consequences only became clear later

### Image-generation prompt

Create a charming but grounded vertical 2:3 realistic painterly fantasy historical illustration of the Misfits' famous Sheep Incident. In a woodland clearing or neutral rural roadside without identifiable named landmarks, show an ordinary woolly sheep standing in the center, looking alarmed and surprisingly intelligent, amid fading wisps of magical transformation. Around its edges, only anonymous adventurers' boots, the lower half of a cloak and a dropped staff imply the confrontation without inventing the party's likenesses or exact spell choreography. The immediate subject is NOKI as a sheep, BEFORE his later disfigurement and long before he became known as Noke. Balance humor with an uneasy hint that the ridiculous encounter will matter. Warm natural daylight, naturalistic fleece, fine painterly textures. No disfigured giant, massive wooden club, dragons, fully visible Noke, mind flayer, extra duplicate sheep, invented magical wand power, legible text or signs. Avoid depicting any later false adventure created by Zauks.

## 4. The Ruined Temple and the Hydra

- **Existing wiki article:** https://yoshi3309.github.io/avalor-wiki/articles/hydra-temple-disaster/
- **Image assignment at selection:** None
- **Image shape:** 2:3 vertical
- **Narrative role:** Fo'Gurn's sacrifice near Stanleyville, depicted without private later revelations

### Image-generation prompt

Create a gripping vertical 2:3 cinematic painterly fantasy illustration of THE RUINED TEMPLE AND THE HYDRA, a tragic encounter in the Misfits' original campaign near Stanleyville. Inside the wreckage of a ruined temple of Bane, the silhouettes of fleeing companions recede toward a broken stone exit while a single brass dragonborn paladin stands his ground amid falling dust and shattered masonry. Beyond him, a huge many-headed hydra looms through smoke and broken pillars, suggesting overwhelming danger; the paladin buys others time to escape. Use dramatic natural rim lighting, falling stones and a noble, desperate composition; keep the armor general unless supplied with an approved Fo'Gurn reference. This illustrates Fo'Gurn's publicly remembered heroic sacrifice, not the Blood King's full ritual or the companions' later Shadowfell encounters. Do not depict a god incarnating, a confirmed divine vessel, Anna's hidden identity, Fo'Gurn being raised by necromancy, an exact number of hydra heads, Cid's retinue as a formal rival fellowship, or the detailed mechanism of the temple's ritual. No invented temple insignia, readable text or gore.

## 5. Freedom for the Rotten Husk

- **Existing wiki article:** https://yoshi3309.github.io/avalor-wiki/articles/rotten-husk-liberation/
- **Image assignment at selection:** None
- **Image shape:** 2:3 vertical
- **Narrative role:** The liberation of an orc community, with the community as the subject

### Image-generation prompt

Create a warm yet hard-earned vertical 2:3 realistic painterly fantasy illustration of the liberation of the Rotten Husk, an orc community helped by the Misfits after an oppressive chieftain endangered his own people. Make the orc families and community the central subjects: at the end of a difficult day, elders and parents help one another gather belongings, an injured but surviving community member is cared for, and children emerge from shelter into soft daylight. Suggest relieved self-determination rather than a royal victory parade. A few anonymous adventurer figures may stand respectfully at the far edge, clearly not ruling the clan or receiving worship. The community's precise homeland, buildings, customs and later leadership are unrecorded; use a deliberately unidentifiable, modest inhabited setting without specific topography, sacred symbols or clan uniforms. No invented named chieftain, no dead orcs as spectacle, no celebration of conquest, no demonization of an entire ancestry, no readable inscriptions or banners. Beautiful textured painterly art, muted ochre, soft green and warm human emotion.

## 6. The Testimony at Jaznaar’s Refuge

- **Existing wiki article:** https://yoshi3309.github.io/avalor-wiki/articles/jaznaar-refuge-revelation/
- **Image assignment at selection:** None
- **Image shape:** 2:3 vertical
- **Narrative role:** The truth of Aldus, the innocence of Jaznaar, and the sword entrusted to Lavitz

### Image-generation prompt

Create an intimate, solemn vertical 2:3 realistic painterly fantasy illustration inspired by the testimony at Jaznaar's hidden refuge. In a quiet, ancient, otherworldly but materially believable scholarly chamber, illuminate a historic longsword resting on a stone table, with a small ice-blue crystal glowing faintly beside archival manuscripts. Beyond the table, a single aged drow scholar is shown from behind or in deep shadow, as if sharing a truth carried across centuries; several anonymous listeners stand respectfully outside the strongest light. The sword symbolizes Cid's Bane, once belonging to Lowkee and entrusted by Jaznaar to Lavitz; the crystal is connected to Tel'Lora's family and the route to the refuge. Mood: weight of evidence, grief and moral recognition, not an action scene. Avoid inventing the exact sword design, crystal mechanics, portal construction, Jaznaar's approved face, quoted testimony, secret magical procedures or a fabricated betrayal by Jaznaar. No readable writing or literal depiction of the Blood Sickness. The image is symbolic historical interpretation, not a documented view of the demiplane.

## 7. Captives of Ros Furband and the Wrath

- **Image assignment at selection:** None
- **Image shape:** 2:3 vertical
- **Narrative role:** The interruption of Queen Melmiri's rescue aboard a pirate galleon

### Image-generation prompt

Create a compelling vertical 2:3 realistic painterly fantasy illustration of the Misfits' captivity aboard Ros Furband's large pirate galleon, after the Tournament of Champions and while Queen Melmiri remained captive elsewhere. Focus on a timber ship's lower deck and galley: lantern-lit planks, heavy oars, rigging, water glimmering through a hatch, ordinary prisoners at work under the distant watch of armed pirates. Keep the prisoners anonymous or shown from behind so none is assigned an incorrect canonical likeness. The impression is humiliation and confinement on a ship, despite the prisoners' courage and reputation. The voyage led toward Jinlin Vechon and the Wrath of Innocent, but this scene is aboard Ros's galleon, not the later battle with the dragonborn vampire. No recognizable Beldir or named companions without approved references, no fabricated pirate flag, no modern ship architecture, no magical collars with invented shapes, no claim that Jinlin served Cid, no readable text. Painterly low-key lighting, sea spray and plausible naval detail.

## 8. Zauks and the False Lives

- **Existing wiki article:** https://yoshi3309.github.io/avalor-wiki/articles/zauks-false-lives/
- **Image assignment at selection:** None
- **Image shape:** 2:3 vertical
- **Narrative role:** Mind flayer captivity and the deceptive appeal of perfect happiness

### Image-generation prompt

Create a psychological vertical 2:3 realistic painterly fantasy illustration for the historical account of Zauks and the False Lives. In a dim natural cavern, place several closed organic cocoons in the lower foreground, suggesting that the Misfits' bodies are trapped. Above them, rather than showing a literal alternate world, let soft beautiful domestic light appear in fragmented reflections on wet cavern walls: a warm cottage window, a peaceful table set for loved ones, a field at dawn—tempting glimpses that visibly cannot exist in the cave. At the upper edge a subtle owl-shaped silhouette hints at Amara's intervention outside the illusion. The feeling should be wistful and frightening at once: an enemy turning happy longing into confinement. Keep the mind flayer Zauks himself offscreen or as an indistinct shadow; no invented exact facial design. Do not establish any of the counterfeit marriages, children, resurrections, or false adventures as genuine history. No four real Stalkers, no dragons at Noki's actual encounter, no explicit portrayal of Jack's false death scene, no legible text or portal diagram. Cinematic but restrained painterly composition.

- **Image assignment at selection:** None
- **Image shape:** 2:3 vertical
- **Narrative role:** The queen's return, mourning, and beginning of the Age of Ascendancy

### Image-generation prompt

Create a luminous, emotionally resonant vertical 2:3 realistic painterly fantasy historical painting of the return of Queen Melmiri to Renada with the Misfits following their victory over Cid. View the royal capital from an elevated street near the River Rhys: ordinary citizens of diverse ancestries quietly gather as a small returning group approaches the city beneath the distant Great Citadel. Some spectators welcome the queen while others carry flowers or stand solemnly in memory of those lost. Capture relief woven together with mourning, not a simple triumphal parade. The queen and adventurers may be suggested only as distant figures or silhouettes; no invented faces, costumes, procession, number of participants or unapproved royal banners. This moment precedes the later reform work and does not reveal private conversations after the royal audience. No depiction of Anna's identity, Sven's unaccepted divine offer, Fo'Gurn's final fate, Samuel's hidden role, specific funeral rites or verbatim proclamation. Warm late-afternoon sunlight, painterly architectural detail and genuine human emotion, no readable text.

3. Add each approved image through Pages CMS to the matching article, with accessible alt text that describes the visible artwork, and a caption such as *Artist's interpretation of ...* when appropriate.
4. These are all **History & Chronicles** entries: their media appears in the portrait-style article sidebar. They will not automatically enter Locations Gallery.
5. Check the latest GitHub Actions run after upload; do not retry an apparently failed CMS fetch without first verifying the commit landed.
