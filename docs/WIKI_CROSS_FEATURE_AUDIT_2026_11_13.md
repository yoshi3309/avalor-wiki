# Archives of Avalor — Cross-Feature Editorial Review
**Review date:** November 13, 2026  
**Repository:** `yoshi3309/avalor-wiki`  
**Working baseline:** 131 published articles before this pass; 132 after adding the new compact gazetteer.

## Scope and method

This was a **whole-catalog technical and stylistic inventory**, followed by selective substantive editing—not an assertion that every sentence in the 131 articles has been independently verified against an original session recording. Every article was scanned for word length, heading structure, public art assignments, editorial reflection features and signs of modern production language. The review also inspected the curated configuration for People & Legends, Places, the relationship atlas, Connections Explorer, Character Comparisons, the Living Chronicle, the Royal Gazette, and the historical reading journey.

The latest **creator-approved canon overrides** and source ledger outrank stale editorial plans or old master-candidate PDF statements. The source master PDFs are not modified by this review. The wiki remains a retrospective public historical archive, not an indiscriminate repository for campaign secrets.

## Appropriate brevity retained

Entries for small ports, villages, ordinary officials and one-off antagonists **were not expanded merely to reach a target word count**. The three minor-character indexes continue to serve that purpose, while the Hunters Guild and consequential figures receive more developed treatment. No new portraits were invented and no group illustration is passed off as a verified named person's face.

## Existing feature integrity

- **Major articles and the 15-chapter Misfits reading journey** retain their structure.
- **House Frozenroar's 34-sovereign register** and the **1997 / 2000 / 2020 AF** decisions remain unchanged.
- **Stalker before Hydra**, and **Jinlin's real defeat before Zauks**, remain unchanged.
- **CID's ultimate death**, **Jinlin's ultimate fate**, **Fo'Gurn's later reappearance**, and **Jaznaar's precise class** are not resolved by aesthetic editing.
- **The Black Diamond's rise** and **Blackwater Bay's adventures** remain undeveloped publicly until the creator approves that history.
- The atlas contains no invented location pin for **Chult, Weyland, Valiant, Labos or the Wood of the Fey**.
- The site still retains all earlier consolidated character and geographic redirects.
- The four candidate master PDFs remain immutable pending dedicated canon reconciliation.

2. **More Hunters Guild characters** when the creator supplies their histories; add to the curated Guild group and appropriate comparison/connection features only if source-backed.
3. **Confirm real locations** of Ilyanamel, Labos, and other unmapped places before introducing atlas markers.
4. **Black Diamond origins before Blackwater Bay:** preserve the existing unresolved Black Diamond insignia connection to Cid.
5. **Master-volume reconciliation:** integrate Addenda 001–009 as a controlled revision, resolving older contradictory manuscript dates and identities with later approvals.
6. **Additional close reading:** the whole-catalog scan was editorial and structural; long articles can still receive line-by-line source collation as original campaign notes are supplied. Do not confuse automated continuity checks with that human judgment.

## Build and publishing assurance

Tests in the validation workflow verify article metadata, article and section links, image references, comparison-dossier chapter citations, connection graph evidence, Royal Gazette source articles, historical journey anchors and atlas constraints. Publication to `main` should occur only after every draft validation job succeeds. This review does **not** establish that the public GitHub Pages deployment has finished.
