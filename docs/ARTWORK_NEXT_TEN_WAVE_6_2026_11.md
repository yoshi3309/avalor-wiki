**Prepared:** November 11, 2026 (Eastern US). Checked against the *current public encyclopedia*, not future or restricted Keeper notes. All ten selected articles had no assigned `image:` at the time of selection.

**Previous batch status:** Wave 5's ten artworks are now assigned to their articles. Several Wave 4 entries may also have been completed; use current Pages CMS/article images rather than the original production checklist to decide what remains. **Production standard:** Realistic painterly fantasy, 2:3 vertical for the History & Chronicles, Organizations & Factions and Faith/Magic categories (sidebar illustration layout), with 1200×1800 or equivalent preferred. No title text baked into images; use truthful captions identifying interpretive scenes. If exact appearances, creatures, official uniforms, inscriptions or buildings are unverified, favor symbolic or anonymous presentations rather than invented hard canon.

## Publishing and editorial review

1. Render and approve each image individually; compare subjects involving named people or monsters against author-approved character images before publishing.
2. Upload as the relevant article image in Pages CMS, with a factual alt description and a caption such as *Artist's interpretation of ...* when the exact historical composition is unknown.
3. These ten images are NOT Kingdoms & Places, so they will appear on their article's portrait-oriented side rail; they do not automatically join the Locations Gallery. Create a curated gallery exhibit later only if an appropriate collection and provenance exist.
4. If publishing fails, inspect the newest GitHub Actions job, not just the CMS upload status. Preserve successful uploaded files and avoid duplicate retries.
5. Do not reveal restricted future-campaign histories or use image details to invent unknown geography, lineages or chronology.
