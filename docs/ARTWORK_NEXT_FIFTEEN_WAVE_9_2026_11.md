# Avalor Artwork — Wave 9: Fifteen Remaining Subjects

**Prepared:** 13 November 2026. Directly checked against the current public 132-article wiki. All ten Wave 8 entries had assigned artwork. The fifteen entries below **exist and had no assigned `image:` field at selection**. Wave 9 is a production queue, not a new change to article facts.

## Theme

The kingdom beyond its great monuments, the village and guild stories that underpin life in Avalor, sacred and magical uncertainties, and notable people who have not yet received visual treatment. We deliberately avoid using a painting to turn uncertain evidence into established history.

## Format and editorial rules

- **Kingdoms & Places**: 16:9 landscape (suggested 1920 × 1080). Article display: wide illustration above the manuscript. Eligible for automatic Locations Gallery inclusion, although multi-vignette images should be captioned as illustrated gazetteer plates rather than literal geographic views.
- **Other categories**: 2:3 vertical (suggested 1200 × 1800). Article display: portrait-style sidebar. Not auto-indexed into the Locations Gallery.
- **Appearance**: Realistic, painterly medieval fantasy, archival finish, varied light and color to suit each subject, strong readable composition, no legible writing within the image.
- **Canon protection**: Do not invent the map positions of Ilyanamel, unknown terrain of the Drought, the true nature of the Beyond or primordial powers, the outcome of Jinlin's and Ros's lives, the portrait of Anna as an approved design, or secret future-era story implications. When precise visuals are unrecorded, prompts explicitly request interpretive rather than authenticated reconstructions.
- **Artwork approval**: Prompts are creative direction; incidental architecture, costume, inscriptions, facial design and decorative motifs do not become canon automatically.
