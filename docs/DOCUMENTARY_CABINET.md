# The Documentary Cabinet — Public Historical Exhibits

Five curated public exhibits at /exhibits/, with standalone displays and contextual links from related encyclopedia articles.

## Historical integrity

The parchment display is an **editorial reconstruction**: a modern, plain-language historical synthesis, **not** an original deed, law, treaty, proclamation, physical manuscript, verbatim quotation, or recovered seal. The distinction is repeated on every exhibit and in its accessible text. The Archives lion crest is decorative editorial branding.

- Source records are public articles under content/articles.
- Exhibit YAML files are in content/exhibits.
- Only records with published: true are shown.
- folio_text is a public paraphrase broken into paragraphs; evidence and uncertainty separate established claims from unavailable details.
- interpretation is explicitly marked as curatorial perspective.
- Historical source article slugs are validated during site generation.
- No Keeper secrets, invented treaty provisions, forged royal voices or signatures, or false provenance may be added.

## Editing

Edit via Pages CMS under "Historical archive exhibits" or modify the corresponding YAML file. Keep the stable id unchanged after publishing. Save new draft records as published: false until reviewed. The site also checks the completeness of the text, supported periods, and valid source and related article references.
