# Avalor — Source-Grounded Artwork Production Plan (November 2026)

## Overview
The 112 encyclopedia articles currently include approximately 37 with attached art and 75 without. Not every page needs an illustration. Character biographies already featuring the owner's portraits should not be regenerated merely to meet a coverage metric. The latest integration automatically adds eligible existing character portraits to the Gallery, and the owner's renamed uploads have been linked to individual biographies and corresponding curated exhibits. A picture is an *interpretation* unless its specific details are established in a public source.

## Priority 1 — Four location scenes to generate first
Each is a **16:9 landscape**, preferably at least 1600 pixels wide, in the archive's established realistic, painterly fantasy style.

1. **Renada** — Capital from a river approach. The River Rhys, monumental citadel and layered city should dominate. Do not invent additional districts, city flags, recognizable royal figures or specific historical ceremonies.
2. **Black Tower** — Forbidden tower amid the Spine of the World, harsh terrain and an ominous ascent. Never depict its concealed inner rituals or future campaigns.
3. **Heaven's Heart** — Paladin sanctuary in the Spine of the World; substantial sacred architecture and austere atmosphere. Its tradition honors Tyr, Helm and Torm; no invented heraldry or clerical hierarchy.
4. **Stanleyville** — A living King's Road smithing town: forges, craft workshops and ordinary residents. The entire settlement should not be shown destroyed; its forge economy endures beyond the Stalker affair.

## Priority 2 — Major geography and daily life
5. **Forest of the Fey** — Avalor's physical woodland, not an illustration of the Feywild plane.
6. **Dead Marshes** — Wetland with uncertain paths and reed beds; no invented monsters or ruins.
7. **Great Desert** — Broad arid landscape and distant mountains; no fabricated city or caravan history.
8. **Spine of the World** — Extensive mountain range and passes, without unrecorded fortifications.
9. **Great Cathedral in Renada** — Civic sanctuary associated with Tyr, Helm, Torm; avoid naming newly invented clergy offices.
10. **River City** — A modest working fishing harbor supplying Renada and the Heartlands, not a major naval port.

## Priority 3 — Approval before generation
11. **Lord Osmo**, mortal military conqueror. A 2:3 character portrait can be commissioned once the author approves his appearance. Do not make him a necromancer, lich or supernatural king.
12. **Rynhold Park memorial**, only after verifying the physical monument and corrected inscription against the public historical record.
13. **Other small towns**, only if an image will represent sourced geography or architecture. Short *Brief Entries* can remain unillustrated.

## Author workflow
1. Generate artwork in ChatGPT or another image tool with the cited public article as visual brief. Use **16:9 landscapes** and **2:3 portraits**. No baked-in titles, map labels, noncanonical flags or event inscriptions.
2. Save files as PNG/JPEG/WebP, with lowercase hyphenated file names such as `stanleyville-forges.png`.
3. Upload to Pages CMS and assign the **article image**, with honest alternative text and caption. The wiki will optimize responsive images.
4. Character article portraits are now eligible for automatic inclusion in Gallery; **new landscape articles still require a manually curated gallery record** if they are also to appear in the Gallery.
5. For replacements, check both the biography and any existing curated Gallery exhibit; image renames alone do not rewire the references.
6. Review any depiction that could imply a new canonical fact (uniform, heraldry, building name, government office, battle participants).

## Don't generate yet
No imaginary Aethrion excavation scene, Godkiller/Cid return, Blackwater Bay post-campaign revelation, unseen gods, historical borders or unverified future events should enter this public collection.

**First request to the author:** Supply or approve only the first four location illustrations (Renada, Black Tower, Heaven's Heart, Stanleyville). The other entries remain optional.
