## Editorial process

1. Direct user corrections take priority over older source documents when they conflict.
2. The published Living Chronicle provides the public historical framing. Keeper-specific material must not be added to the public wiki.
3. Verify names, species, pronouns, offices, places, and temporal relationships before publishing each new group of articles.
4. Run the automated link/ancestry check, then review canon manually. Passing automation **does not certify** narrative accuracy.
5. When an issue cannot be resolved, mark it **OPEN** rather than silently choosing a version.

## Sven's creator-confirmed personal history — November 2026

- **Full Common-tongue name: S'vendyal Shadowwhisperer**, commonly **Sven**. **S'vendyal Gwathrenflos** was spoken by Erevan Ilesere in the divine bargain; **Shadowwhisperer** is the creator-confirmed Common-tongue rendering, superseding the earlier name *Shadowwhisper*. - Sven is an **eladrin** and a carefree Spring-aligned trickster exiled from the Feywild by his parents at age **100** after a disruptive birthday prank. He was sent to the Material Plane to discover responsibility, wandered the Forest of the Fey for about two months, and encountered dangerous fey elves.
- Sven rescued **Kristofer, age five**, a lost human boy. His adoptive father **Tristan** was the child's uncle; Tristan adopted Kristofer after his sister died in childbirth. Sven and Tristan fell in love and raised Kristofer together for **five years**. Sven regarded the boy as his son.
- Sven protected their home from fey incursions. The watchers stopped appearing **toward the end of year four**; Sven relaxed his vigilance. While he searched the fey woods for a gift for **Kristofer's tenth birthday**, the fey elves captured Tristan and Kristofer and burned the home. A **necromancer who led those fey elves** magically bound Sven and forced him to watch their sacrifice, devouring the family's life energy. Its identity and relationship to the Misfits' later enemies are **unconfirmed**.
- Sven called upon **Vandria Gilmadrith**, goddess of war, justice, and grief, who appeared with **Erevan Ilesere**, god of trickery, mischief, and rogues. They freed Sven on condition that he become a rogue assassin serving **justice for wrongs against children and families, never vengeance**.
- Vandria transformed Tristan and Kristofer's bodies into **black and gray stones**, and removed Sven's overwhelming grief as **a second, darker black stone**, turning his once pale green skin **grayscale**. His love and memories remained; grief would never truly leave him but would no longer overpower his purpose.
- Erevan transformed a branch from Sven's binding tree into **a silver ring** incorporating the stones. He gave Sven the Common-tongue name **Shadowwhisperer**, warned that placing the ring on his finger would bind him to the quest, and promised that its gift would reveal itself in time. Sven put on the ring. **Do not invent the ring's eventual power**.
- Source: Creator-supplied *S'vendyal Background Bullets* and *Drawing Color* narrative, November 2026. These facts supplement the earlier PDFs, which did not contain the personal backstory.
