# Public canon editorial overrides

This file records user-approved corrections that take precedence over older, conflicting source editions. It contains **no Keeper-only plot information**.

## Confirmed correction — Samuel the Devoted

- **Samuel is a Monadic Deva, not an aasimar.** The creator corrected an earlier informal use of “aasimar” after providing the original World Anvil backstory (November 2026).
- The older Master Canon Bible, Compendium, and Keeper references using “Monadic Deva” agree with this latest correction. The former aasimar override is obsolete and must not be copied forward.
- Samuel serves as the Devoted on the Aegis and trains paladins at Heaven's Heart. These office facts remain unchanged.
- **Astronoph** is a female deva and High Priest of the Great Cathedral. Her species and office should not be altered as part of Samuel's correction.
- **Jeyse** is a deva; **Cal'el** is an aasimar. These individuals are distinct from Samuel.

## Other preserved editorial distinctions

- Jaznaar is a drow spellcaster. His wizard-versus-sorcerer class is **not settled** in the later source ledger.
- The original Misfits included Fo'Gurn. The five surviving and publicly celebrated Misfits were Lavitz, Tel'Lora, Sven, Heureux, and Amara.
- The Great Citadel and Great Cathedral belong **inside Renada**, not as duplicate world-map pins.
- Karhold's constitutional position remains disputed; a claimed place on an Avalorian map does not settle its political allegiance.
- Aldus Greystone III and Cid the Blood King were the same individual. Do not give them mutually incompatible timelines or describe them as separate people.
- The Ripped Beard, Moonville Manor, and several other early encounters have limited public descriptions. Do not turn missing testimony into invented certainties.

## Editorial process

1. Direct user corrections take priority over older source documents when they conflict.
2. The published Living Chronicle provides the public historical framing. Keeper-specific material must not be added to the public wiki.
3. Verify names, species, pronouns, offices, places, and temporal relationships before publishing each new group of articles.
4. Run the automated link/ancestry check, then review canon manually. Passing automation **does not certify** narrative accuracy.
5. When an issue cannot be resolved, mark it **OPEN** rather than silently choosing a version.

This is public biography shared directly by the creator.

## Institutional histories confirmed by the creator — November 2026

- **Lion's Bane** is a **substantial elite royal guard**, not a ceremonial handful of bodyguards. It is commanded by Lord Marshal Lavitz Shawe, undertakes exceptionally dangerous missions, and includes a **gryffion-mounted contingent**. No precise headcount, rank hierarchy, or special wing name has yet been approved.
- **Dina Hyanda** has **exactly twelve women**, each trained by exceptional instructors from across Avalor; **some are former adventurers**. The order was founded by Queen Melmiri. Do not invent membership names, ranks, recruitment rituals or an organizational link that subordinates it to Lion's Bane.
- **The Silver Forge**, the Clerics Guild now based in **Stannisport**, was established with Avalor's unification in **1 AF**, consolidating loose cross-kingdom networks of clerics. Its defining values include **compassion, profound wisdom, formidable ability, and respect for all deities, beliefs, and spiritual paths**. **Petris** learned there and later taught; **Aldus Greystone III** studied healing there and later returned while investigating how to abolish death. Do not imply the guild supported his cruelty.
- Avalor's **Thieves Guild** is **one branch** of a loose network of professional thieves providing mutual support for illicit endeavors and clients, with **branches elsewhere across the world**. **Salt** is the present male leader of the **Avalorian branch**. Rumors say he answers to **the Ragged Man**, but neither that person's identity nor the rumor's accuracy is confirmed. Do not develop overseas branches without additional permission.
- **Avalorian Standing Army:** existing professional army, royal authority, sheriffdom administration, regional garrisons and militias remain canon. Medieval English levy/contract/retinue arrangements are **historical inspiration only**, not yet approved as exact Avalorian institutions, ranks, taxes or recruitment law.


## Approved internal organizations — November 2026

The creator explicitly selected the organizational options listed here, superseding earlier notes that treated them as proposals:

- **Avalorian Standing Army — hybrid structure (Option C).** Four complementary sources: professional **Standing Regiments**, **Noble Retinues** of armed households, **Regional Musters** raised when needed, and **Local Militias** protecting communities. Keep the permanent royal army, the existing regional sheriffdoms, and distinction between civil administration and military operational command. The precise compulsory-service law, payments, size, ranks, and tenure of soldiers are not yet defined.
- **Lion's Bane — three divisions (Option B).** The **Royal Guard** protects the monarch and royal household; **the Lion's Claws** are elite ground-operating teams entrusted with high-risk rescues, raids and special assignments; **the Skyward Guard** is the gryffion-mounted contingent providing aerial mobility, reconnaissance, and rapid response. All belong to **one** elite order under Lord Marshal Lavitz Shawe. No final numbers, detailed ranks, or wing commander names have been established.
- **Dina Hyanda — three circles of four (Option C).** Exactly **twelve women** in **three flexible circles of four**. The circles can rotate among close protection, advance security and readiness/recovery positions according to need; these are adaptable responsibilities, not fixed permanent ranks. **One of the twelve** provides overall leadership; no thirteenth commander or named title has been established. The order belongs to Queen Melmiri, not Lion's Bane.
- **The Silver Forge — three disciplines (Option B).** **Healing Hand** (healing and medicine); **Guiding Light** (counsel, teaching, guidance, grief and mediation); **Shield of Mercy** (defensive protection of the vulnerable and healers). A cleric may practice across disciplines; they are not separate faiths or new standalone organizations. No Conclave of Voices or prescribed guild hierarchy is approved yet.
- **Thieves Guild — independent crews under Salt (Option C).** Avalor's branch in Olivruch comprises independently operating crews. **Salt** coordinates shared work and mediates conflicting interests rather than personally controlling every job. No rigid code, titles, dues or foreign-branch details are established. The **Ragged Man** remains a rumor only.
- The earlier **Lion's Bane 150–250 headcount**, **First Spear** name and alternative single-division, rank and guild-college proposals remain unapproved. Do not import these as canon.
