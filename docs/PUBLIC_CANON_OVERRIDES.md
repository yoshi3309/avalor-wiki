# Public canon editorial overrides

This file records user-approved corrections that take precedence over older, conflicting source editions. It contains **no Keeper-only plot information**.

## Confirmed correction — Samuel the Devoted

- **Samuel is a Monadic Deva, not an aasimar.** The creator corrected an earlier informal use of “aasimar” after providing the original World Anvil backstory (November 2026).
- The older Master Canon Bible, Compendium, and Keeper references using “Monadic Deva” agree with this latest correction. The former aasimar override is obsolete and must not be copied forward.
- Samuel serves as the Devoted on the Aegis and trains paladins at Heaven's Heart. These office facts remain unchanged.
- **Astronoph** is a female deva and High Priest of the Great Cathedral. Her species and office should not be altered as part of Samuel's correction.
- **Jeyse** is a deva; **Cal'el** is an aasimar. These individuals are distinct from Samuel.

## Other preserved editorial distinctions

- Jaznaar is a drow spellcaster. His wizard-versus-sorcerer class is **not settled** in the later source ledger.
- The original Misfits included Fo'Gurn. The five surviving and publicly celebrated Misfits were Lavitz, Tel'Lora, Sven, Heureux, and Amara.
- The Great Citadel and Great Cathedral belong **inside Renada**, not as duplicate world-map pins.
- Karhold's constitutional position remains disputed; a claimed place on an Avalorian map does not settle its political allegiance.
- Aldus Greystone III and Cid the Blood King were the same individual. Do not give them mutually incompatible timelines or describe them as separate people.
- The Ripped Beard, Moonville Manor, and several other early encounters have limited public descriptions. Do not turn missing testimony into invented certainties.

## Editorial process

1. Direct user corrections take priority over older source documents when they conflict.
2. The published Living Chronicle provides the public historical framing. Keeper-specific material must not be added to the public wiki.
3. Verify names, species, pronouns, offices, places, and temporal relationships before publishing each new group of articles.
4. Run the automated link/ancestry check, then review canon manually. Passing automation **does not certify** narrative accuracy.
5. When an issue cannot be resolved, mark it **OPEN** rather than silently choosing a version.

The creator explicitly selected the organizational options listed here, superseding earlier notes that treated them as proposals:

- **Avalorian Standing Army — hybrid structure (Option C).** Four complementary sources: professional **Standing Regiments**, **Noble Retinues** of armed households, **Regional Musters** raised when needed, and **Local Militias** protecting communities. The circles can rotate among close protection, advance security and readiness/recovery positions according to need; these are adaptable responsibilities, not fixed permanent ranks. **One of the twelve** provides overall leadership; no thirteenth commander or named title has been established. No rigid code, titles, dues or foreign-branch details are established. The **Ragged Man** remains a rumor only.
- The earlier **Lion's Bane 150–250 headcount**, **First Spear** name and alternative single-division, rank and guild-college proposals remain unapproved. Do not import these as canon.

## Sven's creator-confirmed personal history — November 2026

- **Full Common-tongue name: S'vendyal Shadowwhisperer**, commonly **Sven**. **S'vendyal Gwathrenflos** was spoken by Erevan Ilesere in the divine bargain; **Shadowwhisperer** is the creator-confirmed Common-tongue rendering, superseding the earlier name *Shadowwhisper*. Retain the existing wiki slug svendyal-shadowwhisper to avoid breaking links; retain the old spelling as a search alias only.
- Sven is an **eladrin** and a carefree Spring-aligned trickster exiled from the Feywild by his parents at age **100** after a disruptive birthday prank. He was sent to the Material Plane to discover responsibility, wandered the Forest of the Fey for about two months, and encountered dangerous fey elves.
- Sven rescued **Kristofer, age five**, a lost human boy. His adoptive father **Tristan** was the child's uncle; Tristan adopted Kristofer after his sister died in childbirth. Sven and Tristan fell in love and raised Kristofer together for **five years**. Sven regarded the boy as his son.
- Sven protected their home from fey incursions. The watchers stopped appearing **toward the end of year four**; Sven relaxed his vigilance. While he searched the fey woods for a gift for **Kristofer's tenth birthday**, the fey elves captured Tristan and Kristofer and burned the home. A **necromancer who led those fey elves** magically bound Sven and forced him to watch their sacrifice, devouring the family's life energy. Its identity and relationship to the Misfits' later enemies are **unconfirmed**.
- Sven called upon **Vandria Gilmadrith**, goddess of war, justice, and grief, who appeared with **Erevan Ilesere**, god of trickery, mischief, and rogues. They freed Sven on condition that he become a rogue assassin serving **justice for wrongs against children and families, never vengeance**.
- Vandria transformed Tristan and Kristofer's bodies into **black and gray stones**, and removed Sven's overwhelming grief as **a second, darker black stone**, turning his once pale green skin **grayscale**. His love and memories remained; grief would never truly leave him but would no longer overpower his purpose.
- Erevan transformed a branch from Sven's binding tree into **a silver ring** incorporating the stones. He gave Sven the Common-tongue name **Shadowwhisperer**, warned that placing the ring on his finger would bind him to the quest, and promised that its gift would reveal itself in time. Sven put on the ring. **Do not invent the ring's eventual power**.
- Source: Creator-supplied *S'vendyal Background Bullets* and *Drawing Color* narrative, November 2026. These facts supplement the earlier PDFs, which did not contain the personal backstory.

## Heureux (H) expanded creator background — November 2026

- **Heureux, also known as H**, is a **blue-furred firbolg bard** from the **Ringtree Clan** of the Forest of the Fey. His clan lived in semi-nomadic, concealed canopy settlements protected by illusions and invisibility.
- The Ringtree Clan treated historical memory and oral and written traditions as sacred. Its animating spiritual force was called **Her**, believed present in nature, music, invention, growth, decay, and memory. H remains spiritually devoted to Her.
- H's father was the clan's leading historian and prepared H to preserve its stories. He showed H the hidden **Yesteryear Tome**, containing suppressed truths about the clan's origins and customs. H transcribed discoveries as **songs and lyrics** and memorized them.
- That night the **clan library burned**, H's father disappeared in the fire, and H fled through the forest pursued by agents loyal to the clan chief. The creator explicitly confirmed that **"Sid" was a misspelling of Cid the Blood King**. The Ringtree clan chief was manipulated by forces connected to **Cid and mind flayers**. The involvement is established by the creator; its exact mechanism, individual agents, and the father's final fate have not been specified.
- He became a **wandering folklorist** and entertainer, using **dulcimer, drums, and shawm**, folk tradition, blues-inspired ballads, satire, and communal storytelling. His warm, humorous style carries pointed observations about culture and history. He keeps watch for threatened or manipulated historical knowledge.
- H carries private **guilt over fleeing the burning library and leaving his father**, and fears the erasure or rewriting of cultures and their history more than death. His positive manner is genuine, not a denial of that grief.
- H's **otter companion Slick** is described by the creator as his closest companion and greatest love, embodying joy and gentleness. Do not assert a species-independent romantic interpretation or magical abilities not supplied.
- He maintains contact with common people through travel despite his fame and his founding of Heureux's Bard Academy. He is a founding Six descendant through **Kelsalor**, joined the Misfits during their second commission, and later wrote and taught without ceasing his travels.
- His guiding words: **"Our truth is our history, and our history is our truth."**
- Source: creator-supplied Heureux expanded backstory in conversation, November 2026. Preserve in public biography; older compiled PDFs did not develop this origin.
