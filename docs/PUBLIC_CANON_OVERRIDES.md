# Public canon editorial overrides

This file records user-approved corrections that take precedence over older, conflicting source editions. It contains **no Keeper-only plot information**.

## Confirmed correction — Samuel the Devoted

- **Samuel is an aasimar, not a deva.**
- The older Master Canon Bible, Compendium, and Keeper editions call Samuel a “Monadic Deva.” For public wiki content this is **outdated** and must not be copied forward.
- Samuel serves as the Devoted on the Aegis and trains paladins at Heaven's Heart. These office facts remain unchanged.
- **Astronoph** is a female deva and High Priest of the Great Cathedral. Her species and office should not be altered as part of Samuel's correction.
- **Jeyse** is a deva; **Cal'el** is an aasimar. These individuals are distinct from Samuel.

## Other preserved editorial distinctions

- Jaznaar is a drow spellcaster. His wizard-versus-sorcerer class is **not settled** in the later source ledger.
- The original Misfits included Fo'Gurn. The five surviving and publicly celebrated Misfits were Lavitz, Tel'Lora, Sven, Heureux, and Amara.
- The Great Citadel and Great Cathedral belong **inside Renada**, not as duplicate world-map pins.
- Karhold's constitutional position remains disputed; a claimed place on an Avalorian map does not settle its political allegiance.
- Aldus Greystone III and Cid the Blood King were the same individual. Do not give them mutually incompatible timelines or describe them as separate people.
- The Ripped Beard, Moonville Manor, and several other early encounters have limited public descriptions. Do not turn missing testimony into invented certainties.

## Editorial process

1. Direct user corrections take priority over older source documents when they conflict.
2. The published Living Chronicle provides the public historical framing. Keeper-specific material must not be added to the public wiki.
3. Verify names, species, pronouns, offices, places, and temporal relationships before publishing each new group of articles.
4. Run the automated link/ancestry check, then review canon manually. Passing automation **does not certify** narrative accuracy.
5. When an issue cannot be resolved, mark it **OPEN** rather than silently choosing a version.
