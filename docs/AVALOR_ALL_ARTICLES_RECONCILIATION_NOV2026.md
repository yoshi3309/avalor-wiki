# Avalor public archive: article-by-article reconciliation register

**Review date:** 12 November 2026  
**Repository:** `yoshi3309/avalor-wiki`  
**Draft branch:** `editorial/ashenfall-mera-noke-nov2026`  
**Review state:** Draft PR #8; **not merged or deployed**.

## Result and method

- The source archive contained **112 published public articles** before this round. Four new public accounts are staged: Anna, Ros Furband, the Emerald of Armanti and Mera Ashenfall.
- The **116-article public corpus** was screened for recurring continuity conflicts, obsolete terminology, misplaced Amara capture chronology, and leaks of intentionally restricted story information.
- **27 articles** are added or revised in the draft; the other **89 articles** remain unchanged because no issue within this review's defined scope required an edit. The changed articles also received focused manual narrative and cross-reference work. No claim is made that each unchanged article has been line-edited against every sentence of the private Keeper manuscripts.
- Non-deploying GitHub Actions runs validate front matter, unique entry IDs, related slugs, wiki links, article output, narrative navigation, and the site build. This register supplements those tests; it does not replace them.
- Only **publicly authorized** historical information is allowed in article text. The creator authorized Noke's survival and current service to the Den Mother as public knowledge. Anna's undisclosed identity, the Emerald's exact occult purpose, the mechanics of Fo'Gurn's extraordinary return, and Jinlin's private ancient origin are not published.

## Principal editorial decisions

1. **Companionship:** The first adventuring company included Lavitz, Tel'Lora, Sven, Amara and Fo'Gurn; Heureux joined during Gol'Goroth. Anna and Jack traveled with them without becoming founding adventurers.
2. **Anna:** A tavern waitress met during the search for Lowkee's Keeper, keeper of the Emerald of Armanti, rescued from the temple, later imprisoned aboard Ros Furband's ship and present in the last campaign. The circumstances behind her undisclosed history remain private.
3. **Noki / Noke:** The disfigurement occurred **at the end of the encounter**, not beforehand; **Noke** is the later name. The Den Mother is already a known character in the current campaign and may be mentioned publicly.
4. **Campaign order:** Moonville Manor; unnamed-necromancer commission; Gol'Goroth and Heureux; Noki; ruined temple and hydra; Stanleyville; Rotten Husk; Jaznaar; Renada Games / Tournament; Ros Furband; actual Jinlin defeat; Zauks's false lives; the final Shadowfell struggle. Anna's discovery belongs on the early road; Amara's separate capture belongs **after the Games**.
5. **Witnesses and relics:** The Emerald of Armanti and Cid's Bane are separate objects, linked to Lowkee in different ways. The public archive describes the final visible confrontation but not its withheld supernatural mechanism.
6. **Aftermath:** Separate lives and royal appointments, the friendship of the original and later companions, and the memory of Fo'Gurn, Jack, and Anna are all part of the Age of Ascendancy.

## Remaining editorial limits

- A green build establishes that the articles and supporting data **function**, not that unrecorded history has suddenly become knowable.
- Do not independently publish restricted contents from the four Keeper reference volumes. New creator-supplied decisions in this round should be reflected in a **future source-book addendum or revised edition** if the books are reissued.
- Do not merge the draft until the Keeper has reviewed the new Anna, Ros Furband, Emerald and Mera articles and the revised Misfits and Noke biographies.
- Source books and the public wiki have different disclosure boundaries; agreement on the public historical sequence does not require publication of every cause and mechanism known privately.
