# People & Legends — Storytelling editorial standard

**Established November 2026 after a complete review of all 37 People & Legends entries.**

## The voice

The Royal Archives should read like a **living history told by people who care about the people in it**. An archive voice is welcome, but it must not sound like a modern content audit or an out-of-world editor explaining why the author did not invent something.

The reader should finish a biography knowing **what the character is like and why they matter**, not merely what their organization does.

- **Lead with the person:** a vivid established choice, defining relationship, habit, turning point, skill, or loss.
- **Let the story carry uncertainty.** A missing detail usually needs no disclaimer. When uncertainty is historically consequential—Jaznaar's spellcasting class, Fo'Gurn's return, the identity of the black hand—state it briefly, in-world, and do not silently choose a version.
- **Do not pad a biography.** A short, lively record is preferable to several paragraphs repeating the jurisdiction, founding of a school, history of a city or powers of a council. Link the relevant parent article instead.
- **Do not transform inference into fact.** A character's internal monologue, desire, relationship, origin, ability, and pivotal scene require creator approval or support from source. Evocative wording must not sneak an unsupported new event into public canon.
- **Keep distinct histories distinct.** The Misfits and their encounters belong in the fellowship article; the Rotten Husk clan and Wrath of Innocent remain groups; the Black Stair leads to the Black Tower; the Aegis is its own subject, not repeated in each councilor's biography.
- **Preserve the current creator corrections.** Samuel is a Monadic Deva; Miren a dwarf woman; Xiomara a drow woman; Halden a human man. Cassian's early piracy remains set in unspecified distant waters without expanding foreign lore. Species, offices, relationships, and event chronology must survive every tone pass.
- **Respect spoiler boundaries.** Public pages must not disclose Keeper-only information or future campaign plots.

## Before and after

**Overly editorial:**
> The precise waters, vessels, and exploits of those years have not been established in the Avalorian record; the wider world's geography remains outside this account.

**Narrative:**
> Those years among pirates brought danger, excitement, and the freedom he had once craved. He returned from them with stories he seldom tells in full—and with instincts that still serve him in the council chamber.

The second passage is not permission to invent names or dates. It makes the established character history compelling without explaining the limits of the project.

**Overly editorial:**
> The Archives cannot reconstruct every lost year of his life, and should not turn disputed spiritual testimony into invented certainty.

**Narrative:**
> The mysteries surrounding his fate have never mattered as much to his companions as the man himself.

## Article shape

1. **A memorable opening**, anchored in a confirmed identity or event.
2. **Personal history and formative events**, wherever author-approved material exists.
3. **A relationship, virtue, weakness, or decisive choice** supported by canon.
4. **Present responsibilities**, briefly described with links to organizations and regions.
5. **A closing line** that returns to the person's character or legacy.

These are ingredients, not mandatory sections. A small character may need only a few paragraphs. Important NPCs and player characters can naturally be longer.

## November 2026 audit results

Reviewed **all 37 People & Legends entries**. **26** were substantively revised or lightly polished during the audit; **11** already worked well enough to preserve without needless rewriting. The purpose was consistency of storytelling and preservation of established lore, not a mandatory word count.

Key improvements included fully refocusing **Malikeah, Jaaris, Noki, Jack, Astronoph, Faelynn and Bradgem Glav**; then polishing founding-era figures and the Misfits, and removing disjointed disclaimer-like sentences throughout major biographies.

**No articles were consolidated or deleted in this pass**, and no campaign mysteries were resolved without approval. Species, pronouns, former titles, and relationships remain subject to source checking. Build/link validation is necessary but not a substitute for narrative canon review.

## Character scope and proportionality - Creator-approved 12 November 2026

Only major protagonists and antagonists whose choices drove consequential, connected arcs warrant substantial biographies. For short-lived villains, supporting officers and incidental figures, prefer **two meaningful paragraphs** establishing identity, motivation and one relevant action or relationship; add more only if distinctive confirmed history justifies it. A named character's mere existence does not require invented childhoods, family trees, exact dates or exhaustive fictional completeness. A brief entry can be the final entry. Newly proposed motivations remain outside public canon until approved.


## Minor-character collections and inline illustrations

Two Wikipedia-style indexes now hold the short biographies of minor heroes/allies and minor villains/adversaries. Each biography is a `## Character Name` section with a **stable generated heading anchor**. The existing site redirects retired standalone slugs and article links to those anchors; old links must not break when records are consolidated. Keep a short, useful story under each heading rather than creating another full page.

Group appropriate figures by their narrative function, **not just morality**: officers, companions and protectors together; captors and hostile enforcers together. Do not place major villains, central protagonists, former monarchs or characters whose stories warrant independent reading into an index merely to reduce article counts.

Small in-body portraits are welcome when artwork is actually of the named person and approved for the public wiki. Use validated uploaded images with alt text and captions; **do not label a group illustration or an unrelated image as someone's portrait**. The Royal Forces group art in the heroes index is explicitly contextual, not a portrait.
