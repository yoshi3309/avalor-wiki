**Captain Funkin** served among the Renadan officers whose duties brought them into the Misfits' struggle against Cid. When the Stalker killed **[[kel-maris|Kel'Maris]]** in [[Stanleyville]], Funkin **safely returned the Lord Marshal's body to Renada**. His part in that journey allowed Avalor to honor the Lion's Shadow in a state funeral and carry out her final wishes.

Funkin survived Stanleyville, but not the adventure that followed. At the **later ruined temple**, Cid's servants held prisoners and attempted a blood rite that ended in destruction and the emergence of a hydra. **Funkin died there**, along with [[inspector-sihnion|Inspector Sihnion]] and Renadan guards. His story is brief, but his two last acts belong in their correct order: he brought one fallen protector home before becoming another person the kingdom had to mourn.
