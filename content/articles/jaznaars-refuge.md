## The road to the last of the Six

The Misfits' investigation eventually brought them toward a **hidden refuge occupied by [[Jaznaar]]**, the drow spellcaster who had survived the founding generation for almost two thousand years.

Their inquiries led to a passage associated with the tomb of **Bradgem Glav**, the warrior who died defeating Lord Osmo at [[Heroes Hill]]. Heureux's account also mentions an object connected with [[Tel'Lora Ebbenflow]]'s family.

The companions have not disclosed the full means of access, and the Royal Archives do not reconstruct those details from conjecture. The refuge has **no independently verified public map position**.

## A man the kingdom had condemned

For centuries, Avalor's official histories called Jaznaar the **Betrayer**, blaming him for the catastrophe at the [[Black Tower]].

At the refuge, Jaznaar told the adventurers that the celebrated healer **Aldus Greystone III** and **Cid the Blood King** were the same being. He had spent the intervening centuries struggling to contain the consequences of Aldus's work even while the kingdom reviled him.

He also instructed Tel’Lora and shared knowledge essential to the companions' understanding. The full contents of those lessons remain outside the public record.

## A correction established through evidence

The meeting was decisive, but the Royal Commission does not rest its conclusion solely upon the words of one survivor or the memories of his friends. Subsequent inquiry and independently examined evidence supported the correction.

**Jaznaar died after the Misfits departed.** They later laid him to rest in Renada beside the companions of his founding generation. He had survived to tell the truth, but not to hear the kingdom formally acknowledge his innocence.

## A legacy beyond the refuge

The discovery altered the understanding of Cid, the Black Tower, and the treatment of drow communities shaped by the old accusation.

The hidden refuge matters not because it supplies a new dungeon to chart, but because it marks the moment when the companions first received an explanation capable of overturning nearly two thousand years of accepted history.
