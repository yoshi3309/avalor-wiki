## The druid among unlikely companions

**Amara**, a druid and descendant of [[Petris]] of [[The Six]], belonged to the early adventures of [[The Misfits]]. She came from a family line associated with a cleric remembered for compassion and care for those displaced by the founding wars.

Descent does not require imitation. Petris served through the faith of Lathander; Amara's later calling carried her into the natural world. Their different paths belong to the same long inheritance without being identical.

## An owl in a false world

During the ordeal imposed by **Zauks**, a mind flayer who turned the companions’ memories against them, the Misfits experienced convincing false lives in which their grief and loss seemed to have been erased.

Heureux's published history mentions a **recurring owl associated with Amara** as one clue that these seemingly ideal lives were not real. The private details of what the individual companions saw have not been fully made public.

The ordeal remains important because it tested the willingness of the adventurers to return to a world where happiness was uncertain and love exposed them to loss.

## Journeys beyond the capital

After the Blood King's defeat, Amara continued traveling in service of nature, increasingly far from the places where royal chroniclers collected their accounts. Her appearances became rare enough that popular stories began to resemble folklore.

The scarcity of testimony is not evidence of idleness, estrangement, or death. The Commission cannot turn the absence of reports into a detailed itinerary or an account of achievements for which no witness has been named.

## Still one of the five

Lavitz and Tel’Lora remained visible in royal office, Sven entered the world of rumor, and [[Heureux]] continued to perform and preserve their story. Amara took another course.

The five were not reunited as a complete company during the following twenty years, but their friendship endured. Amara's place among them is not diminished by the fact that much of her continuing service occurred beyond the capital’s sight.

The world she protected may remember her in ways a court archive cannot.
