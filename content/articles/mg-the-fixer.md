## The name that finds you

In the taverns and merchant houses of Avalor, there are problems respectable people seldom wish to describe aloud. An heirloom is stolen and must be recovered quietly. A debt threatens to ruin a family. A magistrate knows too much, or a ship's manifest says something its owner would prefer the world not read.

Among those who claim to arrange solutions, few names are spoken as cautiously as **MG the Fixer**.

Those who seek MG rarely know where to look. Yet a sufficiently important problem and a sufficiently persuasive purse have a way of bringing the Fixer to the person who needs help. Often the first sign of MG's involvement is not an introduction but the disappearance of an obstacle.

The results have inspired confidence in some and considerable suspicion in others.

## A person without one description

No two accounts of MG's appearance seem to agree. One witness describes an old sailor with a silver beard. Another remembers a young noble with polished manners. A third insists the Fixer appeared as a woman in rich merchant silks.

The contradictory descriptions have only enlarged the reputation. Was each witness mistaken? Were some encounters arranged by intermediaries? Had MG deliberately cultivated the confusion? Those who deal in secrets have little reason to offer certainty where uncertainty serves them better.

MG's connections reach into the criminal and commercial world around **[[The Smaller Settlements of Avalor#olivruch|Olivruch]]**, a town associated with the **[[The Guilds of Avalor#the-thieves-guild|Sea Serpents]]**. The guild's independent crews give capable operators room to pursue their own methods while finding useful allies among people who understand the value of discretion.

The Fixer's standing rests not upon a public office but upon the belief that a difficult problem can be put into careful hands.

## Every conversation has a price

MG is associated with collecting information, negotiating behind closed doors, recovering objects, untangling bureaucratic difficulties, and finding a way through obstacles others have accepted as permanent. The methods favor leverage and persuasion over open violence.

The Fixer's practical outlook is not one of charity. Information is worth more when it can be traded; a living opponent may yet become a valuable source; a quiet outcome is often more useful than a dramatic victory.

The secrecy surrounding MG has also made the name a subject of rumor. People tell stories of the fixer they met, the voice they heard, and the agreement they were certain had been made. The accounts seldom resolve into a single portrait.

## An offer from the Lord Marshal

MG came among the unconventional operatives recruited by **[[Lavitz Shawe]]** for the undertaking connected to **Blackwater Bay**. Where other recruits brought unusual magic, martial skill, or knowledge of forgotten ruins, MG offered the capacity to find people, read interests, and solve problems that could not be approached openly.

Whether the court's trust could be earned by a person who had built a career on discretion was an unanswered question at the moment of recruitment.

One thing was clear enough: MG understood that a mission is not always won by the person who draws the first blade. Sometimes it is won long before the enemy knows a contest has begun.
