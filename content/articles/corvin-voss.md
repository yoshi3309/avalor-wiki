## The man who knew how to leave

In [[Blackwater Bay]], survival was often a matter of recognizing danger before everyone else and knowing how to escape it. **Corvin Voss** was good at both.

Born among the city's docks, Corvin built a life by moving goods and people through the spaces between ordinary commerce and the underworld. He understood which harbors welcomed strangers, which officials examined every manifest, and which captains asked fewer questions than they should. His charm could buy time; his knowledge of ships and streets could turn that time into an exit.

Yet Corvin was not quite the self-interested rogue he sometimes appeared to be. He believed there was one thing worse than losing a valuable shipment: **leaving behind the people who trusted you to get them home**.

That code would make him an unlikely companion to the operatives of **2020 AF**, and nearly cost him his life.

## A child of the docks

Corvin was **a human of thirty-three in 2020 AF**. He had grown up among Blackwater's sailors, traders, longshore workers and smugglers, learning to recognize ships by their crews, cargo arrangements and habits of departure long before he had the resources to own any such vessel himself.

The harbor taught him that a respectable trade and an unlawful one could use the same warehouses, routes and people. An honest merchant might need an unregistered passage; a smuggler might keep a promise that a uniformed official would break. None of those ambiguities made Blackwater a just city, but Corvin learned to navigate them.

As an adult, he presented the bearing of a man accustomed to both trouble and conversation: **dark, unruly wavy hair, watchful dark eyes, a rough beard, and a face weathered by dockside work and hard travel**. He wore battered dark leather over a worn, open-collared shirt, a strap across his chest, and practical blades close at hand. His careworn appearance did not obscure a quick, knowing smile.

Corvin became a skilled negotiator and guide. He could talk himself into rooms where he did not belong and, more importantly, find routes back out. But he regarded trust as more difficult to acquire than money.

## A smuggler's rule

Corvin was neither innocent of smuggling nor ashamed of his ability to deceive a customs officer. He believed that laws and personal loyalties were not always the same thing.

His rule was straightforward: **never abandon your crew**.

That principle did not require him to fight every battle or protect every piece of cargo. Sometimes survival meant leaving goods behind, forfeiting a payment, or running before a fight became a massacre. What mattered was that everyone who had entrusted their safety to him had a chance to make the same escape.

The distinction defined his friendship with **[[minor-characters#seb-bramble|Seb Bramble]]**, a halfling merchant whose legitimate documents and warehouse business would later prove indispensable to the Blackwater expedition.

## The cargo that went into the sea

Around **2008 AF**, roughly twelve years before the Blackwater Bay undertaking, a dangerous encounter cemented the friendship between Corvin and Seb.

Seb's ship was carrying cargo belonging to **[[ros-furband|Ros Furband]]**, the pirate captain known from his earlier, paid capture of the Misfits in **2000 AF**. During the voyage, a band of outlaws attempted to seize Seb's vessel. The danger was not simply that goods might be stolen: the ship and those aboard it were at risk of being taken.

Corvin faced an ugly choice. He could preserve the valuable shipment entrusted to their care, or he could create the opening Seb needed to save his vessel and crew.

**He sacrificed Ros Furband's cargo.**

With the shipment lost, Seb and his people escaped the outlaws' attempt to take the ship. The cargo's precise contents, the outlaws' identities, and Ros's subsequent reaction have not been established. Nothing in the account proves that Ros belonged to that gang or ordered the attack. What mattered to the two friends was that Corvin had chosen lives over a valuable delivery, even though the cargo belonged to a pirate whose name carried real danger.

The act cost Corvin a profitable shipment and placed him at risk of the consequences of losing another man's goods. To Seb, it proved something money could not purchase: Corvin would return for him when an easier man would run.

Their association continued into the following decade, grounded in business knowledge and that older act of loyalty.

## The secret beneath the Black Diamond

By **2020 AF**, Corvin knew Blackwater Bay's criminal networks and guarded merchant district well enough to be useful—and dangerous—to the **[[The Black Diamond|Black Diamond]]**.

The organization controlled checkpoints, patrols, trade records and an excavation complex attached to ancient structures. Its interest extended beyond ordinary smuggling into forbidden magical work.

Corvin learned that people were being harmed in the excavation's blood-magic experiments. The sight of unwilling people treated as expendable materials shocked even a man accustomed to Blackwater's brutality. He had not discovered the full nature of the immense artifact associated with the Diamond, but he had witnessed enough to recognize the cost of its pursuit of power.

The Black Diamond imprisoned him at its excavation outpost. The circumstances of every victim and the full workings of the experiments remain uncertain, and Corvin did not suddenly acquire knowledge of the artifact's ultimate purpose. He was a witness the organization wanted silenced.

The man who had made a living finding ways out of dangerous places could find no clear exit of his own.

## The five strangers and their improbable guide

Corvin's captivity ended when five visitors infiltrated the excavation. **[[Silas Dreadmore]]**, **[[Naeris Thorne]]**, **[[mg-the-fixer|MG the Fixer]]**, **[[Notfred]]**, and **[[Ambrose Burntwood]]** had been sent covertly by **[[Lavitz Shawe]]** to investigate the Black Diamond's artifact.

After killing two guards in an initial confrontation, the outsiders relied on disguise and a fabricated prisoner-transfer order to take Corvin from custody. Their deception succeeded, and Corvin was released into their company.

He could have fled. Instead he helped the people who had freed him.

From an overlook he showed them the harbor and described three practical ways to enter the Black Diamond-controlled district: hidden smuggler passages, merchant cover, or a waterborne approach. The group chose the merchant gate.

Corvin brought them to Seb Bramble. Through Seb's legitimate trade contacts, the travelers secured credible documents, insignia and clothing, then passed the checkpoint disguised as merchants. Once safely inside, Corvin and Seb laughed at the amount of anxiety and effort the newcomers had devoted to entry.

To anyone who knew Blackwater, **getting inside was much easier than getting out**.

Seb departed after escorting them through. Corvin stayed with the operatives and the small Tide-Touched kobold **[[Skrik, the Tide Blessed|Skrik]]**, who had joined them earlier in the adventure.

## Stealing the impossible

Inside the Black Diamond's district, the company gathered news at the **Artifact's Rest** and discovered that Esogoth's immense cargo was about to depart from **Pier Seven**. The artifact was too large to lift, conceal or carry away.

The others saw an impossible theft. Corvin saw a ship.

**If they could not remove the object, they could steal the vessel transporting it.**

The idea captured Corvin's familiarity with harbor operations. A ship was not merely timber and canvas: it depended upon routine, predictable orders, a captain, mooring lines and sailors who expected others to know their work. Interfere with the right part of that system, and a guarded departure could become an escape.

The crew approached the vessel by water and boarded from the stern ladder, using deception instead of announcing a battle on deck. Silas magically influenced the captain while MG occupied the lieutenant; other companions released the lines and made ready to depart. Ambrose summoned the winds needed to overcome the Black Diamond's attempts to halt the ship.

The vessel passed through the dangerous **Teeth**, escaped pursuing craft, and then rose impossibly into the sky after its monstrous cargo awakened.

Corvin had proposed stealing a ship. None of them had expected it to fly.

## The choice above the sea

The airborne vessel endured damage, unnatural behavior and aerial attacks. The Black Diamond sent Retrievers to reclaim its cargo, followed by **[[minor-villains-and-adversaries#gladios-and-shadowthorn|Gladios]]** on the wyvern **Shadowthorn**.

Gladios repeatedly attempted to seize Naeris alive. The sea elf's condition, called **Blood Touch**, had made him important to the artifact and those trying to recover it.

Corvin had every practical reason to stay away from the struggle. His own part in the mission had already carried him from imprisonment to the open sky. He could have tried to save himself.

But Naeris belonged to the company that had rescued him.

Corvin intervened to prevent Gladios from taking the sea elf and suffered a severe stabbing wound to the abdomen. He nearly died. His companions recovered and stabilized him, stopping the bleeding and preserving his life. Gladios eventually abandoned his efforts and escaped alive on Shadowthorn.

**Corvin had chosen his crew over his own safety**, just as he had done years earlier when Seb's ship was threatened.

## A volunteer for the living Anchor

The danger did not end with the pursuers' retreat. The flying vessel resisted attempts to steer it toward Renada, its course bending toward the **[[Black Tower]]**. The artifact and the strange machinery around it seemed inseparably joined.

Stavros, a former Black Diamond captive who understood parts of the binding, described a desperate alternative. A living person might temporarily replace the artifact's role in the machinery while the others severed its connection.

**Corvin volunteered first.**

He was already gravely wounded. The others recognized that the procedure would kill him, and he could not be permitted to carry it through. Naeris volunteered instead, believing his existing Blood Touch might allow him to endure the ordeal.

The crew succeeded through a collective effort. Naeris survived with lasting scars, the vessel reached **[[Renada]]**, and Corvin came home alive.

The smuggler who had saved Seb's ship by surrendering its cargo had now twice chosen to risk himself for people who depended on him. This time, those people saved him in return.

## The black coin

Several days after the mission, Corvin was recovering at a tavern near Renada's eastern docks when **[[svendyal-shadowwhisper|Sven Shadowwhisper]]**, the Iron Shadow, came to see him.

Sven had heard from **[[Jack of Stanleyville]]**, the ghost who had witnessed the extraordinary voyage, that Corvin acted without hesitation when Naeris was being taken. The testimony mattered more than Corvin's criminal reputation.

Sven offered a small **black coin marked by a silver line**, an invitation to explore confidential work that might sometimes serve the Lord Marshal, sometimes the Crown, and sometimes operations that could openly bear neither name. He was clear that Corvin was not yet qualified for every such assignment and that the invitation was not a completed appointment.

Corvin pocketed the coin.

He had spent his life aboard temporary crews and amid uncertain alliances. Sven's invitation offered a prospect he had seldom allowed himself to seek: **a place where loyalty might become the foundation of belonging**, rather than simply the price of surviving another day.

No first assignment, rank or formal initiation has yet been established.

## More than a smuggler

Corvin Voss had never needed to be law-abiding to understand the meaning of a promise. Blackwater Bay shaped him into a capable guide, negotiator and survivor; it also taught him to value the people who depended upon one another when formal protection failed.

His choices brought a merchant friend home in 2008 AF and helped an extraordinary company survive in 2020 AF. Neither act erased his involvement in smuggling. Together, they showed that the code by which he lived was stronger than the instinct to save himself at any cost.

Corvin's future with the Iron Shadow remains unwritten. His coin is an open door, not proof of an adventure already undertaken.

What earned him that invitation is no mystery.

**When Naeris was taken, Corvin went back for his crew.**
