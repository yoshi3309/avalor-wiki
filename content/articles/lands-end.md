## At the edge of easy government

**Land's End** is a northern stronghold whose importance lies in both the defense and administration of Avalor's frontier. It is far easier to issue instructions in [[Renada]] than to ensure they can be carried out at a distant crossing when supplies are scarce or the roads are difficult.

The place therefore matters less for the thickness of its walls than for the responsibilities those walls support.

## A founding king's northern fortress

**[[Rynhold Frozenroar]]** built Land's End during the expansion of his northern kingdom, securing the frontier against threats from the peoples and routes beyond it.

In that early age, the country was not yet the [[Great Desert]] familiar to modern travelers. The desert grew gradually in the centuries after the Black Tower catastrophe. Land's End thus guards a landscape that has changed since its walls were first raised.

## The Northern Sheriff

In the present age [[Sebastian Rubelda]], the crown-appointed sheriff of the **Northern March**, holds Land's End. The office is delegated by the sovereign, not inherited as an independent northern kingship. Its demands include frontier security, communications, garrison readiness, and the needs of settlements removed from the immediate attention of the royal court.

A fortified position can protect a route only while its defenders are supplied and informed. Messages must arrive; roads must remain passable; communities must know whom to approach when they require help.

## The people behind the walls

Popular accounts of frontier strongholds favor sieges and heroic defenses. Those who live near Land's End may be more concerned with a repaired bridge, a timely patrol, or provisions reaching a garrison before the onset of bad weather.

Such necessities are not the lesser work of a kingdom. They are the means by which the promise of mutual protection becomes credible in places far from the capital.

The soldiers who defend the frontier belong to a wider military system, one that trains its standing forces in places including the [[Great Fork Tower]]. Each institution serves a different purpose, but both depend upon preparation before danger arrives.

## A frontier's divided expectations

To some neighbors a stronghold represents safety. Others may regard it as a reminder of royal demands and obligations. Both judgments may arise from genuine experience.

Land's End is therefore neither merely the end of a road nor the simple outer edge of a map. It is one of the places where the authority of [[The Kingdom of Avalor|Avalor]] must prove useful to its people.
