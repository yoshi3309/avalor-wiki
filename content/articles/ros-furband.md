## A captain for hire

**Ros Furband** commanded a pirate **galleon** with a crew of more than forty pirates in some accounts. Unlike enemies driven by ancient grudges or supernatural ambition, Ros entered the Misfits' history through a **paid arrangement**. He seized the adventurers **after Cid abducted Queen Melmiri at the Tournament of Champions**, turning their urgent rescue into a voyage of captivity.

Ros delivered his prisoners toward **Jinlin Vechon** and the [[Independent Factions of the Misfits Era#the-wrath-of-innocent|Wrath of Innocent]]. Jinlin's hatred of Cid did not make him a friend to the Misfits, and Ros's willingness to transport captives did not demonstrate that he shared the Wrath's ideology. The captain's established motive was contractual: he was prepared to profit from another group's conflict.

## The price of a voyage

The companions were disarmed and restrained with collars that suppressed magic. Ros did not need to defeat them repeatedly to exercise power over them. **Lavitz and Heureux worked at the oars; Tel'Lora and Anna served in the galley; Sven and [[Beldir Willowrun]] kept lookout.** The presence of Beldir, an experienced admiral, is established, though the circumstances that placed him among the captives remain unexplained.

Ros's treatment of the company reduced celebrated adventurers to laborers aboard his ship. Their later victories cannot undo the indignity of that journey, nor the time lost while Melmiri remained in Cid's hands. When the prisoners reached Jinlin, they fought and **defeated him in reality**, before Zauks imposed his false adventures.

The exact fee Ros received and what became of him afterward have not been established. His role needs no invented fleet, secret kingdom, or grand ideological mission. He was a pirate captain who accepted dangerous work for payment—and whose business became an obstacle between a captive queen and the people trying to save her.
