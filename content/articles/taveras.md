## The city that sings

Along a bend of the **River Rhys**, beneath the foothills, **Taveras** has become Avalor's great gathering place for artists, performers, and storytellers.

Music spills into the streets; singers arrive hoping to be discovered; and a successful performance may be remembered long after a merchant's fortune has changed hands. The city's reputation is built upon people willing to risk embarrassment for the chance to be heard.

Its most celebrated stage is the **Great Opera House**, which draws crowds eager to see established artists and aspiring new stars alike.

## Guild and academy

Taveras is home to **[[The Bard Guild]]**, whose members carry songs and stories across the kingdom. In the years after Cid's defeat, **[[Heureux]]** founded **[[Heureux’s Bard Academy]]** here, giving young bards a place to develop the craft that had carried his own adventures into history.

The guild and the academy share a city and a love of performance, but they remain distinct institutions. One should not be mistaken for the other simply because artists may move between them.

## A growing rival to the great ports

Taveras's riverfront is not devoted only to culture. Trade, travel, and growing commerce have made it one of the kingdom's busier cities.

Its growth has inspired talk that it may one day surpass **[[Stannisport]]** in size and significance. Whether that ambition is fulfilled matters less to Taveras's residents than the opportunity already before them: to make a prosperous home where commerce and the arts flourish together.
