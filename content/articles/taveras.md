## The city that sings

Along a bend of the **River Rhys**, beneath the foothills, **Taveras** has become Avalor's great gathering place for artists, performers, and storytellers.

Music spills into the streets; singers arrive hoping to be discovered; and a successful performance may be remembered long after a merchant's fortune has changed hands. The city's reputation is built upon people willing to risk embarrassment for the chance to be heard.

Its most celebrated stage is the **Great Opera House**, which draws crowds eager to see established artists and aspiring new stars alike.

## Guild and academy

Taveras is home to **[[The Bard Guild|the Society of Silk & Story]]**, a professional fellowship whose musicians, playwrights, performers, and storytellers carry their craft across the kingdom. In the years after Cid's defeat, **[[Heureux]]** founded **[[Heureux’s Bard Academy]]** here, giving new generations a place to study the arts that had shaped his travels.

The Society gathers **working performers**; Heureux's academy is a **school**. Their callings overlap, but the guild does not govern the academy, and an academy student does not automatically join the Society. Among the school's known students is **[[Myxi Twinefoot]]**, the Golden Hand of [[The Aegis]], whose presence shows that study can attract people already committed to other duties.

## A growing rival to the great ports

Taveras's riverfront is not devoted only to culture. Trade, travel, and growing commerce have made it one of the kingdom's busier cities.

Its growth has inspired talk that it may one day surpass **[[Stannisport]]** in size and significance. Yet Taveras follows the **River Rhys**, which also passes through the royal capital of [[Renada]], while Stannisport stands upon the separate **River Juce**. Each city's reputation has grown from its own crossings, patrons, and institutions rather than from a common harbor.

Whether the comparison ever favors Taveras matters less to its residents than the opportunity already before them: to make a prosperous home where commerce and the arts flourish together.
