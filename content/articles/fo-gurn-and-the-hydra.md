## The choice to remain behind

During the early travels of [[The Misfits]], the company was threatened by a **hydra**. [[fo-gurn|Fo’Gurn]], the dragonborn paladin and lifelong friend of [[Lavitz Shawe]], **remained behind to buy the others time to escape**.

The companions escaped. They believed Fo’Gurn had died in the struggle.

The surviving public account does not identify the precise battlefield, the hydra's origins, or a complete account of the fight. Its central fact is the decision Fo’Gurn made and the lives it preserved.

## A loss carried for years

Fo’Gurn's friendship with Lavitz began before the Misfits' adventures, during the years associated with paladin training at [[Heaven's Heart]]. His apparent death was therefore more than the loss of a companion acquired on the road.

Heureux later reflected upon the difference between singing about a noble sacrifice and living as one of the friends left behind. The chronicle preserves the sorrow without needing to decorate the event with invented final words.

## The later inquiry

Subsequent investigations connected Fo’Gurn's fate to the necromancer **[[Jaaris]]**. The companions learned that his disappearance had been manipulated in ways they did not then understand.

Those discoveries do not mean the escape was a feint or that their grief was unreal. They had lived through years believing their friend was gone.

Some later accounts of the final confrontation with Cid describe Fo’Gurn's reappearance. The spiritual particulars remain disputed in the public record.

## Memory made into another trial

Zauks later used **the hydra** among the remembered dangers in his illusions. That was a recreation of a past ordeal, not evidence of a second identical sacrifice.

The hydra encounter is remembered because Fo’Gurn put the safety of his friends above his own, while the people he saved were left to carry the cost of that choice.
