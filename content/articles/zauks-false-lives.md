## Familiar danger turned against its survivors

**Zauks**, a **mind flayer**, used the memories of [[The Misfits]] as material for a series of magical trials. They encountered imitations of dangers already faced during their adventures: [[The Misfits#moonville-manor|Moonville Manor]], the necromancer [[Jaaris]], [[The Stalker of Stanleyville|the Stalker]], [[The Cult of Gol’Goroth|the cult of Gol’Goroth]], [[Noki]], the [[Fo’Gurn#the-hydra-and-the-decision-to-remain|hydra]], [[Independent Factions of the Misfits Era#jinlin-vechon|Jinlin Vechon]]'s lair, and other scenes associated with the companions' losses.

These were **not fresh occurrences of the original incidents**. They were manufactured ordeals shaped from memories.

## The more difficult snare

Zauks ultimately offered the adventurers convincing visions of **ideal lives**, free of the losses and grief they had endured.

Heureux remembered a recurring **owl associated with [[Amara]]** as one clue that the apparent happiness was not what it seemed. The visions offered to the other companions survive only in fragments of their testimony.

The trial was harder than a simple confrontation with a visible monster. The company had to relinquish lives in which painful absences seemed to have been repaired.

## The choice to return

The Misfits escaped by returning to a world in which grief remained real and the people they loved were still vulnerable.

That decision was not a rejection of happiness. It was a refusal to abandon reality for a comforting deception that removed the duties and relationships that gave their lives meaning.

## A history limited by testimony

The Royal Archives preserve the shared character of the ordeal, the known examples of remembered dangers, and the fact of the companions' escape.

No witness has authorized historians to invent every private hope or loss represented in the visions. The value of the episode lies in what the five faced together, even where individual experiences remain undisclosed.
