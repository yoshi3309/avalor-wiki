## The heights beneath the lion

**Crownrise** occupies the elevated approaches to [[The Great Citadel]] within [[Renada]]. Royal offices, prominent residences, and ceremonial streets give this district a character different from the working river quarter below.

The distinction is visible in stonework and processions, but it is also social. The households whose business regularly brings them before the crown experience the capital differently from inhabitants who must leave the riverfront before dawn to seek a day's wages.

## The road to the citadel

Crownrise does not encompass the whole of the city. Its streets descend toward [[Old Renada]] and the approaches to the **Great Stone Bridge**, linking the political center to the neighborhoods and commerce that sustain it.

From the high ground, the city can appear almost orderly: roads carrying people to the court, the River Rhys marking its route through the capital, and markets serving the movement of goods. At street level, these same routes are crowded with carts, petitioners, workers, and those whose errands bear little relation to the sovereign.

## A view of unequal prosperity

The heights have provided more comfortable lives to many who reside and work near the court. Elsewhere in Renada, a household may depend upon the arrival of the next boat or the condition of a single worker's health.

The contrast between Crownrise and the [[Rhys Quays]] is therefore more than a difference in altitude. It helps explain why the capital's prosperity is praised by some inhabitants and regarded more cautiously by others.

## Ceremony and obligation

The Great Citadel is a symbol of the realm, but royal decisions must eventually become repairs, provisions, lawful judgments, and protection for citizens far from the ceremonial heights.

Crownrise deserves remembrance as an important district. It should not be mistaken for the entirety of Renada.
