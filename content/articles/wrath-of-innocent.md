## A name with a dangerous promise

**The Wrath of Innocent** is a named faction in the history of the [[The Misfits|Misfits' travels]]. Its founding is attributed to a dragonborn vampire, **Jinlin Vechon**, whose opposition to [[Cid the Blood King]] was genuine.

The organization's name evokes the punishment of wrongdoing, but a name is not a charter. The public historical record does not establish its complete doctrine, membership, base of operations, internal offices, or present status. The Archives preserve the known founder and the encounter without inventing a larger organization in the gaps.

## Jinlin Vechon

**Jinlin Vechon**, a **dragonborn vampire**, founded the Wrath of Innocent. He was no ally of Cid; his hatred of the Blood King was not a pretense. Yet the Misfits found that a shared enemy did not make Jinlin trustworthy.

The record does not establish Jinlin's original transformation into a vampire, his reasons for establishing the faction, the identities of other members, or the details of his eventual fate. His character cannot be established merely from the faction's name or from his hostility toward a real villain.

Jinlin is important as a named historical actor and as founder of this group. He does not require a second article repeating the organization's known history.

## The Misfits' encounter

After meeting [[Jaznaar]], the Misfits encountered Jinlin and entered an affair remembered for its distinction between **vengeance and justice**. Jinlin wished harm upon Cid, but that did not prove that his proposed acts would protect others or serve a righteous cause.

The full events at Jinlin's lair are not preserved in an authenticated public account. The encounter belongs to [[The Misfits#jinlin-vechon-and-the-wrath-of-innocent|the Misfits' history]], rather than forming an additional event article that simply repeats this group entry.

Later, the mind flayer [[Zauks and the False Lives|Zauks]] recreated **Jinlin's lair** among the magical trials drawn from the company's memories. The imitation was not evidence that Jinlin caused or directed those illusions.

## Where certainty ends

The surviving record identifies a founder, an organization, a genuine enemy, and a dangerous meeting with the Misfits. It does **not** support a roster of followers, a fixed creed, a political territory, or a claim that the group still acts as a unified faction in the present day.

Future public evidence may enrich the Wrath of Innocent's history. Until then, the distinction between its memorable founder and the largely undocumented organization he created remains an important one.
