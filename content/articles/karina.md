**Karina** was a **vampiric centaur** and one of [[Cid the Blood King|Cid's]] most dangerous enforcers. Her violence had reached the Misfits long before they recognized her allegiance: **she murdered the mother of [[Tel'Lora Ebbenflow]]**, an accomplished arcane adviser, while seeking the woman's spellbook and **ice-blue crystal**. The crystal later helped guide Tel'Lora toward [[jaznaar|Jaznaar's]] hidden refuge. Karina's crime was therefore both personal and part of the struggle over knowledge Cid wished to possess.

Karina stood among Cid's servants during the affair of the ruined temple near Stanleyville. The **Misfits defeated and killed her** during their original campaign. Her death is associated by later recollection with the period of the [[rotten-husk-liberation|Rotten Husk liberation]], but no confirmed account fixes the precise battle there. For Tel'Lora, Karina's death could not restore his mother; it ended the life of the person responsible for one of his family's deepest losses.
