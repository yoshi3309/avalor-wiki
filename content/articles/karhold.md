## A name on two kinds of map

**Karhold** occupies an eastern peninsula claimed on many Avalorian maps. Its appearance within the kingdom's painted borders should not be mistaken for proof of unquestioned royal control.

The settlement maintains institutions with a longstanding tradition of independence. Officials of [[The Kingdom of Avalor|Avalor]] can point to the crown's claim; Karhold's leaders point to the government they actually exercise. The disagreement has not been resolved.

## A ruler among competing powers

**Cruzeal Cicluen** presently rules Karhold. Older accounts describe repeated struggles for power among **eastern tribal factions**, and Cruzeal's rule is remembered as hard and uncompromising.

Such changes in rulers have never made Karhold a straightforward province of Avalor. Its own institutions and tradition of independence endure, while the king's territorial claim remains disputed.

## The liberties of the port

Karhold is also a **freeport**, participating in the maritime trade that connects the eastern coast with the wider world. Its commercial arrangements reflect local custom and negotiated liberties, not an absence of law.

A freeport's privileges must be considered individually. [[Stems Port]] has its own arrangements, while crown-aligned coastal settlements such as [[Cove City]] and [[Tinsel]] occupy different positions within the networks served by the King's Road. To describe them all as possessing identical charters would create an agreement the surviving record does not establish.

Karhold's merchants have practical reasons to preserve trade even when questions of sovereignty remain unsettled.

## The Eastern Reach and its limits

[[Cassian Veyran]], the royal sheriff of the Eastern Reach, oversees crown-aligned ports and coastal interests. Karhold does not become an ordinary subordinate district simply because an Avalorian official regards it as lying within the realm's eastern claims.

Ships continue to sail while diplomats debate jurisdiction. Goods must be assessed, agreements honored, and disputes addressed by authorities whose positions do not always coincide.

## The kingdom's unfinished question

Karhold's merchants conduct their business while the crown and the peninsula's leaders dispute the authority each may claim. Its place on a royal map has not settled the matter, nor has its government yielded the independence it exercises.

In Karhold, **territory claimed**, **authority exercised**, and **allegiance accepted** remain three different questions.
