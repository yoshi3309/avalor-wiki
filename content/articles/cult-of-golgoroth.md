## The Lord Marshal calls again

The early companions who would become [[The Misfits]] first assembled to investigate an unnamed necromancer. Following that success, Avalor's preceding Lord Marshal called upon them once more, this time to address **a cult devoted to Gol’Goroth**.

The record confirms the cult's presence in their travels, but does not identify a headquarters, named cult leader, complete doctrine, or an authorized list of ceremonies. Nor does it establish Gol’Goroth's precise nature. These matters remain open in the public history.

## The arrival of Heureux

The second commission gained particular importance because it brought [[Heureux]], a firbolg bard descended from [[Kelsalor]], into the company.

The fellowship was not created all at once by prophecy or a single ceremonial oath. It grew through several commissions, experiences of danger, disagreements, and repeated choices to remain together. Heureux's arrival helped complete the band that became known as the Misfits.

## One threat among several

It is tempting to link every early enemy to Cid the Blood King, whose later defeat changed Avalor's history. The Living Chronicle expressly warns against such a simplified account.

The fact that the Misfits investigated Gol’Goroth's cult does **not** by itself establish that Cid founded or controlled it.

Years later, Zauks created illusions drawn from the companions' memories, including their experiences involving Gol’Goroth. These were imitations of an earlier ordeal, not another verified encounter with the original cult.

The cult's historical significance is secure even where its theology and eventual disposition remain uncertain: its investigation was the Misfits' second summons and the event through which Heureux became one of their companions.
