## A tower from the age before Avalor

**Lord Osmo’s Tower** stands within the [[Dead Marshes]], a surviving landmark of the wars that preceded the founding of [[The Kingdom of Avalor|Avalor]]. The stronghold rises from wetland country of dark waters, difficult approaches, and shifting ground. It recalls the reach of the conqueror whose ambitions helped persuade rival northern rulers to make common cause.

Osmo was a **mortal man**, not a wizard or supernatural tyrant. His power lay in armies, politics, and the willingness to compel other rulers to submit to his growing dominion.

## The work of conquest

Victories are insufficient to hold territory. Garrisons require provisions; messages must cross long roads; officers must defend the gains an army has made. Osmo's Tower is associated with this martial and administrative system, whose ordinary mechanisms made his campaigns dangerous to independent neighbors.

The surviving records do not fix every detail of the tower's construction or the use of each chamber. They preserve its significance as part of a wider struggle rather than a complete architectural inventory.

## The defeat at Heroes Hill

Rynhold Frozenroar, then king of Kroubia, assembled a coalition against Osmo. At [[Heroes Hill]], **Lord Bradgem Glav** reached the conqueror and killed him, suffering mortal wounds in the act.

The victory made a new political order possible. It did not, by itself, unite the kingdoms: Rynhold still had to persuade his allies to accept common institutions and obligations. The tower therefore recalls both the danger ended by the founding wars and the difficult peace that followed them.

## Legends and evidence

Travelers repeat stories of enchantments and hidden chambers. Those tales form part of the tower's reputation; the Royal Commission cannot treat them as authenticated events.

The tower's most enduring shadow is the one Osmo cast over his neighbors. It was his armies and his ambitions that drove the northern kingdoms to war, and many lives were spent bringing his campaigns to an end.
