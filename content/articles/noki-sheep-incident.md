## A mage made into a sheep

There are dangers in the Misfits' early adventures that inspire solemn histories, and others that have been preserved largely because their absurdity proved impossible to forget. The confrontation with **[[Noki]]**, a disgraced mage, belongs uneasily to both kinds.

The companions did not meet a hulking, disfigured creature. **Noki was not disfigured when they fought him.** During the encounter, the Misfits turned the mage into a sheep. The transformation became one of the most memorable episodes in the Misfits' early adventures.

The event has been performed and retold for the strange image of a wizard in animal form. The humor has sometimes obscured the serious consequences for the person at its center.

## What happened at the end

**Noki's disfigurement occurred at the end of this encounter**, not beforehand. The companions believed he had died. The later record establishes that this judgment was mistaken: Noki survived, and **only after the disfigurement** did he become known as **Noke**.

The cause and full circumstances of the alteration have not been sufficiently explained to the Royal Commission. A dramatic story can be built by inventing a cursed weapon, a secret bargain, or a single decisive spell. The public evidence authorizes none of those as an established explanation.

The timing is nevertheless certain enough to prevent a persistent confusion: the Misfits fought Noki the mage, **before** they had reason to imagine the monstrous figure that the world would later recognize.

## The life after the sheep

The later Noke is a scarred, formidable being whose altered appearance belies a sharp intelligence and powerful magical ability. He now serves the figure called **the Den Mother**, already known in the present campaign's public history, and fights with both spells and a massive wooden club apparently associated with a dragon. The exact origin of the weapon remains unconfirmed.

That later life is described in [[Noki|Noki's biography]]. It belongs to an era after the Misfits believed the affair had ended. Knowledge of what he became must not be retroactively placed in the heads of companions who did not know he had survived.

## A tale retold through false memory

During the later ordeal imposed by [[Zauks and the False Lives|Zauks]], a counterfeit sheep incident appeared among the adventurers' remembered dangers. The imitation was one more way the mind flayer could make the familiar unreliable. It did not turn Noki's original confrontation into a dream.

The sheep incident therefore survives in two very different forms: as a genuine early encounter that had consequences beyond the party's knowledge, and as a false echo deliberately constructed by a later enemy. Both belong to the story, but never at the same point in its chronology.
