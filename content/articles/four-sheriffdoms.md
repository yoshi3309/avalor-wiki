## Four regions under one sovereign

Avalor's monarchy appoints **four sheriffs** to govern major regional divisions. Each exercises delegated authority on behalf of the crown, but none is a hereditary prince or the independent ruler of a separate kingdom.

The system reflects the difficulty of administering a realm with different landscapes, commercial needs, and inherited rights. A royal order issued in Renada may meet very different practical conditions along a frontier road, in grain country, beside ancient woodland, or at a sea-facing port.

## Northern March — Sebastian Rubelda

The **Northern March** is held by [[Sebastian Rubelda]], who governs from [[Land's End]]. His responsibilities concern frontier defenses, difficult routes, communication with distant settlements, and provisions for regional garrisons.

The need to sustain protection at great distance from the capital gives this office its distinct demands.

## Royal Heartlands — Edran Halwick

The **Royal Heartlands** are administered by [[Edran Halwick]], a member of a noble family whose interests are rooted in agricultural estates. **She resides in [[Tarnapolus]]**, the lake port on Lake Noch, and conducts her regional responsibilities from there.

The region's grain production and roads are fundamental to the prosperity of [[Renada]]. Administration here requires attention to tenant farmers, landholders, merchants, and the needs of a capital that depends upon their labor. Her residence in a southern trade gateway does not create a fifth sheriffdom or reduce her responsibilities to the lakefront.

## Western Verge — Seren Thornwyck

The **Western Verge** is held by [[Seren Thornwyck]], whose family has longstanding connections to woodland diplomacy.

The district includes communities and routes associated with the [[Forest of the Fey]]. The **Elven Concord** protects ancestral rights that a sheriff's enthusiasm for roads or revenue cannot simply set aside. The forest's communities do not all possess identical traditions or attitudes toward royal authority.

## Eastern Reach — Cassian Veyran

The **Eastern Reach** is administered by [[Cassian Veyran]], whose family is associated with maritime trade. Its crown-aligned coastal interests include varied ports and commercial networks.

The disputed freeport of [[Karhold]] is a crucial exception: Avalor's territorial claim does **not** establish that Karhold accepts the sheriff's ordinary jurisdiction. Trade relationships and settled political allegiance are different matters.

## What appointment does and does not grant

Sheriffs possess genuine regional discretion. Nevertheless, their authority is delegated by the monarch, and existing municipalities, guilds, military officers, and local traditions remain important in how government operates.

The names and present holders of the four sheriffdoms are recorded. Their complete boundary surveys, subordinate offices, and administrative divisions are less clearly preserved in the Commission's holdings.

The four sheriffdoms are therefore a way of making the crown's responsibilities local, not a claim that every community's history began with royal administration.
