## The older watch

**The Great Fork Tower** is an ancient stronghold associated with the **First Men** and remembered for its defensive role. Long before the Kingdom of Avalor existed, it stood as a place of protection amid the dangers of the northern lands.

## Ancient stone, present service

Many fortifications built in forgotten conflicts survive only as ruins. The Great Fork Tower still serves a practical purpose. In the present age it is a **major training base for Avalor's standing army**, where soldiers learn the disciplines necessary for dependable organized service.

The structure's defensive inheritance thus continues through the people who train within it, though the kingdom that employs them did not yet exist when the tower first gained its reputation.

## A kingdom defended before battle

Avalor's armed forces include professional soldiers, regional formations, garrisons, and local militias. They must be prepared for different terrain and responsibilities. A frontier post such as [[Land's End]] cannot rely solely on heroism, any more than a coastal garrison can neglect the work of supplying its defenders.

[[Lavitz Shawe]], as Lord Marshal, advises the crown on military affairs and commands the Lion's Bane royal guard. His extraordinary reputation does not remove the need for ordinary soldiers to receive good training, capable leadership, and reliable equipment.

## A purpose that outlived its makers

The Great Fork Tower illustrates a rare continuity: the walls that once sheltered an earlier people remain useful in the protection of a later kingdom.

Its history is not a tale of one unbroken army or one unchanging political order. It is the story of an enduring duty carried out by generations who inherited more of the building than they knew of those who first raised it.
