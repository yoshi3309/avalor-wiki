## Commerce under a different arrangement

**Stems Port** is recognized as a **freeport** within the coastal trade network of [[The Kingdom of Avalor|Avalor]]. This distinction concerns its commercial privileges and arrangements of local authority; it does not mean that the settlement is a place without law.

The customs of a freeport are established through agreements, regulations, and practices that must be understood on their own terms. A merchant familiar with the rules of one harbor should not assume those of another to be identical.

## The uses of a freeport

Maritime commerce joins settlements whose rulers and inhabitants may differ greatly in interests and traditions. Privileges negotiated at a port can make trade possible in circumstances that would otherwise burden merchants with unnecessary disputes or costs.

Stems Port's status makes it distinctive among Avalor's coastal settlements, but the available public record does not specify the contents of its charter, the exact extent of local self-government, or an exclusive trade in any named commodity.

Such matters cannot responsibly be filled with the customary details of another freeport.

## A distinction from Karhold

[[Karhold]] is also described as a freeport, but occupies a peninsula whose political relationship with the crown remains disputed. Its independence of government and Avalor's territorial claim form a separate issue from the trade liberties granted or observed there.

Stems Port is not thereby proven to share Karhold's constitutional dispute. Both participate in commerce; their political histories are not automatically the same.

## The ports of a varied kingdom

The wider coast also includes [[Cove City]], [[Tinsel]], and **Siesta Ford**, each remembered through a different aspect of coastal life. Taken together, these communities demonstrate the limits of a history written entirely from the capital.

Stems Port deserves a place in that account precisely because royal policy, maritime custom, and local interests meet there.
