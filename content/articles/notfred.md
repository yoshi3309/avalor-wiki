## The thing in the caves

The creature called **Notfred** was not always a Nothic. He had once borne the name **Toz Reka**, though it had gone unspoken for decades. A disastrous pact with a **Great Old One** broke his body and his mind, leaving him transformed into a one-eyed being whose hunger and strange powers made ordinary companionship difficult.

He fled into caves outside **Phandalin**, far from the streets where people might see what he had become. There he collected stolen trinkets, fragments of magic, and anything bright enough to catch his attention. His refuge was a hoard, but also a shelter from people who were unlikely to welcome him.

Eventually an adventuring party entered his lair. They were loud, violent, and eager for treasure. They tore through what defenses he had gathered and very nearly killed him. Notfred remembered them as bullies and fools, far more interested in glory than in learning what lived beneath the earth.

One member of the party changed what happened next.

## The bard who came back

**Kilmos Handbar**, a bard traveling with the adventurers, called off the attack. Later he returned alone, carrying food rather than a drawn weapon, and apologized sincerely for what his companions had done.

The gesture was extraordinary to a creature who had every reason to expect fear and violence. Notfred had been spared in battle, but the apology offered something more difficult to understand: the possibility that a stranger might care whether he lived.

The two became friends. Their friendship was unusual, sometimes unsettling, and stronger than the expectations of those who might have met the monstrous figure in the caves.

When Kilmos became **Sheriff of Phandalin**, he made Notfred his **deputy**.

## A deputy in disguise

Kilmos taught his friend **Disguise Self** and **Alter Self**, allowing the Nothic to move among the townsfolk in humanoid form. Few understood who was helping the sheriff keep order.

Crime declined sharply. Notfred was effective, resourceful, and frighteningly willing to make criminals disappear. At least some of those unfortunate enough to meet him ended up eaten.

This was the danger at the center of Kilmos's unlikely success. He had seen a person where others saw only a monster, but Notfred's hunger and instinct for violence had not disappeared merely because someone trusted him. Both friends lived within that contradiction.

For a time, they nevertheless managed to protect the town together.

## The death of Susan

The fragile life they had built ended when **Susan**, the woman Kilmos loved, surprised Notfred while he was feeding in the darkness.

Startled and panicked, Notfred lashed out. He killed her by accident.

Horrified by what he had done, he carried Susan's bloodied body into town, desperate to find Kilmos and explain. He did not understand what the townspeople would see: a monstrous, one-eyed creature bringing the corpse of the sheriff's beloved through their streets.

The town erupted in fear. People raised weapons and torches, and the secret of the deputy could no longer be kept.

Kilmos told the people of Phandalin the truth: the creature they feared had been serving as his deputy. The admission did not save his friend. The townspeople demanded Notfred's death and drove Kilmos from the community he had governed.

Susan remained dead. Whatever affection and public service had passed between the sheriff and the Nothic could not undo that fact.

## Just not Fred

That night Kilmos took his friend to a meadow beyond the town. There he raised his hand as if to strike the killing blow demanded by the people he had served.

He could not do it.

Instead he told the Nothic to run, to hide what he really was, and to take another name if anyone asked. His last warning was painfully simple: whatever name he chose, it should be **“just… not Fred.”**

The Nothic took the words literally.

From then onward, he would call himself **Notfred**.

The name was born from an attempt to spare his life, and from the loss of the one person who had believed he might belong somewhere. Kilmos let him go. Notfred ran.

## The hope of being better

Notfred wandered afterward among lawless ports and dark stretches of coastline, surviving as a hooded mercenary and occasional smuggler. He could disguise himself when circumstances required it, but no change of face could recreate the ordinary trust he had found beside Kilmos.

He missed his friend and hated being alone. Hunger remained a part of him, never entirely quiet. He sometimes pretended to want little more than his next meal, but the years with the sheriff had left him with another desire: to become the kind of person his friend once believed he could be.

That hope did not erase Susan's death or excuse the brutal way he had once helped enforce the law. It meant only that the story did not end with the worst thing he had done.

In time he became one of the unlikely operatives recruited by **[[Lavitz Shawe]]** for the undertaking connected to **Blackwater Bay**. A Nothic who had once been a deputy, then an exile, was again being asked to stand alongside others.

Whether he could truly become a hero remained a question Notfred would have to answer through his choices.
