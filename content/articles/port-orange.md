## When Fort Orange guarded the coast

Before it was a trading city, **Port Orange** was **Fort Orange**, a defensive position raised during the war against **Lord Osmo**, the mortal conqueror whose campaigns helped unite the northern kingdoms.

The fort served a wartime need. With Osmo defeated and the former crowns drawn together under [[Rynhold Frozenroar]], the coast no longer required precisely the same kind of stronghold.

Where soldiers had once watched for enemies, merchants began arriving with cargoes and offers of trade.

## A harbor remade by peace

Fort Orange became **Port Orange**, a substantial commercial harbor where ships from across the great ocean exchanged goods with the kingdom.

With those ships came people. Sailors, merchants, artisans, and families of ancestries less commonly encountered in inland Avalor made homes and livelihoods here. Over generations their different customs became part of the city's ordinary life rather than passing curiosities.

Port Orange is one of the clearest examples of a place reshaped by the end of a war. The fort's purpose was to keep danger away; the city's prosperity grew from welcoming people and goods through the same approaches.
