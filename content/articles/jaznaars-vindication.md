## The accusation overturned

The Misfits' confrontation with **Cid the Blood King** brought to light a truth that transformed the understanding of Avalor’s founding. Aldus Greystone III, long celebrated as a healer and defender, had been responsible for the Black Tower experiments; [[Jaznaar]], condemned as the Betrayer, had spent centuries trying to contain their consequences.

The discoveries were considered through testimony and historical inquiry, not merely accepted as a new heroic song to replace an old one.

## A proclamation by the crown

**[[King Gradvum Frozenroar]] formally proclaimed Jaznaar innocent**, restoring his name to the royal record among those who had defended the realm at its beginning.

The announcement required revisions to public histories, memorials, and teaching. The old accusation could no longer stand as the kingdom's official account.

The law of memory, however, is less certain than the law of a crown. Some people welcomed the truth. Others continued to trust familiar traditions, even after the evidence that underpinned them had been overturned.

## The consequences no proclamation could reverse

For centuries the [[betrayers-legend|Betrayer legend]] had encouraged suspicion of drow communities. Under [[arnauds-edicts|Arnaud]], that suspicion became the foundation of discriminatory decrees.

[[reinas-reforms|Reina]] had already curtailed many such restrictions centuries before Gradvum's proclamation. **The correction of history was not the first restoration of legal equality.** Nor did it repay lost livelihoods or reverse harms endured under the earlier laws.

The [[royal-commission-historical-inquiry|Royal Commission]] distinguishes between restoring an individual’s name and addressing what institutions did while repeating the lie.

## A monument retained, an inscription corrected

At [[Rynhold Park]] in Renada, the statue of Aldus remained but its old inscription was removed. The original plaque was preserved at [[The Great Library of Aldus Greystone]], while a new inscription identified Aldus with Cid and acknowledged Jaznaar's innocence.

The decision remains contested: preserving evidence of an error is not the same thing as honoring the error, yet many citizens disagree over how a monument should communicate that difference.

## A homecoming too late

According to the companions' testimony, Jaznaar was laid to rest beside the friends of his founding generation in Renada. **He died before Gradvum proclaimed him innocent.**

Avalor could correct the record in one reign. It could not return the centuries of vindication denied him or the years of safety lost by others under the false story.
