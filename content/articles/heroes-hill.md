## The last battle against Osmo

**Heroes Hill** is remembered as the battlefield where the coalition of northern kingdoms won its decisive victory against the mortal conqueror **Lord Osmo**. Accounts disagree over the exact maneuvers of the armies, but preserve a central deed: [[Lord Bradgem Glav]] reached Osmo, killed him, and suffered mortal wounds in the act.

Glav's grave bears a monument of a **rearing horse**. Later generations have offered different readings of its meaning, from the courage of a final charge to the grief of a rider who never returned. No surviving inscription settles the question.

## The peace that followed

Osmo's defeat made the founding of [[The Kingdom of Avalor|Avalor]] possible; it did not create unity by itself. Rynhold Frozenroar still had to persuade former allies to share political obligations and a future beneath one crown. The new calendar begins with the kingdom's founding in **1 AF**, not necessarily with the precise date of the battle.

Heroes Hill thus remembers more than military triumph. It marks the sacrifices that made a new kingdom possible—and the responsibility inherited by those who survived.

## The conqueror's surviving tower

[[Lord Osmo’s Tower]] remains a separate landmark of the wars that preceded the kingdom's founding. Its reputation preserves the fear of Osmo's campaigns, though the conqueror himself was a mortal ruler, not a master of supernatural forces.
