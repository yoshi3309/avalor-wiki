## A guard with its own inheritance

**Dina Hyanda** is a **women's royal protective order** established by [[Queen Melmiri]]. Its founding reflects a separate tradition of service to the royal household, rather than an additional name for Avalor's existing guard.

In the capital, different institutions of protection can appear in the same ceremonies or among the same royal buildings. Their presence in those spaces does not make their history or command identical.

## Melmiri's foundation

The queen holds substantial responsibility for the kingdom's ordinary administration. Her foundation of Dina Hyanda is another visible part of her public legacy, though the order's full internal arrangements have not been established in the Royal Archives.

Her ancestry among the **Northern Desert Tribes** is a separate historical fact. It would be unwarranted to claim that the entire order follows the customs of those communities, or that the queen's heritage alone determines its membership and practices.

## Distinct from Lion's Bane

[[Lion's Bane]] serves under the command of **Lord Marshal Lavitz Shawe**. Dina Hyanda arose through Melmiri and possesses its own institutional identity.

Neither is the same as [[The Avalorian Standing Army]], whose duties extend across the kingdom, including regional garrisons and military forces.

The public record does not provide a verified chart of Dina Hyanda's command, its numbers, its selection trials, or its exact division of daily royal security responsibilities. Those details remain open rather than being filled with borrowed traditions from another order.

## Service in the present reign

The image of a royal guard can invite stories of ceremony and splendid equipment. The underlying obligation is more demanding: to protect others reliably, including when the work passes unnoticed.

Dina Hyanda's place within the kingdom is secure as an independent women's royal protective order, one of the institutions shaped by Melmiri's present reign.
