## A district measured in trade

**Lantern Ward** is the chief mercantile quarter of [[Renada]]. Its familiar **Lantern Market** gathers food, fabrics, tools, and goods carried from foreign ports alongside the produce and manufacture of Avalorian communities.

To know the ward is to understand something of the capital's appetite. Grain and flour arrive from the Royal Heartlands; worked steel bears the reputation of [[Stanleyville]]; timber, resin, cloth, dyes, and spices arrive by routes that extend well beyond the city.

## The Hall of Measures

Among the market's institutions, the **Hall of Measures** performs a quieter duty than the vendors of Lantern Market. Merchants maintain recognized weights, consider the quality and delivery of disputed goods, and preserve customary measures upon which trade depends.

Such work may seem unremarkable until a buyer and seller disagree about the contents of a load or the value of an unfamiliar piece of metalwork. Shared standards make commerce possible without requiring strangers to trust one another solely on reputation.

## The labor behind a price

The coinage of Avalor is known by familiar names: the gold lion, silver shield, and copper torch. In Lantern Ward, these coins pass constantly between people whose circumstances differ sharply.

A Stanleyville-stamped longsword may command a higher price than an ordinary blade because of the reputation of its makers. The premium does not, by itself, prove enchantment. Nor does the sum paid at a counter reveal how much of the profit reached those who mined, forged, carried, and sold the item.

## A market and a city

Guilds preserve skills and quality, yet can also decide who receives training or is permitted to compete. Lantern Ward's prosperity consequently raises questions of access and fairness as well as price.

Its commerce is closely tied to the [[Rhys Quays]], where much of the city's cargo changes hands before reaching the market. Neither district can fully explain Renada's economy without the other.
