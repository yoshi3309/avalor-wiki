## Before the lion banner

Before Avalor there were many kingdoms. Their borders shifted through conflict, inheritance, and agreements, while travelers and merchants crossed them more readily than armies. **Kroubia**, ruled by [[Rynhold Frozenroar]], was one of these realms; it was smaller than the kingdom Avalor would become.

**Lord Osmo** sought dominion over his neighbors through armies and political calculation. He was **a mortal conqueror**, not a wizard or an emissary of a hidden supernatural power. His successes revealed how division weakened his opponents. A ruler might refuse troops for fear of strengthening a rival; another might surrender a crossing in the hope of preserving the rest of his lands.

## The gathering of crowns

Rynhold could not simply order other sovereigns into an alliance. He had to persuade them that limited agreements—shared scouts, defended passes, supplies for garrisons—were necessary to resist Osmo’s expansion. The precise proceedings of these negotiations have not survived. Accounts of a single perfect war council are reconstructions rather than documented speeches.

The struggle included skirmishes, marches, forced departures, and the labor of civilians who supplied the armies. Some traditions remember families harvesting at night while defenders kept watch. No particular village or exact date is authenticated for that story.

## Heroes Hill

The decisive battle was fought at [[Heroes Hill]]. The oldest accounts agree that **[[Bradgem Glav]] reached Osmo, killed him, and suffered mortal wounds**. They disagree about the maneuvers that made his action possible.

The [[Dead Marshes]] already contained perilous country. Founding-war traditions associate the region with further magical injury, but neither its entire present condition nor every later disturbance can be assigned to this battle alone.

## Victory and the founding

The armies’ victory did not immediately unite their crowns. Rynhold still needed to negotiate a shared political order with those who had fought beside him. That achievement is remembered in [[unification-of-avalor|the Unification of Avalor]].

**1 AF marks the creation of the kingdom—not an independently established date for the battle.** The war ended one danger; the union created obligations the survivors would have to honor.
