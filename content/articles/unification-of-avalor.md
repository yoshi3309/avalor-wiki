## An alliance was not yet a kingdom

The northern kingdoms had joined forces to resist Lord Osmo, but victory at Heroes Hill did not automatically place them under one crown. They retained their laws, interests, rulers, and distinct histories. Shared danger had created cooperation; lasting unity would require political agreement.

Rynhold Frozenroar, originally king of **Kroubia**, became the central figure in the negotiations that followed [[founding-wars|the Founding Wars]]. The surviving records describe compromise over authority, obligations, and protection, but do not preserve the full provisions of a single founding charter.

## The settlement of 1 AF

In **1 After Founding (AF)**, several northern realms were united as [[The Kingdom of Avalor|Avalor]] beneath Rynhold’s crown. The calendar begins with that settlement, **not necessarily with the exact year of Osmo’s death**.

The former kingdoms retained customs and local obligations. Founding did not mean that every disagreement disappeared, nor that all territories were simply conquered and annexed. Rynhold’s achievement was persuading former allies to accept institutions capable of preserving their mutual defense and addressing competing interests.

## An existing city becomes a capital

Rynhold selected [[Renada]] as the capital. Renada was already inhabited: the founding did not create the settlement, but profoundly changed its position in the region.

The council later known as [[The Aegis]] traces its origins to Rynhold. Its endurance reflects the need for institutions that could function after the first king's death rather than depending on his personal authority alone.

## What a kingdom inherits

Later royal histories often present Rynhold as the father of Avalor. That title does not make him the origin of every people who now live within its borders. Their histories preceded his reign and continued after the union.

The political settlement of 1 AF was thus **a beginning rather than an ending**: an agreement among peoples whose differences could no longer be managed solely by rivalry or force.
