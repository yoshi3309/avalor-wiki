## Briyan's city

**Briyan's Autumn** owes its name to **Briyan**, a wealthy halfling who supplied the fortune needed for the city's construction. What rose from that investment became more than a settlement bearing a benefactor's name: it grew into a heavily defended city near the **[[The Geography of Avalor#forest-of-the-westfold|Forest of the Westfold]]**.

Its position toward the eastern limits of Avalor's crown-governed lands has given it an importance beyond its walls. A merchant approaching from the interior sees a place built to endure; those responsible for protecting the Eastern Reach see a city whose safety cannot be left to chance.

## A city under the Eastern Sheriff

Briyan's Autumn falls under the administration of **[[Cassian Veyran]]**, the **Eastern Sheriff**. His office connects the city's local life with the crown's wider responsibilities, though he does not personally govern every market stall or watchtower.

Briyan's Autumn is sometimes called the kingdom's easternmost city. That description is best understood of **the crown-governed cities**: the farther eastern settlement of [[Karhold]] maintains a disputed relationship with Avalorian sovereignty, and cannot simply be counted as another royal city.

The great walls remain part of Briyan's legacy, while the daily work of the people within them gives the place its life.
