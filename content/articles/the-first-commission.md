## Before a fellowship had a name

The companions later called [[The Misfits]] had **already faced Moonville Manor** before the preceding Lord Marshal of Avalor summoned them to investigate an **unnamed necromancer**. This was their first *royal commission*, not the first adventure shared by the company.

Among the early companions were [[Lavitz Shawe]], [[Tel'Lora Ebbenflow]], [[svendyal-shadowwhisper|Sven]], [[Amara]], and [[fo-gurn|Fo’Gurn]]. They had no reason at the outset to expect that their work would lead them to the ancient history of the kingdom.

They defeated the necromancer. Among the materials recovered were fragments of magical study later linked to the forbidden research of Aldus Greystone III.

## What the recovered fragments did not prove

The necromancer was **not Jaaris**, whose later crimes became connected to Fo’Gurn. The presence of related magical materials also did not prove that Cid personally directed the earlier practitioner.

A person may use dangerous knowledge while misunderstanding its origin. Later histories sometimes compress several enemies into a single grand design; the surviving evidence does not justify that simplification.

## The second commission

The Lord Marshal called upon the adventurers again to investigate a cult devoted to **Gol’Goroth**. During that subsequent work, they gained the companionship of [[Heureux]], the firbolg bard who helped complete the group as it would become publicly known.

Their loyalty grew through decisions to stand together beyond the end of any one task.

## A road of unrelated wonders

Their later adventures included [[Noki]], whose confrontation with the Misfits ended in a sheep transformation and disfigurement—after which the surviving mage took the name **Noke**. They also liberated the [[The Rotten Husk Clan|Rotten Husk]] orc tribe from an oppressive chieftain. Moonville belongs to the period *before* the first commission.

Not every such story led to Cid. For those affected by each adventure, however, the outcome could matter more immediately than the mystery that would one day bring the Misfits renown.

During a subsequent search for knowledge concerning Lowkee's Keeper, they met **[[Anna, Lowkee's Keeper|Anna]]**, a waitress who joined them as a noncombatant companion. Her place in the history of the Misfits is not part of their first commission; it shows how the company grew long after their first royal assignment had ended.

The first commission therefore remains worth recording as a beginning: a service undertaken for a specific danger that became the foundation of an enduring friendship.
