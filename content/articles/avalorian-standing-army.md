## The soldiers who remain when the heroes depart

Avalor maintains a **professional standing army**, providing defense and readiness beyond what can be achieved through temporary levies. Its existence recalls the kingdom's founding: previously divided northern rulers had to combine their strength to defeat the conqueror Lord Osmo.

That cooperation became more than a wartime expedient. The enduring kingdom requires soldiers able to protect communities, borders, and vital routes before a crisis has become a famous battle.

## Training and the Great Fork Tower

[[The Great Fork Tower]], an ancient defensive landmark associated with the First Men, now serves as a major training base for Avalor's forces.

Training is not only a matter of weapons. Units depend upon preparation, reliable supplies, commanders capable of exercising judgment, and the ability to work with formations raised elsewhere.

A soldier's readiness is easy to overlook until the day it is needed.

## A kingdom with several kinds of force

The standing army is supplemented by **regional military formations, garrisons, and local militias**. Frontier defenses, coastal watches, and inland patrols do not face identical demands.

Local forces may know terrain unfamiliar to newly arrived royal troops. The kingdom benefits when those different strengths can be coordinated without assuming every formation must be organized in precisely the same way.

The exact strength, equipment lists, and detailed regimental structure of the army remain unspecified in the established public record.

## The crown and the Lord Marshal

The sovereign exercises **ultimate political authority** over Avalor's armed forces. The [[The Four Sheriffdoms|regional sheriffdoms]] coordinate important aspects of local administration, while military commanders retain their own operational responsibilities.

[[Lavitz Shawe]] is the **Lord Marshal** and the crown's principal military adviser. He commands [[Lion's Bane]], the royal guard. This does **not** mean he automatically possesses direct operational command of every regional garrison or formation.

Queen Melmiri's [[Dina Hyanda]] is another distinct royal protective order, not a subdivision of the army merely because its members serve the crown.

## The less visible work of defense

The best outcomes of military readiness are often events that do not happen: supplies that arrive before winter, a post still guarded when an alarm sounds, a commander who questions an unnecessary risk.

Avalor's lasting security cannot depend solely upon the renown of its champions. It depends upon the disciplined service of people whose names may never be recorded in a chronicle.
