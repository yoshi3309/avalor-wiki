## Three lights, one set of obligations

The **Threefold Devotion** honors **Tyr**, **Helm**, and **Torm**, the gods whose connected traditions have a prominent place in the religious life of [[The Kingdom of Avalor|Avalor]].

Their worship is influential in [[The Great Cathedral]] of [[Cathedral Ward]] and among [[The Cathedral Paladins]] at [[Heaven's Heart]]. It is not, however, a declaration that every Avalorian must follow those gods or that the kingdom has one universal religion.

The people of Avalor inherit many faiths. The prominence of the Threefold Devotion is best understood through its teachings and institutions, not by assuming uniform belief.

## Justice, vigilance, and duty

**Tyr** is honored as a god of **justice**, whose teaching places weight upon fairness, moral judgment, and the distinction between lawful commands and righteous action.

**Helm** is revered as the **vigilant protector**, whose watchfulness reminds the faithful that danger may arrive before an ordinary community knows to prepare.

**Torm** is associated with **loyalty and duty**, especially the obligation to remain steadfast when honorable service becomes difficult.

The Cathedral's clergy teach that the three responsibilities must inform one another. Justice without vigilance may arrive too late; vigilance without purpose may become suspicion; duty severed from justice can be used to serve power that does not deserve it.

## The Three Lights

In a number of Cathedral congregations, important ceremonies begin with the lighting of **three lamps** for Justice, Vigilance, and Duty. The observance is known as **the Three Lights**.

The form of the ritual varies among communities and is particularly familiar where the Cathedral Paladins are influential. It is therefore a recognized tradition, not a universal ceremony prescribed identically in every temple of the kingdom.

Its simplicity makes the lesson visible. No single flame represents the whole duty of a protector.

## Lowkee's teaching and the right of sanctuary

[[Lowkee]], the founding paladin of the Six, translated these ideals into a teaching about the responsibility of strength. His order was to defend those unable to protect themselves, even when the demands of authority made that responsibility dangerous.

The principle endured at Heaven's Heart. Under the reign of Arnaud Frozenroar, the paladins gave sanctuary to drow families threatened by discriminatory royal decrees. The order's leader **Jeyse** refused to surrender innocents on the grounds of ancestry, with **Cal'el** supporting the refusal.

The dispute demonstrated that loyalty to the kingdom and obedience to every royal demand are not always the same obligation.

## Present institutions, different duties

**Astronoph**, a female deva, leads the Great Cathedral as High Priest. **Samuel**, an aasimar, trains paladins at Heaven's Heart and serves as the Devoted on [[The Aegis]]. Their responsibilities are not interchangeable merely because both serve within related religious traditions.

Other faiths remain active among the people of Avalor, including the worship of Lathander, Corellon, Moradin, and Eilistraee. The Threefold Devotion contributes a significant public language of obligation without exhausting the religious life of the realm.

Its most enduring lesson is not merely that justice, vigilance, and duty matter. It is that none can safely be practiced without the others.
