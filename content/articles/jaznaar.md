## The sixth name

Among the great figures of Avalor's founding age, few have endured so grave an injustice as **Jaznaar**. A drow spellcaster and trusted companion of [[The Six]], he was long remembered under a false name: **the Betrayer**.

The old histories placed upon Jaznaar the blame for the catastrophe at the Black Tower. For generations, this accusation shaped songs, official records, and the treatment of drow citizens. It was repeated so often that its familiarity became a substitute for evidence.

## What the kingdom learned

The truth was different. Aldus Greystone III had pursued the experiments that brought disaster, while Jaznaar worked to contain the danger they left behind. His extraordinary service went unrecognized for centuries.

The revelations brought by [[The Misfits]] after the defeat of [[Cid the Blood King]] overturned the old accusation. The royal record now acknowledges Jaznaar's innocence.

## A vindication with unfinished consequences

The restoration of a name does not undo every law, injury, or prejudice that grew from the lie. The kingdom's attempts to reckon with that legacy remain part of its present history.

Surviving sources disagree about the exact form of Jaznaar's arcane practice; some describe a wizard, others a sorcerer. The Commission therefore preserves the title **spellcaster** without imposing a certainty the evidence does not support.
