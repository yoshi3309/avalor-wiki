## A quiet place in a busy harbor

At **[[Port Orange]]**, the **Burntwood Tavern** is a familiar stop for sailors, traders, travelers, and adventurers who need shelter, conversation, or a decent place to sit after a difficult crossing. Its proprietor, **Ambrose Burntwood**, is a mountain dwarf whose friendliness has never prevented him from noticing exactly who comes through his door.

Ambrose remembers names and faces with uncommon precision. He speaks easily with harbor workers, captains, and merchants, and knows when a visitor needs questions answered and when that visitor would rather not be asked anything at all.

He is often seen carrying a **copper mule mug** to which he is sentimentally attached. The tavern's rooms and stores also contain an extensive collection of blankets, a detail that has become one of the more affectionate oddities in stories told about its owner.

## A life built with Aelianos

Ambrose shares his life with **Aelianos Burntwood**. Together their home is bound up with the ordinary work of a harbor tavern: keeping guests comfortable, dealing with changing company, and making sure the establishment remains a place people wish to return to.

His manner is patient and observant. Friends describe a man who prefers to settle trouble by talking, arranging a compromise, or helping someone take a safer road. He does not seek out quarrels for their own sake.

The instinct extends beyond paying customers. Individuals in difficulty have sometimes found help at the Burntwood Tavern without being pressed for immediate repayment. Ambrose seems constitutionally unable to leave a neighbor's hardship entirely alone.

## The operative who chose to come home

Long before the undertaking at Blackwater Bay, Ambrose worked in the shadow of **Crown intelligence networks**, particularly where a person needed to be extracted from danger or moved safely through hostile territory. He learned to arrange routes, avoid surveillance, relocate threatened people, and use the discretion of sailors and travelers when a direct approach would have caused harm. His preference for talk, negotiation, distraction, and escape over needless bloodshed was not a lack of capability; it was the habit of someone who knew the cost of a poorly planned rescue.

By the time he became known for the Burntwood Tavern, Ambrose had largely made a civilian life with Aelianos in Port Orange. He did not lose his old knowledge, coastal contacts, or talent for removing people from dangerous circumstances. Even his collection of blankets speaks to the everyday comforts he had chosen to value.

## More capable than he appears

People who judge Ambrose by his comfortable domestic life risk underestimating him. He knows coastal routes, keeps friendships across ports and merchant circles, and has a talent for finding practical exits from complicated situations.

He prefers conversation to violence and planning to needless risks. His calm is not uncertainty, and his friendliness should not be mistaken for an inability to act decisively when people are endangered.

Much of what makes him effective is quiet work: remembering a useful name, arranging a passage, distracting an unfriendly observer, or helping someone reach safety before a confrontation becomes unavoidable.

## The call to service

Ambrose had good reason to be content with the life he had made. The Burntwood Tavern gave him a home, a livelihood, a beloved spouse, and the company of people who came and went with the tides.

Yet he was among **five original operatives** recruited by **[[Lavitz Shawe]]** for a discreet undertaking in **[[Blackwater Bay]]** in **2020 AF**. The Lord Marshal's choice suggests that a useful operative need not be the loudest warrior in a room.

The mission demanded more than his contacts. Ambrose's sorcery helped create the winds needed to pull a captured vessel away from its pier, and later helped the company contain a breach when Naeris took water form to fill a hole in the hull and Ambrose froze that water solid. When the crew fought to separate a dangerous artifact from the ship's control, Ambrose also helped stabilize the ritual by managing magical surges. His actions belonged to a collective effort in which many people kept one another alive.


## The voyage that made freedom possible

The complete account of Ambrose's contribution, from creating the wind that freed the ship at Pier Seven to freezing Naeris's temporary hull patch and containing surges in the Anchor ritual, appears in **[[Escape from Blackwater Bay]]**. He returned to [[Port Orange]] as someone who had fulfilled an extraordinarily dangerous assignment. Lavitz's later release made explicit that further service could only be voluntary.

## No debt remains

In the aftermath of the mission, **Lavitz came to the Burntwood Tavern in Port Orange**. Ambrose expected the familiar burden of another demand. Instead the Lord Marshal brought a formal release from every compulsory debt or obligation Ambrose had owed the Marshal's office, including legal and financial consequences if he declined future service.

Ambrose studied the document carefully before accepting that it meant what it said. Lavitz wanted the ability to ask for assistance from someone he respected, not the power to compel an unwilling subject. Ambrose's home, his livelihood, and his life with Aelianos remained his to choose.

The release did not mean Ambrose would never help again. It meant that the next time he did so, the choice would be his.
