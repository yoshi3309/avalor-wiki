## A village built on an older city

**Jogar Village** stands upon the ruins of **Illmire**, remembered as a city of the **First Men**. The stones beneath the modern settlement belong to an older chapter of Avalor's history, but life above them is unmistakably present: homes, work, and the changing friendships of a growing community.

Jogar began as a home to **halflings and humans living together**. The later arrival of a substantial community of **gnomes** brought new families and skills, creating opportunities as well as adjustments to village life.

The First Men are also associated with the ancient defenses of [[The Great Fork Tower]]. That shared name does not yet tell the whole story of Illmire's fall or the people who once lived there.

## The apprentice who left for Renada

Among Jogar's children was **[[Myxi Twinefoot]]**, whose family arranged an apprenticeship with a respected gnome banker after the newcomers settled in the village.

Myxi's aptitude for commerce eventually took her to [[Renada]], where she opened her own consulting practice before becoming Golden Hand of the Aegis. Her journey began in a community whose different peoples learned to make a shared home.

## The visiting wizard

Years before he became High Wizard, **[[Tel'Lora Ebbenflow]]** sought help in Jogar with an undeciphered spell in his mother's book. The village's learned gnomes could not resolve the mystery, but the search carried him a little farther along the road that would eventually bring him to the Misfits.

Jogar therefore belongs to more than one kind of story: an ancient city underneath, a village growing above, and the beginnings of two people who would one day advise the crown.
