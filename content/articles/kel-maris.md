## A swordswoman from Ilyanamel

**Kel'Maris** came from **[[The Smaller Settlements of Avalor#ilyanamel|Ilyanamel]]**, an elven village whose name would become familiar to the courts of Avalor through one of its daughters. She was the first daughter of a prominent elven family, raised in a tradition that valued mastery of arms. She trained from an early age with several kinds of weaponry, although two blades together became her particular distinction.

The young high elf was only **eighty-five** in the account of her service, an age still considered youthful among her people. She nevertheless came to occupy one of the kingdom's highest military offices. **Kel'Maris became the first elf to serve as Lord Marshal**, a distinction that placed her at the center of royal responsibility long before the Misfits became known.

Her reputation did not begin with an appointment. It was earned through discipline, skill, and years spent close enough to the throne for the crown to trust her judgment as well as her sword.

## The Kel Scythe

Kel'Maris favored a **longsword in one hand and a shortsword in the other**. Her distinctive dual-wielding form became known as the **Kel Scythe**. It was remembered for its devastating effectiveness against shield formations: a technique intended to break the ordered protection upon which armed opponents relied.

Her skill was demonstrated beyond private instruction. After moving to the capital, she entered the **Avalor Games** year after year, frequently placing among the kingdom's five leading swordsmen. **Twice she defeated every other competitor and won the tournament outright.** Such achievements made her formidable in a field where reputations were repeatedly tested rather than simply inherited.

The Games were not her only proving ground. The ability to train others would become central to the course of her life.

## Sword instructor to a prince

By order of **[[king-gerran-frozenroar|King Gerran Frozenroar]]**, Kel'Maris traveled to [[Renada]] to instruct his son **[[King Gradvum Frozenroar|Gradvum]]**, then a young prince expected to inherit the throne. She taught the future king swordsmanship and became his friend over the years that followed.

That friendship crossed the ordinary boundary between a royal pupil and a hired instructor. Kel'Maris knew Gradvum before he wore the crown; he knew her before the office of Lord Marshal made her one of Avalor's most visible guardians.

When the opportunity arose, she readily joined **[[The Avalorian Forces|Lion's Bane]]**, the elite royal order charged with protecting the crown. Her service took her from the practice ground into the permanent duties of a protector.

## The Lion's Shadow

Kel'Maris rose quickly through Lion's Bane. She became **second in command beneath Lord Marshal **[[kurt-reyginald|Kurt Reyginald]]** and was assigned as the king's personal guard, staying close enough to respond to a threat at a moment's notice.

Her almost constant presence earned her the name **the Lion's Shadow**. It was an epithet rooted not in secrecy but in constancy: the crown rarely stood without her nearby.

When Kurt retired, Kel'Maris succeeded him as **Lord Marshal**. She was already serving in that role when Gradvum inherited the throne. Her appointment therefore bridged two reigns and preserved an unusually personal bond between the new sovereign and the officer responsible for his protection.

## The commissions that brought the Misfits into royal service

Before they were celebrated as Avalor's champions, the companions later called **[[The Misfits]]** had survived the strange living halls of Moonville Manor. Kel'Maris then called upon them on behalf of the crown for their **[[the-first-commission|first royal commission]]**, an investigation of an unnamed necromancer.

She subsequently employed them again during the inquiry into the **[[golgoroth-second-commission|cult of Gol'Goroth]]**, the adventure that brought Heureux into their company. The choice to entrust these missions to adventurers rather than rely only upon the royal guard became an important part of the fellowship's early history.

Her final investigation took place in Stanleyville, before the later ruined-temple disaster. **[[captain-funkin|Captain Funkin]]** would return her body to Renada, then die at the Hydra temple along with Inspector Sihnion and guards, while Fo'Gurn sacrificed himself to save the others.

## Death at Stanleyville

When mysterious deaths troubled **[[Stanleyville]]**, Kel'Maris accompanied the Misfits into the town's danger. They were hunted by a creature called **[[The Stalker of Stanleyville|the Stalker]]**, later connected to the direction of [[Cid the Blood King]].

**The creature pierced Kel'Maris through the chest and killed her.** The Stalker affair preceded the ruined Hydra temple: she was already dead when the Misfits faced that later catastrophe. It was the final loss of the woman who had entrusted the adventurers with their first commissions.

**Captain Funkin returned her body safely to Renada** for a state funeral. For **three days** she lay in state at the **[[The Great Cathedral|Great Cathedral]]**, where the kingdom could honor the officer who had defended the crown through two reigns. In accordance with her wishes, she was **cremated in a private ceremony**, and her ashes were scattered across the **[[The Geography of Avalor#king-s-forest|King's Forest]]**.

With the Lion's Shadow gone, the office of Lord Marshal remained vacant for the rest of the Misfits' campaign. Only after Cid's defeat did King Gradvum appoint **[[Lavitz Shawe]]** as her successor.

## A lifetime beside the lion

Kel'Maris was a champion swordswoman, a royal officer, and a tutor entrusted with the education of a future king. She was also his friend. Those roles help explain why her death mattered to Avalor beyond the vacancy it created in the council.

The stories of the Misfits now remember her by name: not simply as the unnamed Lord Marshal who sent them upon their first commissions, but as the **Lion's Shadow**, whose judgment helped place an uncelebrated company on the path that eventually changed the kingdom's history.
