## Two enemies, two histories

Not every adversary of [[The Misfits]] served the same master. Among the groups the fellowship faced were **the Cult of Gol’Goroth** and **the Wrath of Innocent**, distinct organizations whose encounters with the adventurers left a mark upon their story.

Both suffered defeats during the Misfits' adventures. **Whether either still operates is unknown.** Their histories belong together here without making them allies, descendants of one another, or parts of Cid's designs.

## The Cult of Gol’Goroth

After their first commission against an unnamed necromancer, the companions were summoned again by the preceding Lord Marshal to investigate **a cult devoted to Gol’Goroth**.

The second commission became especially important because **[[Heureux]] joined the company** during its course. The band that would become the Misfits grew through such encounters, rather than forming all at once.

The cult's known threat was defeated, but its wider teachings, named leadership, and the fate of possible surviving adherents remain unclear. Neither the cult's beliefs nor Gol’Goroth's precise nature have been fully described.

**There is no established evidence that Cid founded or commanded this cult.** The Misfits' early dangers were not all branches of the Blood King's work.

## The Wrath of Innocent

**The Wrath of Innocent** was founded by **Jinlin Vechon**, a dragonborn vampire whose hatred of **[[Cid the Blood King]]** was genuine.

The Misfits encountered Jinlin after their meeting with [[Jaznaar]]. His hostility toward Cid might have seemed a reason to trust him, but the fellowship discovered that sharing an enemy did not guarantee the same understanding of justice.

The group was defeated during the companions' travels. What became of its remaining followers and whether the name survived as an active cause have not been established.

## Jinlin Vechon

Jinlin remains the group's most recognizable figure. As its founder, he belongs within this history rather than needing a second article that repeats the same few events.

The encounter with him is preserved in [[The Misfits#jinlin-vechon-and-the-wrath-of-innocent|the Misfits' chronicle]]. Later, [[Zauks and the False Lives|Zauks]] recreated **Jinlin's lair** from the companions' memories. That false return was part of the mind flayer's illusions, not evidence that Jinlin had resumed his work.

His hatred of Cid never made him a trustworthy ally, and his group's name was no guarantee of its justice.

## After their defeats

Both factions are remembered through the Misfits' victories, but **defeat is not the same as proven extinction**. Perhaps their adherents scattered; perhaps a few carried old loyalties into later years. Neither story has been confirmed.

The Chronicle preserves what the companions faced and the consequences known to them, without making claims about secret survivors or renewed activity in the Age of Ascendancy.
