**Black Agnes Mudwallow** was a **night hag** who moved in the company of [[Cid the Blood King]] and his other servants. Her name appears among the enemies present at the ruined temple near Stanleyville, where the Misfits and other prisoners were held for a rite involving blood and divine power. She belonged to the same dangerous circle as [[jaaris|Jaaris]], [[karina|Karina]], and [[zauks|Zauks]], though that association does not establish that they shared a single purpose beyond serving the Blood King's interests.

The **Misfits defeated and killed Black Agnes** during the original campaign. Unlike Jaaris, whose crimes marked several of the companions personally, Agnes is chiefly remembered through her place among Cid's enforcers. The location and circumstances of her death are not securely recorded. Her story needs no imagined childhood, elaborate secret society, or second confrontation to explain why an encounter with a night hag in the Blood King's service was perilous.
