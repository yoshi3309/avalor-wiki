## The labor of a queen

**Queen Melmiri** shares the throne of Avalor with [[King Gradvum Frozenroar]]. The king retains ultimate sovereign authority, but the surviving record gives the queen an unusually substantial place in the kingdom's daily administration.

The management of a realm depends on matters that seldom inspire songs: roads and ports, public order, supplies, revenue, correspondence, and the competing needs of communities far from the capital. Melmiri attends closely to this machinery of government.

To describe her only as the king's consort would neglect the work through which the present reign is conducted.

## Inheritance of the Northern Desert Tribes

Melmiri's ancestry is associated with the **Northern Desert Tribes**, peoples whose histories extend far beyond the royal household. Her family heritage has helped bring the north's communities into the public conversation of Avalor's court.

That connection must not become a claim that one queen speaks for every tribe, or that communities possessing different customs belong to a single undifferentiated people. The [[Great Desert]] is inhabited country with its own traditions and memories, not merely a distant region from which the queen emerged.

## Dina Hyanda

Melmiri founded [[Dina Hyanda]], a **women's royal protective order** distinct from the guard known as [[Lion's Bane]].

The distinction is institutional as well as personal. Lion's Bane serves under the Lord Marshal, [[Lavitz Shawe]]. Dina Hyanda derives its founding tradition from Melmiri. The two orders may appear in some of the same spaces of royal life, but they should not be mistaken for one company with two names.

The public record does not establish a complete command roster, membership count, or detailed division of guard duties. The Royal Archives preserve the distinction without inventing regulations.

## A familiar figure in arms

The queen is known to ride the griffin **Dune Rakka** and carry the scimitar **Darkheart**. Such details are readily repeated in public accounts, where the image of a queen bearing arms attracts more attention than the work of arranging a kingdom's ordinary affairs.

Melmiri's influence cannot be measured solely through her mount or weapon.

## The Tournament and the rescue

During [[The Tournament of Champions]], Cid the Blood King seized Queen Melmiri. [[The Misfits]] pursued him to the Shadowfell, where they fought to free her and brought his immediate threat to an end.

The rescue became a defining event in the stories told about the age. It remains only one chapter of her public life. The queen returned to the demands of government in a realm whose history had been permanently altered by the revelation of Cid's true identity.

Her present legacy rests as much in administration and enduring institutions as in the danger from which she was rescued.
