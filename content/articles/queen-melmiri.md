## More than a royal consort

**Queen Melmiri** is among the most important working figures in Avalor's government. While [[King Gradvum Frozenroar]] retains ultimate sovereign authority, she oversees much of the daily administration that turns the crown's decisions into action.

The work is rarely celebrated in ballads. Letters must be answered, competing claims assessed, and arrangements made so that the kingdom continues to function when no great danger is approaching its walls.

Melmiri is therefore not best understood simply as the king's companion in ceremony. Her influence is found in the work she undertakes.

## A queen who acts

Melmiri founded **[[Dina Hyanda]]**, a women's royal protective order with a distinct identity from the [[Lion's Bane]] guard commanded by Lord Marshal [[Lavitz Shawe]]. Its creation is a decision associated with her personally, not merely a tradition she inherited as queen.

She is also known to ride the griffin **Dune Rakka** and to bear the scimitar **Darkheart**. Those symbols have made her a recognizable figure in accounts of the royal household. They belong beside, rather than in place of, her administrative accomplishments.

The available history does not identify her complete system of command or every officer of Dina Hyanda. Her importance does not depend upon an invented military record.

## Heritage and family

Melmiri's ancestry connects her to the **Northern Desert Tribes**, whose communities maintain histories and traditions of their own. She is not presumed to speak for every tribe simply because she has become Avalor's queen.

Her brother, **[[Sebastian Rubelda]]**, serves as sheriff of the Northern March. Sebastian admires Avalorian customs and wishes to see greater cooperation among the tribes, having grown weary of their infighting. His hopes are his own; the record does not make them a policy imposed by Melmiri.

She and Gradvum have three children: **Theodric**, **Leontes**, and **Vivienne**. Their relationships and personalities beyond the established family record remain to be developed.

## The queen who returned

At [[The Tournament of Champions]], [[Cid the Blood King]] abducted Melmiri and carried her into the Shadowfell. [[The Misfits]] pursued him, rescued the queen, and defeated the immediate threat he posed.

The rescue was extraordinary, but it was not the end of her story. Melmiri returned to a kingdom now forced to reconsider its oldest history, and to responsibilities that had not ceased while she was gone.

Her defining public record is one of **continued service**: not only a queen who survived a famous danger, but a ruler who returned to the everyday work of keeping Avalor together.
