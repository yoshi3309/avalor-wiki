## From the Mudsteps to the citadel

The name of **Lavitz Shawe** is now spoken in the same halls where royal policy is made. Yet his childhood belonged to the **Mudsteps**, the working river quarter of [[Renada]], far below the heights of the Great Citadel.

Lavitz trained in the paladin tradition of [[Heaven's Heart]]. The convictions taught there—and the friends with whom he later traveled—shaped the warrior who would help confront the Blood King.

## One of the Misfits

As a member of [[The Misfits]], Lavitz fought alongside companions whose courage helped end the long deception of [[Cid the Blood King]]. Their victory also made possible the public vindication of [[Jaznaar]], whose false condemnation had helped sustain centuries of prejudice against drow communities.

## The burden of command

Lavitz now serves on [[The Aegis]] as **Lord Marshal**. He is the crown's chief military adviser and commands the **Lion's Bane** royal guard. The office demands a different kind of courage from that expected of an adventurer. A hero may risk his own life; a commander must prepare others to face danger and answer for decisions that affect the lives of soldiers and civilians alike.

His public honor has helped alter Avalor's understanding of who may serve the kingdom. His greater task is less easily celebrated: keeping the realm prepared, safe, and worthy of those who defend it.
