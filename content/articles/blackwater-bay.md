## A harbor and its people

**Blackwater Bay** is a coastal place known in accounts of sailors, merchants, and people who make their living in the movement of vessels and goods. Its name is inseparable from the later undertaking for which **[[Lavitz Shawe]]**, Lord Marshal of Avalor, recruited a company of unusually capable operatives.

The surviving accounts place Blackwater Bay within a maritime world of harbors, coastal routes, and practical arrangements. They do not establish its exact place on the royal atlas, a particular neighboring port, or a surveyed boundary for the bay.

## The Burntwood Tavern

Among its familiar establishments is the **Burntwood Tavern**, kept by **[[Ambrose Burntwood]]**, a mountain dwarf whose patient hospitality draws sailors, traders, adventurers, and travelers. Ambrose is known for remembering faces and names, keeping useful friendships across coastal communities, and helping people who arrive in difficult circumstances.

The tavern is more than a convenient place to find a drink or bed. Its proprietor has a reputation for finding quiet, practical ways through problems, usually preferring conversation and safe passage to needless violence.

## A mission before its chronicle

The name Blackwater Bay later became associated with an undertaking that drew together **[[Silas Dreadmore]]**, **[[Notfred]]**, **[[Naeris Thorne]]**, **[[mg-the-fixer|MG the Fixer]]**, and **[[Ambrose Burntwood]]** under Lavitz's recruitment.

Their different backgrounds—cursed magic, life beyond the law, maritime archaeology, clandestine negotiation, and coastal experience—made them a distinctive company. The sequence and outcome of their mission belong to its own historical account; the present entry records the place and the people before that story is told.
