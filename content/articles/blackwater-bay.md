## A harbor of more than one kind of fortune

From the cliffs overlooking its approaches, **Blackwater Bay** appeared as an immense harbor city. Ships, waterfront trade, merchant business, and the commerce of a busy port unfolded beside smuggling, intimidation, and violence. It was a place where a respectable livelihood and a criminal enterprise might stand within sight of one another, neither wholly defining the city.

Blackwater's lawlessness was more than a colorful reputation. A powerful organization could claim streets and docks, station armed patrols, and exercise influence over commercial movements without behaving as an ordinary crown authority. Yet the people of the city were not all outlaws. Sailors needed wages, merchants needed cargo delivered, and families and dockworkers lived amid the dangers that accompanied the trade.

The city lay beyond the ordinary reach of Avalor's royal forces. To those who traveled its streets, Blackwater was defined less by distant royal charts than by the merchants, captains, syndicates, and communities whose competing interests shaped its harbor.

## Commerce, violence, and the Black Diamond

In **2020 AF**, the **[[The Black Diamond|Black Diamond]]** possessed substantial influence within Blackwater Bay. Its reach included a district where trade was not left to chance: merchants moved through inspections and checkpoints, officials and guards scrutinized documents, and shipments were organized according to commercial schedules.

The guarded quarter stood in striking contrast with the wider city's unpredictable streets. Here, manifests, passes, merchant insignia, and patrol discipline could be as important as a sword. Being able to conduct lawful-looking business within that system did not mean the Black Diamond itself was an ordinary merchant association. Its armed presence and hidden work gave its commercial order a darker purpose.

The organization was associated with **[[Esogoth]]**, a figure powerful enough to direct operations and confront intruders at the harbor. Accounts of its deeper structure and origins remain incomplete. Nor should the district's elaborate controls be mistaken for proof that the Black Diamond governed every street or that every resident supported it.

## The merchant gate

One approach to the guarded quarter passed through a **merchant checkpoint**. Entry depended upon recognizable papers, appropriate attire and marks of commercial identity, and the ability to satisfy those inspecting the movement of goods and people.

The fact that merchants could pass this threshold did not make the district safe. Those familiar with Blackwater's underworld understood that getting inside with convincing documents could be easier than leaving once the Black Diamond became interested in a person's business.

During the 2020 AF affair, a party of outsiders entered under merchant cover, assisted by a local trader's legitimate papers and his knowledge of the inspection process. The particulars of their assignment belong to the separate history of the undertaking, but the episode illustrates how easily ordinary commerce could become entangled with the organization's guarded interests.

## The Artifact's Rest

The **Artifact's Rest** was a harbor tavern within the Black Diamond-controlled district. Sailors, dockworkers, captains, and merchants came there, bringing with them news of arrivals, schedules, cargoes, and the sudden changes of plan that could unsettle a port.

It was not simply a hideout for criminals, nor a gathering place free from the pressures of its surroundings. Information moved through a room of working mariners precisely because ships and cargo could not depart unnoticed by everyone who relied upon the docks.

It was here that the visiting operatives sought to understand a hurried departure connected to Esogoth. Conversations with patrons gave them fragments of the movements around the harbor, even as the clock of the district's commercial business continued to turn.

## Pier Seven

**Pier Seven** was one of the harbor's documented berths and the point from which a vessel associated with Esogoth prepared to depart in 2020 AF.

A shipment of exceptional size was seen being loaded there. The object was no easily carried trinket: it was so immense that moving it separately from the ship would have required far more than a quiet theft or a hurried flight through the streets.

The pier became central to the events later remembered as the Blackwater Bay affair.

## The Teeth

Beyond the harbor lay a perilous passage called **the Teeth**, known for its jagged rocks and the demands it made upon anyone trying to take a vessel through them. Even for experienced sailors, careless steering could mean a ruined hull or a ship left exposed to pursuit.

In the 2020 AF affair, a vessel leaving Blackwater under pressure passed through the Teeth with help from its experienced captain and sailors. The dangers of the rocks were real, yet the passage also offered a chance to put distance between the ship and those pursuing it.

The Teeth were a hazard of the harbor's maritime approaches, forcing even capable captains to pay close attention to the rocks and currents around their hulls.

## The affair of 2020 AF

In **2020 AF**, twenty years after the Misfits defeated the Blood King, **[[Lavitz Shawe]]** commissioned five discreet operatives to investigate a dangerous object associated with the Black Diamond. Their presence in Blackwater was not an open royal expedition. The mission demanded the sort of discretion that famous champions or uniformed royal troops could not have brought into a volatile foreign port.

Those recruited were **[[Silas Dreadmore]]**, **[[Naeris Thorne]]**, **[[mg-the-fixer|MG the Fixer]]**, **[[Notfred]]**, and **[[Ambrose Burntwood]]**. Others would become indispensable as the undertaking unfolded, but these five were its original operatives. Their company had no formal party name.

The mission is reconstructed in the separate historical account **[[Escape from Blackwater Bay]]**. The main operation occupied **approximately one day**. Accounts of what followed extend beyond that day and beyond the harbor, into the lives of those involved and the history of Avalor itself. Its clandestine beginnings should not be confused with an announcement to the people of Blackwater, nor should the whole city's identity be reduced to the acts of its most powerful criminal interests.

Blackwater Bay was a city before the operatives arrived and remained a city after they departed: a place of work, ambition, desperation, and everyday life amid powers whose hold could be felt from the guarded gate to the water's edge.
