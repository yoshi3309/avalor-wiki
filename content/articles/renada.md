## The city beneath the lion

The Great Citadel rises above [[Renada]], yet the capital belongs as much to the people working its riverfront as to those who govern behind its walls. Traders, shipwrights, clerks, scholars, and families fill a city whose broad community numbers between **one hundred thousand and two hundred thousand** people, depending on how its outlying settlements are counted.

## Older than the crown

Renada existed before the founding of Avalor. Rynhold Frozenroar chose it as the capital of his new realm rather than simply extending the power of Kroubia's previous royal seat. The choice signaled a political union, not merely the enlargement of his former kingdom.

[[The Great Citadel]] was first raised during Rynhold's reign and has been expanded and altered by generations of sovereigns. It remains the seat of royal government as well as a working household sustained by scribes, servants, craftsmen, and officials.

## The River Rhys

The **River Rhys** connects the capital to inland commerce and routes toward the coast. The celebrated **Great Stone Bridge** carries both ceremonies and ordinary traffic; **Millers' Bridge** is an indispensable crossing for grain merchants and riverside workers. The [[Rhys Quays]] are the city's working heart, where cargo moves between vessels and the King's Road.

## A capital of contrasts

Renada contains districts of considerable wealth and neighborhoods where a family's wages may depend upon the next ship to arrive. [[Crownrise]] climbs toward the citadel, while [[Old Renada]] preserves older streets and traces of the settlement that preceded the kingdom. Along the waterfront, the [[Mudsteps]] is a poor river-quarter neighborhood where the current Lord Marshal, [[Lavitz Shawe]], spent his childhood.

The city has benefited from the kingdom's Age of Ascendancy, though its expansion has not reached every household equally. One can admire the lion banner above the heights and still ask whose work keeps it flying.

## Cathedral Ward and the heights

At the heart of the capital, [[Cathedral Ward]] surrounds [[The Great Cathedral]], which serves the city's religious and charitable life. Above the working streets, [[The Great Citadel]] remains the seat of royal government. Both institutions are woven into Renada's daily life, though they represent different obligations to its people.


## The neighborhoods and public places

Renada's public life extends beyond the buildings of the crown. The merchants of [[Lantern Ward]] maintain the city's markets and the Hall of Measures, while the [[Rhys Quays]] connect its labor and warehouses to the River Rhys. The [[Mudsteps]], within the quays, are remembered as the childhood neighborhood of Lord Marshal Lavitz Shawe. In [[Rynhold Park]], the surviving statue of Aldus Greystone III bears a revised inscription acknowledging the false history once taught about Jaznaar.

[[Cathedral Ward]] completes the record of Renada's five major districts through its religious and charitable institutions. These places remain parts of Renada, not separate settlements of the kingdom. The Royal Archives record their distinct histories without giving each another pin on the world map.
