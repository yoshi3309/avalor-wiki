## The many forms of devotion

Religion in Avalor is woven into daily life through temples, family traditions, sacred obligations, and acts of service. Though the [[The Kingdom of Avalor|kingdom]] is home to influential religious institutions, its peoples do not share a single manner of worship. Faith may be expressed in formal rites, in care for those without shelter, in the work of a craftsman, or in the remembrance of those who came before.

## The Great Cathedral

The **Great Cathedral** in [[Renada]] is among the realm's most influential religious institutions. Its [[The Threefold Devotion|Threefold Devotion]] honors **Tyr**, **Helm**, and **Torm**, whose teachings concern justice, vigilance, courage, and duty. The Cathedral is led by [[Astronoph]], a female deva serving as High Priest.

At [[Heaven's Heart]], the [[The Cathedral Paladins|paladin traditions]] founded by [[Lowkee]] draw upon those same divine ideals while placing special emphasis on the protection of people who cannot defend themselves. The legacy of the founding paladin reminds the faithful that sacred duty is more demanding than unquestioning obedience to authority.

## Dawn, craft, and song

Worship of [[Lathander and the Devotion of the Dawn|Lathander]] is associated with renewal and new beginnings. In some communities, the custom called the **First Loaf** sets aside food at dawn for neighbors who might otherwise go hungry. The memory of [[Petris]], one of [[The Six]], remains closely associated with such compassionate service.

As explored in [[The Faiths of Families and Peoples]], some elven communities honor **Corellon** through art and ceremonies connected with beauty and creation. Certain dwarven families call upon **Moradin** at the forge and in traditions of patient craft. Devotion to **Eilistraee** survives within some drow communities through gathering, song, and teachings concerned with freedom and self-determination.

These traditions cannot be assigned to every member of any ancestry. Avalorian families may inherit several forms of devotion, observe different rites, or share faiths with neighbors of very different backgrounds.

## Sacred balance and the living world

Some Avalorians understand spiritual duty through the cycles of nature rather than worship of a single named deity. [[The Sacred Balance]] describes the druidic tradition exemplified by [[Kelsalor]], who regarded care for living systems as a responsibility shared across generations.

## Faith and the life of the realm

During the persecution of drow citizens, individuals at Heaven's Heart protected families endangered by royal decrees. Their actions became part of a larger history in which religious duty has sometimes supported the crown and sometimes challenged it.

Avalor's present monarchy permits religious freedom. Differences of devotion remain, but belonging to the kingdom does not require its people to address the divine in the same language.
