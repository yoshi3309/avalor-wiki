## A house unlike any other

**Moonville Manor** is remembered in the travels of [[The Misfits]] as a **living house**. The simple description invites questions that the public chronicle does not answer: what made the house alive, what it wanted, and how its inhabitants experienced the encounter.

The name is established. A room-by-room account, its precise location, and the manner in which the adventure ended are not. They should not be supplied from the conventions of other haunted-house tales.

## An adventure of its own

The Misfits encountered Moonville Manor among several strange experiences that did not all arise from the schemes of Cid the Blood King. Accounts of the period also mention the Ripped Beard and Noki, a wizard transformed into a sheep.

Those events belonged to the companions' wider travels. An unfamiliar enchantment was not automatically evidence of the same necromancy or of a common master behind every danger.

## The house in remembered trials

Later, the mind flayer Zauks confronted the companions with magical **imitations of earlier ordeals**. Moonville Manor was among them.

That later vision was not a second documented visit to the original house. It was a memory turned into a weapon against those who had survived it. The distinction matters when reconstructing the fellowship's chronology.

Moonville Manor remains one of Avalor's better-known mysteries precisely because its witnesses have not left a complete public description. The Royal Archives record its existence without pretending that the unanswered questions are already settled.
