## Different callings, different guilds

Not every guild in Avalor has a history filled with named founders and famous expeditions. Some are remembered first through the towns where they operate. The **Thieves Guild** and **Clerics Guild** are two such organizations: important to the places they call home, but still awaiting fuller histories of their own.

They are gathered here for convenience, **not because they answer to a common authority**.

## The Thieves Guild

The **Thieves Guild** has its home in **[[The Smaller Settlements of Avalor#olivruch|Olivruch]]**, an otherwise modest town that rarely commands the attention of court historians.

Its presence gives the settlement a reputation beyond its size. There is more to Olivruch than the guild, however, and the name does not make every resident a thief.

Stories of the guild's members, leadership, customs, and rivalries can grow as the people involved become better known. Its established place in Olivruch needs no invented hierarchy to be worth recording.

## The Clerics Guild

The **Clerics Guild** is based in **[[Stannisport]]**, a trading port spanning the River Juce and joined by Heroes Bridge.

The city brings together sailors, merchants, travelers, and families from different routes. That busy setting is part of the guild's identity, though its particular services and internal traditions remain stories to be developed.

The Clerics Guild is not automatically the same institution as [[The Cathedral Paladins|the Cathedral Paladins]] or the leadership of [[The Great Cathedral]]. A shared connection to religious life does not give every organization the same members or authority.

## The guilds with fuller stories

Two other guilds already have histories substantial enough to tell separately. [[The Hunters Guild]] was founded by [[Faelynn]] at [[Wayword Sun]] to protect travelers and vulnerable settlements. [[The Bard Guild]] is based in [[Taveras]], whose great performance culture also nurtured [[Heureux’s Bard Academy]].

The academy is a school, distinct from the Bard Guild. And neither the hunters nor the bards are branches of the smaller guilds described here.

In time, the Thieves Guild and Clerics Guild may acquire stories that deserve their own pages. Until then, their shared account preserves their names and homes without turning a handful of facts into two thin biographies.
