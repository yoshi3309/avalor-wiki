## Different callings, different guilds

The word **guild** can hide very different meanings in Avalor. In one city it describes a fellowship of healers whose doors are open to every faith. In another it describes a loose association of professional thieves who would rather the crown paid them no attention at all.

The **Silver Forge** and **Thieves Guild** have little reason to share a charter, a cause, or a set of rules. Their stories belong together here because both are part of the life of Avalor, even though their origins and purposes could scarcely be more different.

## The Thieves Guild

In **[[The Smaller Settlements of Avalor#olivruch|Olivruch]]** operates Avalor's branch of the **Thieves Guild**, an association of **professional thieves** who provide mutual support for the unlawful work of their members and their clients.

Calling it a *guild* is generous. It has neither the respectability nor necessarily the rigid structure the word suggests when applied to a craft or scholarly order. Its members nevertheless recognize the value of allies, contacts, and people prepared to help one another when a job proves dangerous.

Olivruch is only **one branch of a network with a presence across the wider world**. Avalor's underworld is not the whole of that network, and the guild's existence elsewhere does not establish who rules its other branches or where they operate.

### Salt and the Ragged Man

The man known as **Salt** presently leads the **Avalorian branch** of this unofficial, decidedly real organization. His position makes him an important figure to those whose business relies upon the guild, even if the kingdom's respectable institutions would rather not treat his office as legitimate.

There are **whispers that Salt answers to someone known as *the Ragged Man***. Whether the Ragged Man is a person, a title, or merely a rumor has not been established. For now, the suggestion of another authority remains precisely that: a whisper, not proof of a hierarchy.

The existence of the guild says little about the ordinary people of Olivruch. A town can harbor an unlawful enterprise without every citizen belonging to it.

## The Clerics Guild

The **Clerics Guild**, known as **the Silver Forge**, is based in **[[Stannisport]]**, the great trading city that spans the River Juce. Its beginnings reach back to the founding of the Kingdom of Avalor itself.

Before unification, clerics were connected by **loose fellowships scattered among different kingdoms, towns, and communities**. They shared knowledge and brought healing where it was needed, but did not belong to a common institution. When [[Rynhold Frozenroar]] united the northern crowns in **1 AF**, those scattered traditions came together to found **the Silver Forge**.

The guild's reputation rests upon profound wisdom and formidable strength, but **empathy is its defining virtue**. Its members may come from different peoples, honor different gods, and disagree about the meaning of their faiths. Within the Silver Forge, **every deity, belief, and spiritual path is to be treated with respect**. A cleric need not surrender their convictions to find a home among its people.

The Silver Forge's calling is to be a place of healing and hope amid suffering, rather than the property of one temple or a single god's priesthood.

### Petris and Aldus

Two of the earliest heroes whose lives crossed the Silver Forge were **[[Petris]]** and **[[Aldus Greystone III]]**.

Petris, the elven cleric of Lathander, **studied among the guild's healers and later returned to teach**, passing on the compassion that defined her service. For her, healing meant helping the person before her without asking what reward they might provide.

Aldus also **studied restorative magic at the Silver Forge**. Much later, when his desire to relieve suffering had become a determination to **abolish death itself**, he returned in search of further knowledge. His eventual atrocities were his own; his studies do not mean the Silver Forge endorsed the methods or ambitions that led him toward the Black Tower.

The two histories offer a painful contrast. A place founded to respect life helped teach both a woman who spent her life tending it and a man who would eventually decide that life must be remade at any cost.

The Silver Forge is distinct from [[The Cathedral Paladins|the Cathedral Paladins]] and from the leadership of [[The Great Cathedral]]. Its hospitality across faiths is its own calling, not proof that all of Avalor's religious orders are one institution.

## The guilds with fuller stories

Two other guilds already have histories substantial enough to tell separately. [[The Hunters Guild]] was founded by [[Faelynn]] at [[Wayword Sun]] to protect travelers and vulnerable settlements. [[The Bard Guild]] is based in [[Taveras]], whose great performance culture also nurtured [[Heureux’s Bard Academy]].

The academy is a school, distinct from the Bard Guild. And neither the hunters nor the bards are branches of the smaller guilds described here.

The Silver Forge's founding and the names of Salt and the Ragged Man have added depth to these guilds, but both still have stories to tell. Their shared account can grow with the people who shape them, without dividing their histories into unnecessary volumes.
