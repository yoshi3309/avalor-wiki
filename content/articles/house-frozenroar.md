## The first crown

Before he became Avalor's first king, **Rynhold Frozenroar** ruled the northern kingdom of Kroubia. His coalition against Lord Osmo survived its own victory and became a new political union in **1 AF**. He chose [[Renada]] as the capital and founded institutions that could outlive their creator.

## Thirty-four sovereigns

The royal register records **thirty-four successive rulers**, from Rynhold to the present king. Their reigns encompass the Elven Concord, the War of the Two Lions, conflicts over royal authority, the persecution and later restoration of drow rights, periods of maritime expansion, and the difficult political settlement following Wolfram's war overseas.

The line has endured not because every ruler governed wisely, but because the kingdom has repeatedly been compelled to confront its failures and revise its understanding of its founding promise.

## The present royal household

[[King Gradvum Frozenroar]], on the throne since 1983 AF, is widely admired for his approachable manner. [[Queen Melmiri]], whose family traditions are associated with the Northern Desert Tribes, plays a central role in the daily administration of the realm. Their family has three children; the eldest son is heir.

The king's griffin is **Fis Uskeche**, and his sword is **Lionsbane**. Queen Melmiri rides **Dune Rakka** and bears the scimitar **Darkheart**.

House Frozenroar stands at the center of Avalor's political history. It has never been the whole of that history.
