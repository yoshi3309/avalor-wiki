Avalor's history is shaped by people who did not lead famous fellowships or sit upon the throne. Some served the kingdom for years; others appear in the record only through a final duty. These **short biographies** preserve what is known without inventing lengthy private histories to fill the gaps.

Each name below has a permanent section link. Readers looking for more extensive histories can follow the related accounts of [[The Avalorian Forces|Avalor's royal forces]], [[kel-maris|Kel'Maris]], and [[Queen Melmiri]].

## Kurt Reyginald

**Kurt Reyginald** was **Lord Marshal of Lion's Bane** under King [[king-gerran-frozenroar|Gerran Frozenroar]]. He commanded the royal guard while [[kel-maris|Kel'Maris]] rose through its ranks to become his second in command. Her skill and the trust she gained at court were formed in part during those years of service under Kurt.

Kurt **retired**, and Kel'Maris succeeded him before Prince Gradvum inherited the throne. The old marshal's private life and earlier campaigns are not established; his place in the archive is as the officer who led Lion's Bane before the Lion's Shadow.

## Captain Funkin

**Captain Funkin** served among Renada's officers during the Misfits' struggle against the Blood King. When the Stalker murdered **[[kel-maris|Kel'Maris]]** in [[Stanleyville]], Funkin **returned her body safely to Renada** so the kingdom could honor its Lord Marshal and carry out her final wishes.

He survived that investigation but died in the **later** [[hydra-temple-disaster|Hydra temple disaster]], alongside Inspector Sihnion and trapped guards. He died in **2000 AF**, during the third month of the Misfits' campaign; the exact day is unrecorded. The sequence is important: Funkin brought a fallen protector home before becoming one of the people Avalor itself had to mourn.

## Inspector Sihnion

**Inspector Sihnion** was caught with Captain Funkin and Renadan guards in the disaster at the ruined temple of Bane near Stanleyville. The prisoners there faced magical restraints, a bloody rite, and servants working toward [[cid-the-blood-king|Cid's]] ambitions.

When the failed rite gave way to a hydra and the temple's destruction, **Sihnion died** along with Funkin and other guards, during the third campaign month of **2000 AF**. His earlier assignments are not recorded, but his name belongs beside theirs, not hidden in a general count of casualties.

## Quill

**Quill** is a **tortle** and commander of the **Royal Guard**, one of the divisions of [[The Avalorian Forces|Lion's Bane]] under Lord Marshal [[Lavitz Shawe]]. His uncle is **[[Rastrin Diltir]]**, the Aegis's Warden of Order. Both hold responsibilities for protecting Avalor, though they serve in different capacities: Rastrin through law and deliberation, Quill through the work of the royal guard.

Quill's place in the army's command structure is established; a personal military career beyond that office has not yet been supplied.

## Mira

**Mira** is a **female tabaxi** who commands the **Lion's Claws**, Lion's Bane's elite ground operatives. These soldiers undertake demanding missions as part of the same royal institution as the Royal Guard and Skyward Guard.

Mira answers to Lord Marshal Lavitz, but her role is not interchangeable with that of the other division commanders. Her authority belongs to the Claws and the dangerous ground assignments entrusted to them.

## Nimbus

**Nimbus**, an **aarakocra**, leads the **Skyward Guard**, the aerial division of Lion's Bane associated with gryffion riders. Unlike the riders he commands, Nimbus **flies on his own wings**, meeting his troops in the air without a gryffion mount.

He serves under Lavitz alongside Quill and Mira. His unusual form of flight distinguishes him from his riders; the record does not require a newly invented campaign or personal legend to justify his command.

## Nadja Akayrn

**Nadja Akayrn** is a **female dragonborn** who commands **Dina Hyanda**, the royal protective order of twelve women established by **[[Queen Melmiri]]** after her captivity under Cid. The queen's decision created an intimate guard with a distinct identity from Lion's Bane and the standing army.

Nadja leads the Twelve **as one of their own members**. The order trains with the kingdom's finest teachers, including some former adventurers. Its separate identity matters: Nadja is the commander of Melmiri's protectors, not another division leader beneath Lord Marshal Lavitz.
