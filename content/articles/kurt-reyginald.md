**Kurt Reyginald** held the office of **Lord Marshal of [[The Avalorian Forces|Lion's Bane]]** during the reign of King Gerran Frozenroar. Under his command, the high-elven swordswoman **[[kel-maris|Kel'Maris]]** rose through the royal guard to become second in command. While she served as the king's close personal protector, Kurt remained the officer at the head of the order.

Kurt **retired**, and Kel'Maris succeeded him as Lord Marshal before Prince Gradvum inherited the throne. That orderly transfer placed the Lion's Shadow in command of a guard whose duties she already knew intimately. Kurt's earlier battles and private life have not been recorded in the creator's account, and his place in Avalor's history is adequately marked by the office he held and the successor he left behind.
