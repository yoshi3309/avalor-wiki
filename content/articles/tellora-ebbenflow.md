## Scholar and companion

**Tel'Lora Ebbenflow**, a male half-elf wizard, is counted among [[The Misfits]], the adventuring companions whose actions brought the long history of Cid the Blood King to an end. His knowledge of magic and of the dangers associated with its misuse has shaped his later service to Avalor.

## The eighth seat

Tel'Lora's appointment as **High Wizard** marked a change in the governing structure of [[The Aegis]]. Previous holders of the office had advised the crown through other royal institutions; his appointment elevated magical affairs to their own independent council seat.

## Shamman’s Tower

After the victory over Cid, Tel'Lora revived [[Shamman’s Tower]], a magical institution off the eastern coast, as a center of arcane education. His duties now join scholarship and the responsibilities of public office.
