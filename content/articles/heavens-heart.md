## At the foot of the mountains

**Heaven's Heart** is a sanctuary and settlement at the foot of the [[Spine of the World]], known for paladin training, scholarship, and the preservation of the kingdom's historical records. Its influence reaches beyond the mountain approaches through the students, religious traditions, and histories that have passed through its halls.

## The teaching of Lowkee

The order of [[The Cathedral Paladins|Cathedral Paladins]] traces its origins to [[Lowkee]], one of [[The Six]], and honors the ideals associated with Tyr, Helm, and Torm. Lowkee's most enduring teaching placed the protection of those who cannot defend themselves above simple obedience to authority.

The paladins who train here inherit obligations rooted in that conviction. Their service has, at times, placed them in conflict with unjust commands, including during the historical persecution of drow families.

## A place of learning

[[The Great Library of Aldus Greystone]] preserves historical and theological scholarship. Its collections have been especially important in an age when the recovery of the true Black Tower history has forced the kingdom to reconsider once-trusted accounts.

The nearby [[The Black Tower#the-black-stair|Black Stair]] is the difficult ascent **to the Black Tower**. Heaven's Heart stands apart from that ruin. The sanctuary's libraries and the tower's history have become linked through the correction of Aldus's legend, not through a common entrance.

## Sister institutions of the faith

The paladin traditions here share the faiths of Tyr, Helm, and Torm with [[The Great Cathedral]] in [[Renada#cathedral-ward|Cathedral Ward]], Renada. **The Cathedral Paladins** train protectors at Heaven's Heart; the **Great Cathedral**, led by [[Astronoph]], serves worshippers and petitioners in the capital. [[Samuel the Devoted|Samuel]] teaches paladins and also serves on [[The Aegis]], but these duties do not make the two institutions one order.

The **[[The Guilds of Avalor#the-clerics-guild|Silver Forge]]** at Stannisport follows another calling: a clerical fellowship open to differing faiths, rather than a branch of the paladins or the Renadan cathedral.

## Companions who trained here

During a visit from Renada's High Priest [[Astronoph]], the young [[Lavitz Shawe]] was encouraged to enter the paladin order. He trained alongside [[fo-gurn|Fo’Gurn]], a brass dragonborn who became one of his closest friends. Fo’Gurn's later sacrifice against a hydra became an important part of the fellowship's public memory.
