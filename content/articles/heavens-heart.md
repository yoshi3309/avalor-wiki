## At the foot of the mountains

[[Heaven's Heart]] is known for its traditions of paladin training and scholarship near the [[Spine of the World]]. Its influence reaches beyond the surrounding settlement through travelers, religious houses, and the preservation of the kingdom's historical records.

## The teaching of Lowkee

The order of [[The Cathedral Paladins|Cathedral Paladins]] traces its origins to [[Lowkee]], one of [[The Six]], and honors the ideals associated with Tyr, Helm, and Torm. Lowkee's most enduring teaching placed the protection of those who cannot defend themselves above simple obedience to authority.

The paladins who train here inherit obligations rooted in that conviction. Their service has, at times, placed them in conflict with unjust commands, including during the historical persecution of drow families.

## A place of learning

[[The Great Library of Aldus Greystone]] preserves historical and theological scholarship. Its collections have been especially important in an age when the recovery of the true Black Tower history has forced the kingdom to reconsider once-trusted accounts.

The nearby [[The Black Tower#the-black-stair|Black Stair]] is the difficult mountain ascent **to the Black Tower**, not an approach to Heaven's Heart. The sanctuary lies nearby in the same mountainous region.

## Sister institutions of the faith

The paladin traditions here share the faiths of Tyr, Helm, and Torm with [[The Great Cathedral]] in [[Cathedral Ward]], Renada, though the two institutions serve distinct communities and duties.

## Companions who trained here

During a visit from Renada's High Priest [[Astronoph]], the young [[Lavitz Shawe]] was encouraged to enter the paladin order. He trained alongside [[fo-gurn|Fo’Gurn]], a brass dragonborn who became one of his closest friends. Fo’Gurn's later sacrifice against a hydra became an important part of the fellowship's public memory.
