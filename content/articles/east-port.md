## A harbor built for readiness

Ships visit **East Port** to trade, but commerce is not the reason the settlement commands royal attention. Its harbor serves as a principal dock for **Avalor's eastern navy**, making the town part of the kingdom's maritime defenses as well as its economy.

Sailors share the waterfront with soldiers, traders, repair crews, and families whose daily routines follow the movements of the fleet. Where another port may measure a good season in sales, East Port must also count ships made ready to sail.

## The guarded eastern approaches

The fleet and its defenses stand as a deterrent against threats associated with **eastern raiding peoples**, **nomadic groups farther north**, and dangers connected with the **fey to the west**. These are concerns remembered by those tasked with protecting the eastern approaches, not evidence that every neighboring community is hostile.

Goods still travel onward from East Port through the kingdom's inland network, including routes toward [[Stannisport]]. The place is a reminder that military harbors are also lived-in towns, with inhabitants whose livelihoods continue whether or not the warships put to sea.
