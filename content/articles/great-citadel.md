## The lion above the river

The **Great Citadel** rises on elevated ground beside the River Rhys and dominates the approaches to [[Renada]]. Its stonework has become inseparable from the image of the Avalorian crown, though the city beneath it existed before a united kingdom was proclaimed.

Rynhold Frozenroar chose Renada as the new realm's capital rather than simply expanding the earlier royal seat of Kroubia. The first citadel was built during his founding reign. What stands today bears the additions and alterations of later generations; a residence that has served many sovereigns cannot remain unchanged in every stone.

## A working seat of government

The citadel is a home for the royal household, but also an institution through which government operates. Councillors advise the crown, officers convey instructions, and petitioners seek audiences. Decisions taken behind its walls can affect communities whose inhabitants will never enter them.

The advice of [[The Aegis]] belongs to the same political world. Yet government is maintained by more than councils and royal ceremony. Clerks copy records, suppliers bring provisions, repairs are made, guests receive meals, and laborers sustain the visible grandeur of the court.

These occupations seldom command the attention granted to a sovereign's speech. Without them, the speeches could not be delivered.

## Crownrise and the river below

From **Crownrise**, the citadel overlooks the city rather than enclosing it. Its approaches pass residences and offices before descending toward Old Renada and the Great Stone Bridge. From above, the roads may appear as lines of administration; from the working riverfront, they mark hours of travel and labor.

[[Lavitz Shawe]], raised in the poor **Mudsteps** quarter, now serves as Lord Marshal. His appointment has changed how many inhabitants understand the distance between the citadel's heights and the streets below.

## More than a monument

Some citizens look upward with pride, others with expectation or resentment. Protection and power may be felt differently by those who live under the same banner.

A history of the Great Citadel that records only its towers has described a building. To understand its place in Avalor requires the lives and institutions sustained beneath them.
