## The library beneath the mountains

**The Great Library of Aldus Greystone** stands in [[Heaven's Heart]] near the [[Spine of the World]]. Its collections preserve historical and theological materials that have shaped Avalor's understanding of its founding, its faiths, and the deeds of those who came before.

For much of its existence, the library's name appeared to honor one of the kingdom's greatest early healers. The revelations concerning Aldus Greystone III transformed that inheritance.

The library has retained its name, even as the truth of the man it commemorates has been corrected.

## The weight of a false account

For centuries, official histories accused [[Jaznaar]] of betraying [[The Six]] at the [[Black Tower]]. In the corrected record, it was Aldus himself who undertook the catastrophic experiments, later becoming known as **Cid the Blood King**.

The persistence of the false story cannot be attributed to a single mistaken manuscript. Its repetitions spread through institutions that believed themselves to be guarding a settled truth. The library's collections are consequently evidence not only of what earlier generations knew but of how an error acquired authority.

The difficult work of preservation requires keeping records even when they reveal that an institution has been wrong.

## A plaque removed from Rynhold Park

In [[Rynhold Park]] at Renada, the statue of Aldus Greystone III remains standing. Its former celebratory inscription, however, was removed after the truth emerged.

The original plaque was transferred to the **historical collections of the Great Library**, while a new inscription at the statue acknowledges the identification of Aldus with Cid and the correction of Jaznaar's record.

The transfer was not an attempt to hide the old words. It preserved material evidence of the history Avalor once taught and the errors that history perpetuated.

## Inquiry rather than erasure

The question facing the library is not whether difficult evidence should survive, but how it should be explained. Scholars distinguish the original context of a document, the motives of those who wrote it, and the decisions of institutions that preserved or repeated it.

The [[Royal Commission of Historical Inquiry|Royal Commission]] and its scholars have urged the kingdom to recognize that the authority of an old text cannot replace investigation. The library's role in that work is as essential as any newly discovered manuscript.

## Different work from Shamman’s Tower

The library is a center for preserving and studying records. [[Shamman’s Tower]], revived by [[Tel'Lora Ebbenflow]], is an institution of arcane education and inquiry off the eastern coast. Both value knowledge, but their purposes should not be collapsed into a single school.

The Great Library's enduring task is to preserve what survives—including evidence that challenges the stories the kingdom would prefer to believe.
