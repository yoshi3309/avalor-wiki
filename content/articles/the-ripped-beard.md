## A name without a complete tale

The **Ripped Beard** appears among the notable early dangers faced by [[The Misfits]], alongside the living Moonville Manor and the wizard Noki.

Its name has endured, but the available public record does **not establish whether the Ripped Beard was an individual, creature, band, or some other kind of threat**. To classify it without evidence would create a false certainty at the very point where the chronicle is incomplete.

## Not every adventure belonged to Cid

The Misfits' later victory over Cid the Blood King has encouraged some storytellers to treat every earlier confrontation as a preliminary move in his design.

The Living Chronicle cautions otherwise. The early fellowship traveled, took commissions, met strangers, and encountered dangers whose histories were not all connected to the Blood King.

The Ripped Beard belongs to those travels, but the record does not identify a link to Cid, an origin in the Black Tower, or a shared command with the company's other enemies.

## What a responsible archive preserves

The Royal Commission has retained the Ripped Beard's name instead of removing it because the details are incomplete. That choice matters: missing evidence should not erase an event already attested by the surviving narrative.

At the same time, readers should distinguish **the fact that the encounter was remembered** from later imaginative accounts of its appearance or defeat.

Until further testimony is authorized or found, the Ripped Beard remains one of the unfinished entries of the Misfits' history—important enough to be named, and too poorly documented to be made into a longer legend by invention.
