## A kingdom of many peoples

Avalor is often described through its royal line, but the kingdom is more than the history of its crowns. Merchants follow the River Rhys, farmers supply the Royal Heartlands, soldiers guard the northern approaches, and communities of many ancestries and customs share the banner of the lion. The royal court sits in [[Renada]], but the character of the realm is found well beyond the citadel's walls.

## From divided crowns to a single banner

Before Avalor existed, Rynhold Frozenroar ruled **Kroubia**, one of several northern kingdoms. When the mortal conqueror Lord Osmo threatened their independence, Rynhold assembled a coalition of rulers whose old rivalries made alliance difficult. The struggle culminated at [[Heroes Hill]], where Lord Bradgem Glav killed Osmo and received mortal wounds in the act.

Victory did not end those rivalries. Through negotiation, shared military interests, and political compromise recorded in [[The Unification of Avalor]], Rynhold established Avalor in **1 After Founding (AF)**. The former realms retained distinct traditions and local obligations within the new kingdom.

## The crown and the Aegis

The king retains broad sovereign authority, exercised with the counsel of [[The Aegis]], an eight-seat council whose offices oversee different needs of the realm. [[The Four Sheriffdoms|Four crown-appointed sheriffs]] administer its major regions. Their powers are real, but so are the rights of local communities and the customs established long before unification.

The present monarch, [[King Gradvum Frozenroar]], is widely regarded as approachable; [[Queen Melmiri]] oversees much of the kingdom's everyday administration. Together they govern during a period of growing international importance.

## A kingdom reshaped by memory

For centuries, the drow founding hero [[Jaznaar]] was wrongly remembered as the Betrayer. The truth emerged after the defeat of [[Cid the Blood King]] by [[The Misfits]], obliging Avalor's institutions to confront the harm done by their own histories. The correction of that record and the appointment of [[Lavitz Shawe]] as Lord Marshal have helped transform the place of drow citizens in public life, though inherited prejudice has not vanished overnight.

The years after Cid's defeat are described by historians as the **Age of Ascendancy**. Trade and diplomacy have expanded, and Avalor's champions are known far beyond its borders. Prosperity has brought new expectations, and the kingdom must decide how widely its advantages will be shared.

## The land beyond the royal roads

The kingdom's landscapes are as varied as its people. The ancient [[Forest of the Fey]] preserves the memory of elven communities and their negotiated rights; [[Denzians Forest]] holds a separate tradition of a wizard, lost treasure seekers, and disputed relics. The northern [[Great Desert]] bears the long consequences of the Black Tower catastrophe, while the [[Dead Marshes]] recall injuries from more than one age. Above the approaches to [[Heaven's Heart]], the [[Spine of the World]] holds both places of sanctuary and the mountain site of that disaster.

These regions are more than obstacles between settlements. Each has its own history, inhabitants, and claims upon the memory of Avalor.

Smaller landscapes, including the **King's Forest**, **Forest of the Westfold**, and **the Drought**, are gathered in [[The Geography of Avalor]], where travelers can follow the names without needing a separate chronicle for each.


## Strongholds and contested shores

The [[Great Citadel]] anchors royal government in Renada; the [[Great Fork Tower]] trains the realm's soldiers. Farther from the capital, [[Land's End]] helps secure the Northern March. On the eastern peninsula, [[Karhold]] maintains independent institutions despite the crown's disputed territorial claim. Such places demonstrate that administration, defense, and allegiance are not identical matters.


## The coastal trading towns

Along the coast, [[Cove City]] and [[Tinsel]], once rivals, share the rich and dangerous kraken trade. [[Siesta Ford]] is known for its beaches, fine food and a royal retreat, while [[Stems Port]] remains a freeport notorious for piracy and smuggling.

Beyond them, [[East Port]] serves the eastern navy and [[Osmostown]] the western fleet. [[Port Orange]] became a cosmopolitan harbor after beginning as a wartime fort; [[River City]] supplies fish to the heartlands. The busy quays of [[Stannisport]] and the lake harbor of [[Tarnapolus]] distribute goods from different routes, while [[Taveras]] draws artists to the River Rhys. Inland, [[Jogar Village]] stands above the ruins of Illmire and fortified [[Briyan's Autumn]] watches the eastern approaches.

Smaller communities have stories too: [[The Smaller Settlements of Avalor#alexiverde|Alexiverde]], [[The Smaller Settlements of Avalor#ferrymen-village|Ferrymen Village]], and [[The Smaller Settlements of Avalor#olivruch|Olivruch]] each deserve their place in the kingdom's memory.
