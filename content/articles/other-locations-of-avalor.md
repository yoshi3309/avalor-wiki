## Places in the travelers' accounts

Not every place linked to Avalor's later histories is as well described as Renada or the great ports. Among the places preserved in accounts of the operatives recruited for the **[[Blackwater Bay]]** undertaking are the drowned delta ruins of **Vey'thal** and the ancient **Tide Gate** beneath Renada. The precise position of Vey'thal is uncharted; the Gate lies beneath Renada without a public survey of its concealed access. The town of [[lesser-settlements-of-avalor#phandalin|Phandalin]], also associated with the travelers, is recorded with Avalor's smaller settlements.

These accounts preserve what is known about the places without assigning them unsupported coordinates, borders, or political jurisdictions.

## Vey'thal

**Vey'thal** is known through its **delta caverns and submerged ruins**, explored by the sea-elven archaeologist **[[Naeris Thorne]]**. During a solitary dive into one ruin, a collapsing archway trapped Naeris beneath the water. He remembered seeing a second version of himself beside the fallen stone before awakening ashore hours later.

The encounter marked the beginning of the strange echo that eventually led others to call him an Echo Knight. For the fuller history, see **[[Naeris Thorne]]**.

## The Tide Gate

The **Tide Gate** was an ancient magical passage reached **beneath [[Renada]]**, prepared for use by **[[Tel'Lora Ebbenflow]]** when five operatives needed to travel discreetly toward the danger at Blackwater Bay in **2020 AF**.

Inside the Gate, travelers walked an impossible corridor **between walls of enchanted water**. Creatures of the sea and remnants of drowned worlds could be seen beyond the path. A small manifestation of Tel'Lora formed of water guided the company and warned its members not to touch the surrounding water. Each traveler experienced tempting visions of hopes, fears or possibilities. These visions were **not evidence of actual travel to another time**.

**[[Naeris Thorne]]** touched the water despite the warning. That contact was the **onset of Blood Touch**, a dangerous condition that later intensified in the presence of an immense artifact. Shortly afterward, **crimson blood magic** entered the passage and destabilized it. Tel'Lora's aquatic manifestation held the failing corridor long enough to push the travelers toward an emergency exit.

They emerged in a **forgotten, partially flooded tide chamber**, where they encountered the **[[clans-and-communities-of-avalor#the-tide-touched-kobolds|Tide-Touched kobolds]]**. The Gate had served as both a passage and a place of peril. Its shifting visions promised possibilities, but they offered no certainty about what awaited the travelers beyond the water.
