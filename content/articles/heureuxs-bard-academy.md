## A school built after the victory

In the years following the Misfits' defeat of Cid, [[Heureux]] founded a **bardic academy in Taveras**, an institution devoted to the study and practice of his art.

The surviving history does not preserve a formally established school name. *Heureux’s Bard Academy* is therefore a descriptive name used in the Archives, not a purported official inscription.

## The founder remained a traveler

Heureux did not withdraw permanently from the road. He continued to perform, collect stories, and write about the experiences of [[The Misfits]]. His published account became an important part of the wider public understanding of Avalor’s corrected history.

The next generation could now learn a vocation shaped by his experience without needing to endure the dangers that had made him famous.

The academy’s exact curriculum, faculty, selection practices, and rules are not yet established in the public records.

## A councilor among the students

Among those studying the bardic arts at the academy is **[[Myxi Twinefoot]]**, the halfling who serves as **Golden Hand** on [[The Aegis]]. She attends when the demands of royal finance allow, training as a bard alongside her responsibilities to the crown.

Her presence is a reminder that the academy is not solely a place for travelers intent on making music their profession. The public record does not identify Myxi's preferred instrument, the classes she has completed, or any special standing she holds at the school.

## A different institution from the guild

[[The Bard Guild]] is also influential, but the Royal Commission has no settled account of its governance or administrative relationship with the academy. A guild and a school can both serve the same art without being a single legal or institutional body.

## Why Taveras matters

Taveras is **the known location of Heureux's school**. Its precise placement upon the official atlas and its political or administrative classification have not yet been established.

No unapproved map pin is assigned here. Taveras can become a fuller geographical entry when its place in the world is confirmed.

## What a generation inherits

The academy is one of the quieter legacies of a group best known for battle and rescue. Its existence demonstrates that peace can be measured by schools that teach, not merely by enemies who no longer threaten the road.
