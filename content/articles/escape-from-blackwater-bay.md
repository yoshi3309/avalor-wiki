## A commission without a royal banner

The great victories of the [[The Misfits|Misfits]] had made their names known throughout Avalor. That very fame had become a limitation. When **[[Lavitz Shawe]]**, now Lord Marshal, learned that the **[[The Black Diamond|Black Diamond]]** had obtained an object of dangerous ancient power, he could not simply dispatch celebrated champions or a column of royal soldiers to seize it. The object lay in **[[Blackwater Bay]]**, a vast and volatile port beyond the Crown's ordinary jurisdiction. An open intervention might have created the conflict he was trying to prevent.

Lavitz instead gathered five people who could travel without representing an official invasion: **[[Silas Dreadmore]]**, a wanderer familiar with dangerous bargains; **[[Naeris Thorne]]**, a sea-elven archaeologist and diver; **[[mg-the-fixer|MG the Fixer]]**, an accomplished problem-solver with underworld connections; **[[Notfred]]**, a former deputy who longed to be more than the monster people feared; and **[[Ambrose Burntwood]]**, a sorcerer and former Crown extraction operative who had built a quieter life in [[Port Orange]].

They were not a newly founded order, nor another incarnation of the Misfits. They had different reasons to accept. What united them was the immediate task: discover what the Black Diamond possessed and prevent it from remaining safely in the hands of **[[Esogoth]]**. **[[svendyal-shadowwhisper|Sven]]** assisted with arrangements, while **[[tellora-ebbenflow|Tel'Lora]]** prepared the extraordinary passage beneath Renada.

The undertaking that followed occupied **approximately one day**. The consequences would reach well beyond it.

## Through the Tide Gate

The five entered the ancient **[[other-locations-of-avalor#the-tide-gate|Tide Gate]]**, a passage unlike any ordinary corridor. Walls of water held images of sea creatures and drowned remnants, and the travelers confronted visions drawn from their fears, desires, and possible lives. Tel'Lora's watery guidance warned them to keep apart from the water.

Naeris did not. Drawn by what he perceived within it, he touched the enchanted water and acquired a condition later called **Blood Touch**. Its first signs were disquieting rather than fully understood. As the company approached the Black Diamond's object, the condition would become increasingly difficult to ignore.

Crimson blood magic then disrupted the passage. The route shuddered and began to fail, and the travelers were forced out into forgotten, partially flooded chambers rather than their intended destination. The strange visions belonged to a magical journey, not a voyage backward through time. The company had been diverted, not cast into another age.

## The kobolds and the buried outpost

The chambers were home to the **[[clans-and-communities-of-avalor#the-tide-touched-kobolds|Tide-Touched kobolds]]**, whose lives and traditions had grown around the old waterway. Their Tidecaller interpreted the strangers' arrival through the tribe's beliefs, while the more combative Grik challenged the encounter. Fighting broke out, but the five ultimately helped settle the underlying disturbance rather than treating every kobold as an enemy.

One of the people they met there was **[[Skrik, the Tide Blessed|Skrik]]**, a small kobold with enormous confidence and a tendency to speak of his destiny as though it were already recognized by the world. The tribe sent him onward with the outsiders. His bravado could make him sound ridiculous; his later actions showed that he was also willing to act when danger arrived. Skrik became a traveling member of the company, not merely an amusing encounter left behind in the caverns.

Beyond the Tide-Touched settlement lay a Black Diamond excavation among older structures. The intruders killed two guards during their first confrontation, then changed tactics. Using uniforms, confident impersonation, and false transfer authority, they persuaded other guards to surrender custody of a prisoner named **[[Corvin Voss]]**. An encounter with a drunken guard became a chaotic failure of their more violent intentions, but it did not ruin the rescue.

Corvin was a smuggler who knew the city's routes, gatekeepers, and criminal operations. He had seen something his captors wanted concealed. He could not explain the artifact's full nature, but he could lead his rescuers toward it. From an approach overlooking Blackwater's harbor, he offered a choice of entry routes. The operatives chose to look like merchants.

## The city behind the merchant gate

**[[minor-characters#seb-bramble|Seb Bramble]]**, a halfling trader and document specialist, supplied genuine commercial papers, passes, insignia, and suitable clothing. He escorted the company through the gate into the Black Diamond-controlled district. Their precautions included releasing a goat as a distraction and an unexpectedly costly effort to obtain the right coins. The papers, rather than the theatrics, carried the day.

After the checkpoint, Seb departed and Corvin remained. The smugglers' warning was simple in substance: **entry was the easy part; leaving would be harder**.

The guarded quarter was surprisingly orderly. Cargo manifests, inspected goods, commercial timetables, and disciplined patrols gave the streets the appearance of competent administration, even as the armed organization directing them remained dangerous. At the **Artifact's Rest**, a tavern full of sailors and harbor workers, the company gathered news of unusual loading arrangements and a rapidly approaching departure.

Naeris's Blood Touch worsened as the artifact drew nearer. Silas grew wary of the change in his companion. Their distrust was real, but it never became an established betrayal or fight between them. The company continued to work together as the available time narrowed.

## Pier Seven and the impossible theft

At **Pier Seven**, the object was no ordinary relic that could be concealed beneath a cloak. It was immense, secured aboard a vessel preparing to depart under Esogoth's direction. Their attempts to talk their way aboard had failed. Removing the object separately seemed impossible.

It was **[[Corvin Voss|Corvin]]** who proposed the audacious answer: if they could not steal the artifact, they would steal **the ship carrying it**.

Silas's bird scouted an approach from the water. The group reached the stern ladder and boarded unnoticed, even after the indignity of a sailor urinating over the side. Once aboard, they did not announce a takeover. They performed the roles of sailors convincingly enough to reach the people and mechanisms that mattered.

Silas magically influenced the ship's captain. MG occupied the attention of the lieutenant. Others removed the gangplank, cast off the moorings, and prepared the vessel to move. Original sailors remained aboard because operating a large ship required experienced hands. The escape was a coordinated deception, not a single heroic assault upon the deck.

Esogoth appeared at the pier with soldiers and blood mages as the vessel pulled away. His spellcasters interfered with the wind. **Ambrose summoned powerful gusts**, filling the sails and giving the stolen ship the speed it needed to break from the berth.

The Black Diamond pursued. Attackers tried to board, ropes were cut, fighting spread between vessels, and some pursuing craft were destroyed or disabled. The fugitives steered into **the Teeth**, a dangerous passage of jagged rocks. Their success there belonged as much to the captain and trained sailors as to the five who had stolen the vessel. Pursuers fell behind or struck the rocks; the ship reached open water and an approaching storm.

It was not yet safe.

## The ship that rose into the sky

The object in the hold began to pulse. Ancient markings answered it. The vessel's controls behaved in ways no ordinary shipwright could explain, and the same ship that had escaped by sea rose impossibly above the water.

The company discovered that the artifact was bound into the vessel's strange workings. It seemed to reach toward Naeris, whose Blood Touch had begun in the Tide Gate. **[[Jack of Stanleyville]]**, the ghost boy once befriended by the Misfits, had appeared aboard during the voyage. He watched the travelers' choices and became a witness to the danger surrounding Naeris and the object.

They also met **[[minor-characters#stavros-the-pale-man|Stavros, the Pale Man]]**, a captive servant of the Black Diamond who understood parts of the blood magic that kept the artifact restrained. **Notfred treated him with compassion rather than threatening him into obedience.** The kindness won an ally whose knowledge would matter when the vessel threatened to carry everyone toward disaster.

The flying ship was damaged and difficult to control. MG worked at its unfamiliar machinery; the others held the vessel together. When a breach opened in the hull, Naeris assumed water form and filled the opening with his own transformed body. Ambrose froze the water into a temporary seal. It was an improvised solution, dependent upon the trust and abilities of two companions who could not have achieved it separately.

## The Retrievers, Gladios, and Corvin's choice

The Black Diamond's pursuit continued in the air. Retrievers attacked from flying mounts, striking at the ship and attempting to board. The damaged vessel was barely manageable, yet the company repelled repeated assaults.

Then came **[[minor-villains-and-adversaries#gladios-and-shadowthorn|Gladios]]** on his wyvern **Shadowthorn**. His purpose was not simply to destroy the ship. He repeatedly tried to take **Naeris**, whose unnatural connection to the artifact had made him a particular target. Again and again the other travelers fought to recover their companion.

Corvin placed himself between Gladios and Naeris and suffered a severe stab wound in the abdomen. He nearly died. The people he had guided through Blackwater saved him in turn, staunching the wound and preserving his life. Gladios eventually abandoned the attempt and escaped on Shadowthorn. His retreat was not his death, nor proof that the Black Diamond had ceased to exist.

The crew had survived the enemy. Now they faced the ship itself.

## The living Anchor

When the fugitives tried to direct the vessel toward Renada, its ancient mechanisms resisted. Its heading swung toward the **[[Black Tower]]**, the forbidden landmark long associated with Aldus Greystone's catastrophe. Stavros believed the object and the flying ship had become inseparably entangled, though no one aboard possessed a complete explanation.

Their choices were grim. They could remain aboard and follow the object toward the Tower. They could sever its connection and risk losing the power that held the ship aloft. Or one living person could temporarily replace the artifact's role in the vessel's machinery while the others disentangled it.

**Corvin volunteered first.** He had already nearly given his life for Naeris. His wound was too severe to sustain the attempt, and the company understood that he would not survive it.

**Naeris volunteered instead.** He already carried Blood Touch. Rather than surrender to its influence, he chose to use the connection deliberately and help break the artifact's hold.

The separation was no single spell cast by a single hero. Naeris held the living connection. MG worked through the machinery and severed its links. Ambrose contained magical surges. Silas tended the vessel's physical systems and helped keep it on course. Notfred confronted and refused a mysterious influence that tried to press through the breach. When one person's effort faltered, another stepped in.

The four stages succeeded. Naeris endured terrible strain, nearly dying, but he **survived**. He bore lasting physical scars from the act. No established account makes him the missing artifact, grants him mastery over it, or claims that the mission ended in his death.

The central choice belonged to everyone aboard: they would try to return together rather than save themselves by leaving someone behind.

## What came home to Renada

The damaged flying ship reached **[[Renada]]** and crash-landed near the Lord Marshal's residence. Lavitz, Tel'Lora, Sven, and Avalorian personnel came to meet the survivors. The five original operatives returned, as did Corvin, Skrik, Stavros, and the surviving members of the ship's hired crew. Some sailors had died in the harbor escape and pursuit; the successful homecoming did not restore them.

**Only after Lavitz boarded was the artifact discovered missing.** Its restraints remained in the hold. No one present could identify an ordinary removal, an observed theft, or a confirmed method by which it had vanished. The ship had reached Avalor, but the object the mission had been meant to recover was no longer aboard.

The Lord Marshal received the company's report. Stavros remained in the Crown's custody, cooperating with intelligence inquiries under **Katiri Tenderfoot** and Lavitz. The surviving captain and hired sailors, found not to be Black Diamond members, were questioned and released. **[[Beldir Willowrun]]** recognized the captain's seamanship and recruited her into naval service.

Accounts of the final hours also preserve a disquieting image of the distant Black Tower: a heartbeat sounding deep beneath it. That image was not proof that the vanished object had physically arrived there. Its source remained undiscovered.

## Lives changed beyond the mission

The one-day operation ended in Renada. The rewards, reckonings, and private choices that followed occurred afterward; they were not additional hours of the same voyage.

### Silas — a bargain answered

Lavitz honored his promise to help Silas by personally confronting **[[minor-villains-and-adversaries#mother-vey|Mother Vey]]**, the hag who held him under a terrible bargain. Silas woke free of the curse's hold, the brambles once bound to his flesh now forming a removable crown. The hag's walking house stood still. Its occupant's ultimate fate was not established by the encounter.

### Naeris — the archives and the question

Tel'Lora gave Naeris carefully limited access to the Royal Archives, allowing him to continue his study of submerged ancient lands. The favor was real; it did not amount to official confirmation of all his theories. His injuries and encounter with the artifact made him a subject of continuing concern as well as a scholar with renewed opportunity.

### MG — the favor returned

The Crown commuted the remaining sentence of **Salt**, leader of the **Sea Shadows**, in recognition of MG's extraordinary service. The release gave the Fixer new standing and connections. It did not confer an official future rank or establish that the Sea Shadows now controlled the city.

### Notfred — the Dawnstar

At a public ceremony, Notfred received the **Dawnstar of Renada** for service whose classified particulars were not announced. Lavitz subsequently offered him the voluntary office of **Marshal's Envoy**, empowering him to represent the Lord Marshal in unusual investigations and to request limited official assistance. Notfred accepted. The creature who had feared he could never be a hero was honored for having acted like one.

### Ambrose — no debt remains

Lavitz visited **[[Port Orange]]**, where Ambrose kept the **Burntwood Tavern** with his spouse Aelianos. Instead of demanding another dangerous assignment, the Lord Marshal delivered a formal release from compulsory service and its associated debts. Ambrose could now choose freely whether he would answer any future call. His home remained in Port Orange, not Blackwater Bay or Renada.

### Corvin — a door left open

Corvin recovered from the wound he had taken protecting Naeris. His actions had not gone unnoticed, particularly by Jack and Sven. In the days after the mission, Sven made a private approach that offered Corvin a path toward further confidential work. No particular subsequent assignment was established merely by that invitation.

### Skrik — the story grows

Skrik's impossible confidence had accompanied the crew from the Tide-Touched chambers to the flying ship. Lavitz, Tel'Lora, and Sven later heard his extravagant stories and weighed what witnesses could confirm. Some feats were real; others remained the kobold's own assertions. His future promised new mysteries, not a settled explanation for every strange thing that seemed to follow him.

## A gathering storm

In the weeks surrounding Blackwater Bay, signs of unease spread across the region. The Black Diamond faced unrest within parts of its city. Strange storms disturbed the **[[Spine of the World]]**; routes through the **[[Dead Marshes]]** and near the **[[Forest of the Fey]]** no longer behaved reliably. Disappearances troubled the approaches to **[[Denzians Forest]]**, and tensions between **Birid and the United Krahan Empire** threatened southern trade.

Some of these events began **before** the mission. They were not all consequences of the stolen vessel, and no single mortal organizer had been established behind them. The same restraint applied to the Black Diamond's troubles: unrest had weakened its reach in some neighborhoods, not conclusively destroyed the organization.

For Lavitz and his old companions, the scattered reports carried an uncomfortable familiarity. Before Cid's defeat, apparently separate dangers had likewise become part of a much larger crisis. Whether history was repeating itself could not yet be known. What was certain was that the day at Blackwater Bay had ended without settling the question that first sent the five to sea.

The artifact was gone. **The people who chose to save one another had come home.** And beyond Renada, Avalor's next chapter had already begun.
