## A different kind of hunter

The **[[The Hunters Guild|Hunters Guild]]** has a reputation for stern faces, quiet warnings and work undertaken after dark. **Mera Ashenfall** is a reminder that its members need not resemble the creatures they pursue.

A **spirit bard** of quick intellect and seemingly endless curiosity, Mera brings enthusiasm to questions most people would rather avoid. Curses, mysterious enchantments, magical malfunctions and impossible-seeming problems have a way of drawing her attention. Where another hunter might see a closed door, Mera is already wondering what might happen if the hinges were examined, the runes translated or a wholly unconventional solution attempted.

Her methods can be so strange that those standing nearby sometimes question whether she has lost her senses. That judgment usually says more about the observers' expectations than about her intelligence. Mera's inventiveness is not an absence of care. It is one of the ways she cares.

## The mind behind the unlikely answer

Mera is often the thinker behind the guild's **strangest solutions**. She does not approach every mystery with the same tools, and she is rarely content to accept that a problem is unsolvable simply because familiar methods have failed.

The breadth of her curiosity has made her a formidable partner to the practical hunters around her. She may investigate a curse or an inexplicable magical effect while others secure the roads and watch for physical danger. Her work belongs to the same family business, even when it looks unlike the work of a tracker carrying a bow.

The Royal Archives do not preserve an inventory of her experiments, the names of particular spirits she has encountered, or a catalogue of spells unique to her. To invent such details would diminish the more certain truth: Mera's intellect, daring and enthusiasm are central to how the refuge answers difficult problems.

## A warmth deliberately kept

Beneath her cheerful eccentricity lies a woman who has witnessed more harrowing things than she usually allows others to see. Her kindness is not born of ignorance. It is a choice she continues to make in spite of experience that might have made another person bitter.

That distinction is important to those who come to the Wayward Sun after surviving loss. Mera can listen without demanding that someone immediately become brave, cheerful or useful. Her humor may lighten a room, but it does not erase the fear or pain that brought its occupants there.

When circumstances demand it, her warmth can give way to a **powerful, commanding presence**. The same woman who delights in bizarre possibilities is capable of reminding experienced hunters why she is counted among the guild's most formidable members.

## The Ashenfalls and their refuge

Mera maintains the [[Wayward Sun]] **as an equal partner with her husband, [[Malikeah Ashenfall]]**. He is the guild's familiar figurehead, a seasoned hunter whose gruff caution often serves as its first defense. She is his bright and unconventional counterbalance, willing to explore possibilities he might initially distrust.

Their differences are part of the strength of the household. Neither is merely the other's support. Together they make the refuge both a center of operations and a place to which exhausted hunters can return.

A hall that is only a place to prepare for the next danger may slowly become indistinguishable from a barracks. Mera helps preserve something more human: conversation, humor, affection and the permission to feel safe, however briefly, among companions who understand the cost of the work.

## Hope and the crow called Pecker

Of those who have found a place at the Wayward Sun, few illustrate Mera's protective warmth better than **Hope**. Her real name is **Amber**, although almost everyone at the refuge knows her by the name she has come to carry.

No one seems able to recall precisely when the girl arrived or how she first became part of their household. Over time, that uncertainty has ceased to be the most important thing about her. Hope paints, draws and plays with her pet crow, **Pecker**, a near-constant companion whose presence has become as familiar as the girl herself.

Mera treats Hope with something very like a **mother's love**. The girl brings an innocence and brightness that might seem out of place among those who hunt unnatural things. To Mera, that brightness is not an inconvenience that must be kept away from the guild. It is part of what makes the Wayward Sun worth defending.

The Archives do not presume to assign Hope a hidden ancestry or an explanation for her arrival, nor to turn Pecker into anything other than the beloved crow the surviving accounts describe.

## The courage to remain hopeful

The Hunters Guild was founded by [[Faelynn]] from grief, and even its recognition phrases remember a desperate flight toward sunrise. It would be understandable if such an institution became entirely defined by sorrow.

Mera refuses that outcome in her own life. She knows what darkness can do. She approaches it with intelligence, invention and a kindness she has deliberately kept alive. When Malikeah worries that their family's long watch may be nearing its end, her presence offers a different kind of resolve: not a guarantee of survival, but a reason not to surrender hope before the work is done.

The Wayward Sun would be a very different place without her.
