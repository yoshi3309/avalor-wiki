## Founded to defend the vulnerable

**The Cathedral Paladins** trace their beginning to [[Lowkee]], a human paladin of [[The Six]], who founded the order at [[Heaven's Heart]] near the [[Spine of the World]].

The order honors **Tyr, Helm, and Torm**, whose traditions join justice, vigilance, and duty. Lowkee insisted that these ideals could not be fulfilled merely by obeying those with power. The purpose of strength was the protection of people who lacked it.

His **First Teaching** remains the moral foundation of the order and is remembered in its instruction and ceremonies.

## Arms, restraint, and judgment

Training at Heaven's Heart concerns more than martial skill. The order teaches restraint, the exercise of judgment, and the responsibilities incurred by a sworn defender.

An oath can be challenged by a ruler's command, a frightening accusation, or the need to choose between competing dangers. The tradition does not pretend that conscience can make every decision painless. It insists that legal authority alone cannot make an unjust act righteous.

These demands distinguish the order's ideals from the simpler image of an armed guard waiting for orders.

## The sanctuary under Arnaud

During the persecution of drow families under **Arnaud Frozenroar**, people driven by royal decrees sought refuge at Heaven's Heart.

**Jeyse**, a deva leading the order at the time, refused to surrender those who had done nothing except belong to a people declared suspect by law. **Cal'el**, an aasimar recognized as an earthly representative of Tyr, Helm, and Torm, supported the refusal.

The paladins did not respond with one voice. Some feared that defiance could provoke violence and endanger the very families they sheltered. Others argued that surrender would betray their founding promise.

The sanctuary held. Its memory is among the clearest examples of Lowkee's teaching tested against the authority of the crown.

## The vigil of the open door

Communities associated with Heaven's Heart observe a practice known as the **Vigil of the Open Door**, offering food and shelter to travelers, displaced people, and those without homes. Its form varies: some mark it with a shared evening meal, others through longer periods of charitable service.

The custom is not a uniform law imposed upon every Avalorian place of worship. It expresses the local conviction that refuge is an obligation rather than a favor reserved for the respectable.

## Service in the present age

**Samuel the Devoted**, a Monadic Deva who holds a seat on [[The Aegis]], trains paladins at Heaven's Heart. The order's relationship with the [[Great Cathedral]] in Renada is close, but the two are separate institutions.

The Lord Marshal [[Lavitz Shawe]] trained within the paladin tradition of Heaven's Heart. His public role reminds the kingdom that the order's legacy is measured not simply in the skill of its graduates, but in the choices they make when authority and justice diverge.
