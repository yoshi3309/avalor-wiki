## An angel who has no use for pretense

It is difficult to mistake **Samuel** for an ordinary traveler. He is a **Monadic Deva**, tall, lean, and muscular, with angelic wings that he makes no effort to conceal. Where other celestial beings might prefer to disguise their nature, Samuel would rather people know exactly what stands before them.

For all his unmistakably divine appearance, he is surprisingly comfortable among mortals. He listens to their stories, takes an interest in their habits, and speaks to them without the lofty distance sometimes associated with heavenly messengers.

He also **swears**. Frequently. Samuel has developed a particular enthusiasm for collecting curses from different languages and combining them into entirely new expressions. The result can be startling to anyone expecting a solemn angel whose every sentence sounds like scripture.

There is no contradiction in Samuel's mind. An immortal can be devoted to justice and still be thoroughly exasperated by the world.

## The patience of Tyr

Samuel is an earthly representative of **Tyr and Helm**, and one of the most patient of Tyr's angels. That patience is a principal reason he was entrusted with a long and frustrating mission: **the pursuit of [[Cid the Blood King]]**.

Samuel sought the immortal criminal across years of investigation and had **more than one encounter with him**, yet Cid continued to elude final justice. It became the work around which Samuel organized much of his life in Avalor.

His patience with ordinary people is genuine. His frustration with Cid is just as genuine. The collision between those two sides of him helps explain why an angel capable of singular focus is also capable of a remarkably colorful tirade.

The public history records the Misfits' later defeat of Cid and his removal into an unknown darkness. Samuel's earlier pursuit is part of the long struggle that preceded that confrontation; the witnesses to Cid's removal did not determine his ultimate fate.

## The Bloodless

Samuel founded **the Bloodless**, a **covert organization** whose mission was to **discover Cid's whereabouts**.

The Bloodless were not simply another name for the Cathedral Paladins. Samuel's pursuit of Cid demanded a focused search, while the paladins of [[Heaven's Heart]] had the wider obligation to protect people, train defenders, and fulfill their oaths.

Samuel personally oversaw the Bloodless while also training young paladins devoted to Tyr and Helm. He regarded both forms of service as part of the greater effort to restore balance to Avalor and the lands around it.

Those who knew Samuel as a patient teacher might not have understood how relentless he could become when pursuing the Blood King. Those who knew only the hunt might have been surprised to find him taking such pleasure in the ordinary company of mortals.

## Two interventions in the Misfits' campaign

Samuel's long pursuit of Cid intersected with the travels of [[The Misfits]] on more than one occasion. **At the ruined temple near Stanleyville**, after the Stalker had already killed Kel'Maris, Samuel helped interrupt Cid's hold over the prisoners. Anna escaped that disaster. Fo'Gurn stayed to confront the hydra so his friends could flee, and truly died there.

**Later, during the final struggle in the Shadowfell**, Samuel appeared with Anna, who was revealed after the royal audience in Renada to have been **Erevan Ilesere in disguise**. Through the **[[Emerald of Armanti]]**, Samuel genuinely but **temporarily channeled [[Lowkee]]'s spirit**. Lowkee confronted Cid as Aldus, the man he had once known, but the Blood King severely wounded Samuel and the manifestation ended. Lowkee was not permanently resurrected.

Lavitz and the necromantically restored Fo'Gurn subsequently intervened to protect the wounded Samuel. **Fo'Gurn pressed the Emerald into Cid's chest**, fulfilling its intended role in barring the Blood King from returning to the Material Plane. Enormous black hands drew Cid into the Beyond; no witness established who or what directed them, or that the Emerald summoned them.

Samuel's service joins two generations of paladins without erasing their different choices. His intervention at the temple did not save Fo'Gurn from his sacrifice; his channeling of Lowkee at the end did not remove the cost the fellowship still bore.

## Thirty years at the Aegis

Samuel has served for **roughly thirty years** as **the Devoted** on [[The Aegis]]. He succeeded **Bral Dergen**, the previous holder of the seat, who retired in old age.

The appointment brought a celestial perspective into the king's council, though Samuel does not pretend that divine service relieves him of the need to understand mortal circumstances. His place among the other councilors involves listening to people whose expertise concerns trade, law, intelligence, military defense, or magic rather than the direct service of the gods.

The Devoted's seat belongs to the Aegis's original seven offices, even though its present occupant has held it for only a fraction of Avalor's history.

## At Heaven's Heart

Samuel spends most of his time at **[[Heaven's Heart]]**, near the [[Spine of the World]]. There he helps oversee the paladins who swear themselves to Tyr and Helm and brings his experience to their training.

The broader [[The Cathedral Paladins|Cathedral Paladins]] honor Tyr, Helm, and Torm, following the tradition founded by [[Lowkee]]; Samuel's specific divine representation remains **Tyr and Helm**.

His duties also differ from those of [[Astronoph]], the female deva who leads [[The Great Cathedral]] in Renada. Samuel is a teacher, hunter, and royal adviser. Astronoph leads a distinct religious institution.

The two can share sacred convictions without occupying the same office.

## A divinity with a mortal heart

Samuel's presence at Heaven's Heart is perhaps the clearest measure of the kind of angel he is. He could devote himself solely to a celestial mission and regard the people around him as temporary allies in a much longer struggle.

Instead, he takes an interest in the lives he has been sent to protect. He teaches, befriends, argues, and occasionally invents a new profanity when the universe refuses to cooperate.

His mission against Cid revealed his capacity for extraordinary patience and unwavering purpose. His daily life among mortals reveals the other half of him: **an angel who believes the people of Avalor are worth knowing, not merely saving**.
