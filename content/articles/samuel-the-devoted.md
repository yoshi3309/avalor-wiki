## A teacher with two duties

**Samuel**, an **aasimar**, serves the Kingdom of Avalor in two significant capacities. At [[Heaven's Heart]] he trains paladins in an inheritance shaped by [[Lowkee]]. In the royal council known as [[The Aegis]], he occupies the seat of **the Devoted**, bringing religious and moral obligations into the deliberations of the crown.

These responsibilities are related but distinct. Teaching a paladin how to exercise judgment is not identical to advising a sovereign whose decisions affect many communities.

Samuel specifically represents **Tyr and Helm**. The wider Cathedral tradition honors **Tyr, Helm, and Torm**, but these are separate statements: the gods of an institution should not be substituted uncritically for those an individual represents.

## The inheritance of Lowkee

Heaven's Heart is associated with [[The Cathedral Paladins]] and their tradition of **Tyr, Helm, and Torm**. Their founder's instruction placed the protection of those who could not defend themselves above obedience to powerful patrons.

The order's history supplies reasons this teaching is more than ceremony. During the persecution of drow families under Arnaud Frozenroar, the sanctuary at Heaven's Heart resisted royal demands to surrender people made suspect by ancestry.

Samuel's work takes place in the shadow of that history. The public record establishes his teaching role; it does not provide a complete syllabus, a personal account of his earliest years, or an exhaustive list of those he has trained.

## The Devoted upon the Aegis

The Aegis coordinates matters of finance, intelligence, public order, sea power, magical affairs, military protection, and sacred duty. Its members are not expected to agree on every proposal.

Samuel's office concerns the realm's religious and moral obligations. The presence of a devoted adviser does not make every policy sacred, nor does it replace the sovereign's responsibility to rule. On matters where safety, law, and conscience conflict, such counsel can be especially difficult.

The seat is one of the council's original seven, established in the founding reign. The modern Aegis has eight seats following the addition of the High Wizard.

## Distinct from the Great Cathedral

The [[Great Cathedral]] in [[Cathedral Ward]] is led by **Astronoph**, a female deva serving as High Priest. Samuel's office as the Devoted and his teaching responsibilities at Heaven's Heart should not be mistaken for Astronoph's separate leadership of the cathedral.

Both participate in the broader religious life of Avalor, whose traditions are not confined to any single institution.

Samuel is known as a teacher and counselor. The enduring question of his offices is how sacred ideals become sound judgment when the lives of other people depend upon it.
