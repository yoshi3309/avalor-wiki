Not everyone important to a history can be categorized as a hero or villain. Rulers who performed the ordinary work of government, fellow hunters met along the road, and travelers whose actions helped another person find a home deserve a place in the archive. The individuals below have **short, individually linkable entries**; neither a moral judgment nor a lengthy invented biography is required.

## King Gerran Frozenroar

**King Gerran Frozenroar** was Avalor's **thirty-third sovereign**, ruling from **1946 to 1982 AF**. He was the father of [[King Gradvum Frozenroar|Gradvum]], and **died peacefully in 1982 AF**; his son inherited the throne in 1983 AF. The years of his reign formed the royal generation from which many important appointments in Gradvum's court emerged.

Gerran summoned the young elven swordswoman **[[kel-maris|Kel'Maris]]** from Ilyanamel to teach Prince Gradvum swordsmanship. She became a trusted friend and eventually Lord Marshal, the Lion's Shadow. Gerran also appointed Admiral **[[Beldir Willowrun]]** as **Sentinel of the Seas** on [[The Aegis]]. These decisions outlasted his reign. His place in history needs no invented conspiracy or war: he prepared the people who would help guide the kingdom under his son.

## Gomo

**Gomo** was a **halfling hunter** whose reputation and stories drew the attention of **[[heyden-of-the-goldenfoot|Heyden Goldenfoot]]**. They met at a tavern after Heyden and local gnomes killed a Giant Constrictor Snake that had threatened a village. During their conversation, Gomo told the wandering Leonin prince about the magically altered creatures of Avalor's **[[Dead Marshes]]**.

Before they parted, Gomo entrusted Heyden with a **Hunters Guild coin** and told him that it could gain him entry when he reached the guild. He also advised Heyden to seek out **[[minor-characters#silver-of-moonlight|Silver of Moonlight]]**. The gesture sent a traveling hunter toward the fellowship that would become his family. Gomo's later travels and formal rank in the guild have not been recorded.

## Silver of Moonlight

**Silver of Moonlight** was a **female hunter** at **[[The Hunters Guild]]** in Avalor's Dead Marshes, recommended to Heyden by Gomo. When Heyden arrived carrying Gomo's coin, Silver helped him understand the guild's customs, introduced him to other hunters and explained the threats that made the marsh country unlike any wilderness he had known.

Their acquaintance grew into friendship. Silver's guidance helped an outsider become comfortable among people who cared more about what he could do on a hunt than about his royal title. No further biography, family name, or personal rank has been established for Silver, and none is necessary to explain the welcome she offered.
