Not everyone important to a history can be categorized as a hero or villain. Rulers who performed the ordinary work of government, fellow hunters met along the road, and travelers whose actions helped another person find a home deserve a place in the archive. Each of these lives is recorded under its own name, without forcing a moral judgment upon the deeds by which that person is remembered.

## King Gerran Frozenroar

**King Gerran Frozenroar** was Avalor's **thirty-third sovereign**, ruling from **1946 to 1982 AF**. He was the father of [[King Gradvum Frozenroar|Gradvum]], and **died peacefully in 1982 AF**; his son inherited the throne in 1983 AF. The years of his reign formed the royal generation from which many important appointments in Gradvum's court emerged.

Gerran summoned the young elven swordswoman **[[kel-maris|Kel'Maris]]** from Ilyanamel to teach Prince Gradvum swordsmanship. She became a trusted friend and eventually Lord Marshal, the Lion's Shadow. Gerran also appointed Admiral **[[Beldir Willowrun]]** as **Sentinel of the Seas** on [[The Aegis]]. These decisions outlasted his reign. He prepared the people who would help guide the kingdom under his son, and the consequences of those appointments continued long after his peaceful death.

## Gomo

**Gomo** was a **halfling hunter** whose reputation and stories drew the attention of **[[heyden-of-the-goldenfoot|Heyden Goldenfoot]]**. They met at a tavern after Heyden and local gnomes killed a Giant Constrictor Snake that had threatened a village. During their conversation, Gomo told the wandering Leonin prince about the magically altered creatures of Avalor's **[[Dead Marshes]]**.

Before they parted, Gomo entrusted Heyden with a **Hunters Guild coin** and told him that it could gain him entry when he reached the guild. He also advised Heyden to seek out **[[minor-characters#silver-of-moonlight|Silver of Moonlight]]**. The gesture sent a traveling hunter toward the fellowship that would become his family. Gomo's later travels and formal rank in the guild have not been recorded.

## Silver of Moonlight

**Silver of Moonlight** was a **female hunter** at **[[The Hunters Guild]]** in Avalor's Dead Marshes, recommended to Heyden by Gomo. When Heyden arrived carrying Gomo's coin, Silver helped him understand the guild's customs, introduced him to other hunters and explained the threats that made the marsh country unlike any wilderness he had known.

Their acquaintance grew into friendship. Silver's guidance helped an outsider become comfortable among people who cared more about what he could do on a hunt than about his royal title. No further biography, family name, or personal rank has been established for Silver, and none is necessary to explain the welcome she offered.

## Stavros, the Pale Man

**Stavros**, called **the Pale Man**, was an enslaved servant of the **[[The Black Diamond|Black Diamond]]** who understood parts of its blood-magic practice and the bindings placed upon an immense captive artifact. He was aboard the vessel seized during the **2020 AF** Blackwater Bay affair. The adventurers initially had reason to fear anyone associated with the Diamond, but **[[Notfred]]** approached Stavros with unusual compassion and established the trust that made his assistance possible.

Stavros explained that the Diamond had used its workings to contain the artifact and that **[[Naeris Thorne]]** appeared to disturb the restraints through a condition acquired at the Tide Gate. His knowledge was useful yet incomplete. He did not claim to know the artifact's origin, and his explanations should not be mistaken for proof of the object’s ultimate purpose.

He survived the return to Renada. **Avalorian intelligence officers under [[Katiri Tenderfoot]] questioned him**, and he remained in custody while **cooperating with Katiri and [[Lavitz Shawe]]** by sharing knowledge about Esogoth and the Black Diamond. Neither a pardon nor a final condemnation has been established. His experience of being treated as a person rather than property helps explain the trust he placed in Notfred.

## Seb Bramble

**Seb Bramble** was a **halfling merchant and document specialist** associated with a legitimately run warehouse at **[[Blackwater Bay]]**. He knew how people and goods passed through the **Black Diamond's** merchant checkpoint, and was a trusted contact of the smuggler [[Corvin Voss]].

In **2020 AF**, Seb provided genuine merchant documentation, passes, clothing, insignia and cargo papers for a group attempting to enter the Black Diamond-controlled district. He **personally escorted them through the gate** and departed once their cover was safely established. His knowledge and legitimate business made their disguise workable; the episode does not prove that he belonged to the Black Diamond or an invented thieves' rank.

Their friendship had already been tested around **2008 AF**, when outlaws attempted to seize Seb's ship. Corvin sacrificed cargo belonging to pirate captain **[[ros-furband|Ros Furband]]** to enable Seb and his crew to escape. The loss of the shipment and its consequences helped establish a bond stronger than an ordinary trade arrangement. The cargo's contents and Ros's reaction are not recorded.

Seb and Corvin later laughed at the outsiders' disproportionate worry over the checkpoint. In their experience, entry was the easier half of a difficult undertaking. **Leaving the district alive** was another matter.
