## A name feared in later ages

**Cid the Blood King** stands among the most consequential threats recorded in the histories of Avalor. His influence endured long after the catastrophe that first divided [[The Six]].

## Aldus and the Black Tower

The being known as Cid had once been **Aldus Greystone III**, a healer and worker of weather whose determination to conquer mortality gave way to forbidden experiments. At the **Black Tower**, the ritual he attempted threatened consequences far beyond its walls. His former companions interrupted it and believed him slain.

Aldus endured in the **Shadowfell**, however, and his later existence became inseparable from the false accusation against [[Jaznaar]]. Agents serving Cid helped shape a history in which Aldus was honored as a heroic victim and Jaznaar condemned as the author of catastrophe.

## The ending of an age

The [[The Misfits|Misfits]] eventually confronted Cid in the Shadowfell and defeated him. That victory allowed the kingdom to examine its records, vindicate Jaznaar, and begin the difficult task of reconciling its celebrated founding myths with the truth.

The defeat of the Blood King marked the beginning of the period now remembered as the **Age of Ascendancy**.
