## Places known from a hunter's travels

Avalor's royal atlas charts the kingdom and the places its surveyors can reasonably identify. The life of **[[heyden-of-the-goldenfoot|Heyden Goldenfoot]]**, fourth prince of Labos and hunter of the Dead Marshes, reaches beyond those familiar roads. His travels name a foreign kingdom, faraway jungle, a forest of extraordinary quarry, and communities that taught him to hunt.

These accounts preserve **what the traveler encountered**, not a speculative route between places. The names are useful even where surviving descriptions do not supply reliable positions on Avalor's map.

## The Kingdom of Labos

**Labos** is the royal homeland of Heyden Goldenfoot, ruled by **King Geoff and Queen Viviliv**. Their four sons are **Jeothor**, the heir; **Verbos**, prepared for military command; **Seals**, who entered monastic life; and **Heyden**, who chose the hunter's road. His family remained close even as he spent long periods far from the palace.

Labos maintained diplomatic contact with **[[The Kingdom of Avalor|Avalor]]**. The king and queen brought a delegation to the Avalorian capital to explore closer relations, including the possibility of an alliance. Heyden traveled with them but soon left the ceremonies to seek the **[[Hunters Guild]]** in the [[Dead Marshes]]. Talks about an alliance are not evidence that a treaty had already been concluded.

## Chult

**Chult**, across the sea from Heyden's home, was among the earliest distant lands to test him as a hunter. Its hot, ancient jungles harbored formidable creatures, including the **Allosaurus** that Heyden pursued with a hunting party over several days.

When the beast turned on the hunters, it cut the right side of Heyden's face and mane. He killed it at close quarters, surviving with a scar that he would carry for the rest of his life. The jungle is remembered here through that expedition; its cities and political history have not been described in this account.

## Weyland Forest

**Weyland Forest** was the hunting ground of the elusive **Great Elk**, a creature about which travelers told competing stories. Heyden's pursuit lasted nearly **two years**, and the woodland became a place of apprenticeship in patience as much as a place to track prey.

He ultimately found and killed the elk, but his long stay mattered because the Woodland Elves who knew the forest came to respect the determination of a foreign prince. The forest's position relative to Avalor has not been fixed by the surviving account.

## Valiant and the Woodland Elves

The **Woodland Elves of Valiant** taught Heyden the ways of Weyland Forest during his long pursuit. They asked him to read the life of the woods rather than merely follow disturbed foliage, and to endure the seasons while his quarry remained out of reach.

After the hunt, they accepted him among their people, tattooing their sigil upon his left thigh and giving him a **Revenant Double-Bladed Scimitar**. The name *Valiant* identifies the people associated with these honors; the account does not establish whether it names a city, settlement, or wider homeland.

## The Wood of the Fey

In Avalor, Heyden later ventured into a place he called the **Wood of the Fey**, seeking lessons and quarry beyond ordinary guild contracts. He spoke only sparingly of its changing paths and elusive teachers, but returned with an uncanny facility for concealment and swift movement among branches. His fellow hunters eventually called him **Shadow**.

The name should **not automatically be equated** with Avalor's [[Forest of the Fey]]. The archives preserve the latter as a named region, but Heyden's telling does not firmly locate his woodland lessons within it. Its uncertain identity is more faithful to the traveler's account than an invented point on the royal map.
