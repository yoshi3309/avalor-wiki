## Alexiverde

**Alexiverde** is a small town between the **[[Dead Marshes]]** and a woodland remembered as the **King's Forest**. Farmers and woodworkers make their livelihoods here, attending to fields and timber rather than the affairs of great ports.

The town's proudest historical association is **[[Bradgem Glav]]**, the knight who led the charge at [[Heroes Hill]] and died after defeating Lord Osmo. Alexiverde remembers him not merely as a name in royal history but as a hero linked to its own community.

The King's Forest is a name preserved in older accounts. Its precise relationship to Avalor's other named woodlands remains to be established.

## Ferrymen Village

**Ferrymen Village** is the home of the **Ferrymen**, a small community of people of different ancestries who live at the edge of a difficult region called **the Drought**.

Their livelihood is helping travelers cross that region. The crossing is part of their daily knowledge: how to guide strangers safely, what to carry, and how to make a journey others might hesitate to attempt.

The Drought's older name does not by itself establish that it is identical to the [[Great Desert]]. For now, what matters to those who rely upon the Ferrymen is the community and the passage it knows.

## Olivruch

At first glance, **Olivruch** is a town unlikely to command the attention of royal chroniclers. Its reputation, however, is tied to a less conspicuous institution: **the Thieves Guild** is situated here.

That association gives an otherwise modest settlement a place in the stories of people whose work depends upon remaining unseen. Its presence does not mean every resident is a thief, or that Olivruch's everyday life can be reduced to the guild.

The town and the guild are distinct. Until the organization's wider history is developed, Olivruch's small place in this gazetteer preserves its name without inventing its streets, leaders, or secret operations.
