## Alexiverde

**Alexiverde** is a small town between the **[[Dead Marshes]]** and the **[[The Geography of Avalor#king-s-forest|King's Forest]]**. Farmers and woodworkers make their livelihoods here, attending to fields and timber rather than the affairs of great ports.

The town's proudest historical association is **[[Bradgem Glav]]**, the knight who led the charge at [[Heroes Hill]] and died after defeating Lord Osmo. Alexiverde remembers him not merely as a name in royal history but as a hero linked to its own community.

The forest and the town are remembered together, with more about the woodland recorded in [[The Geography of Avalor#king-s-forest|Avalor's regional geography]].

## Ferrymen Village

**Ferrymen Village** is the home of the **Ferrymen**, a small community of people of different ancestries who live at the edge of the difficult region called **[[The Geography of Avalor#the-drought|the Drought]]**.

Their livelihood is helping travelers cross that region. The crossing is part of their daily knowledge: how to guide strangers safely, what to carry, and how to make a journey others might hesitate to attempt.

The name of the region and its connection to the Ferrymen are preserved in [[The Geography of Avalor#the-drought|the geography of Avalor]].

## Olivruch

**Olivruch** is an island town west of Avalor's mainland, visible from the coast on a clear day but seldom prominent in affairs of the royal court. Its reputation, however, is tied to a less conspicuous institution: [[The Guilds of Avalor#the-thieves-guild|the Sea Shadows, Avalor's branch of the Thieves Guild]], presently led by a man named **Salt**, is situated here.

That association gives an otherwise modest settlement a place in the stories of people whose work depends upon remaining unseen. Its presence does not mean every resident is a thief, or that Olivruch's everyday life can be reduced to the guild.

The town and the guild are distinct. Though the guild has branches beyond Avalor, Olivruch remains the home of this one, with rumors of a mysterious **Ragged Man** spoken in its underworld.

## Ilyanamel

**Ilyanamel** is an **elven village within the [[Forest of the Fey]]**, remembered as the birthplace of **[[kel-maris|Kel'Maris]]**, the first elf to hold the office of Lord Marshal of Lion's Bane. She was the first daughter of a prominent local family and trained from childhood in several forms of swordsmanship. King Gerran Frozenroar later called her to Renada to instruct his son Gradvum, beginning the long royal service that earned her the name the Lion's Shadow.

Ilyanamel remains known chiefly through the life of Kel'Maris. Its name survived in the royal histories because of the swordswoman who left it to serve the Frozenroar court. **Its home within the Forest of the Fey is established**, though the precise position of the village within the woodland has not yet been marked on the published atlas.

## Phandalin

**Phandalin** is a small town with a sheriff's office, townspeople, and caves in the surrounding countryside. Its history is preserved most clearly through the life of **[[Notfred]]**, once a Nothic named Toz Reka. After his transformation, he sheltered in those caves until the bard **Kilmos Handbar** became sheriff and brought the creature into his service as a deputy. Kilmos taught him magic to conceal his monstrous appearance among the townsfolk.

The arrangement ended in tragedy when Notfred accidentally killed **Susan**, the woman Kilmos loved. Kilmos revealed his deputy's identity, and the townspeople demanded the Nothic's death and drove the sheriff away. Unable to kill his friend, Kilmos instead urged Notfred to flee. The full account belongs to **[[Notfred]]**.

Phandalin is a town, not a dungeon or an independent region. Its precise position upon Avalor's published atlas remains unrecorded.
