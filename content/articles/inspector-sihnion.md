**Inspector Sihnion** was among the officers caught in the disaster at the **ruined temple of Bane near Stanleyville**. The prisoners there faced captors working in Cid's interests, restraints intended to suppress magic, and a rite that treated living people as expendable instruments. Sihnion's presence places him within that catastrophe, although the available history does not establish his investigative assignment or an earlier career.

When Jaaris fashioned a hydra from the failed rite's sacrificial mass, the collapse and escape claimed many lives. **Sihnion died alongside Captain Funkin and trapped Renadan guards**. Their loss occurred **after** the Stalker had killed Kel'Maris in Stanleyville; they were not victims of that earlier creature. Recording Sihnion by name keeps the temple from becoming a story remembered only for Fo'Gurn's heroic stand.
