**Jinlin Vechon** had reason to hate the man Avalor once celebrated as **[[Aldus Greystone III]]**. Nearly two thousand years before the Misfits' campaign, Aldus's experiments associated with the [[Blood Sickness]] left Jinlin a **vampire against his will**. The dragonborn endured a condition that made the ordinary release of death seem unattainable. As Aldus became **[[Cid the Blood King]]**, Jinlin's desire for justice hardened into a consuming wish to bring his tormentor down.

Jinlin founded the **Wrath of Innocent**, a faction dedicated to pursuing Cid. Its name expressed a grievance that was real; its methods did not remain innocent. Jinlin's followers harmed others who had not caused his suffering, and their captain-for-hire, **[[Ros Furband]]**, brought the captive Misfits before them while the adventurers were trying to rescue Queen Melmiri. Hatred of the Blood King did not make Jinlin a safe ally to people who shared his enemy.

The Misfits **defeated Jinlin in an actual confrontation**. Only afterward did Zauks imprison them in false adventures and ideal lives. Jinlin's tragedy is that his injury was genuine, ancient, and undeserved, yet he allowed that injury to become an excuse for cruelty toward people who owed him nothing.
