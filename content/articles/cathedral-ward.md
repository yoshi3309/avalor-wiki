## The ward around the Great Cathedral

**Cathedral Ward** lies near the heart of [[Renada]], taking its name from [[The Great Cathedral]] and its place in the city's public life. While the cathedral is one of the capital's most visible religious institutions, its district is more than a ceremonial precinct. Worshippers, travelers, petitioners, and residents arrive with needs that may have little to do with the affairs of the royal court.

The charitable houses associated with the cathedral offer an important counterpoint to the city's more affluent neighborhoods. Faith, within a living capital, is encountered in everyday acts of service as well as in splendid rituals.

## The traditions of three gods

The Great Cathedral honors **Tyr**, **Helm**, and **Torm**, whose teachings have long shaped Avalorian ideas of justice, vigilance, and duty. **Astronoph**, a female deva, serves as High Priest.

Those traditions are influential, but Cathedral Ward must not be mistaken for the only religious center in Renada. The wider kingdom includes communities devoted to Lathander, Corellon, Eilistraee, Moradin, and other faiths. The cathedral's public prominence does not erase this variety.

## The cathedral's work

The institution's place in the capital extends beyond formal worship. Those who seek shelter, aid, counsel, and a fair hearing may find themselves at its doors. An institution that promises justice and protection is measured by the attention it gives the people who can least afford to be ignored.

These duties also connect the cathedral to Avalor's more difficult history. Earlier persecutions of drow communities sometimes appealed to inherited authority and religious language. Since the vindication of [[Jaznaar]], the kingdom's public institutions have faced renewed questions about the stories they repeated and the people harmed by them.

The Great Cathedral's responsibilities therefore include conscience and memory as well as celebration.

## Sister traditions beyond the city

The Cathedral Paladins trace their founding to **Lowkee**, one of [[The Six]], and share an inheritance of Tyr, Helm, and Torm. Their principal training settlement, however, is [[Heaven's Heart]] beneath the [[Spine of the World]].

Heaven's Heart is a separate place and institution. Cathedral Ward is a **district within Renada**, whose concerns arise from the city's crowded streets, petitions, and work of charity. The two are connected by traditions of service, not by a shared geographical boundary.

## One district among five

The capital's major districts each preserve a different part of its character. [[Crownrise]] contains the approaches to royal government; [[Old Renada]] keeps traces of the older settlement; [[Lantern Ward]] serves its merchants; the [[Rhys Quays]] sustain its river trade; and Cathedral Ward represents important traditions of public faith and service.

Together, they form a more complete account of Renada than any history written solely from the heights of the Great Citadel. The ward belongs beneath **Renada's one world-map pin**, not at a competing point on the atlas.
