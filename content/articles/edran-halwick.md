## The country that feeds the capital

**Edran Halwick** serves as sheriff of the **Royal Heartlands**, the productive region whose grain country and roads are essential to the life of [[Renada]].

The capital's markets may display the abundance of a prosperous kingdom, but that abundance begins beyond the city walls. Fields must be tended, harvests transported, grain milled, and goods brought to households whose occupants seldom see the estates that supply them.

The sheriff's responsibilities are consequently woven into the practical relationship between rural work and urban prosperity.

## The interests of House Halwick

**House Halwick** is an influential noble family whose wealth is rooted in productive estates and agriculture. Edran's service as sheriff therefore takes place in a region where the interests of landlords, tenant farmers, merchants, and the crown do not always coincide.

A decision concerning roads, harvests, or the movement of grain may benefit one group while imposing costs upon another.

The court can describe an initiative as a benefit to commerce; a tenant family may wish to know who will pay for it. Both concerns belong to the work of government.

## An appointed office, not an inheritance

The sheriffdom is a **crown appointment**, even though its current holder comes from an influential house. Avalor's regional offices should not be mistaken for hereditary crowns beneath the Frozenroars.

The crown expects its sheriffs to administer the regions while respecting existing local institutions and customs. Guilds, municipalities, and established property rights may place competing demands upon a royal officer.

The exact boundaries of the Royal Heartlands and the identities of lesser officials remain incompletely documented. The public record gives no reason to invent an elaborate hierarchy beneath Edran's name.

## The route from field to quay

The grain country is connected to the capital through principal roads and routes of exchange. Within Renada, the [[Lantern Ward]] receives goods for sale, while the [[Rhys Quays]] tie the city to the river.

These places do not become part of Halwick's personal estate merely because they depend upon agricultural trade. The sheriff's influence is regional and administrative, not ownership of all commerce his province supports.

## A question of fair governance

Edran's office is judged by more than the yield of successful farms. The Royal Heartlands is a place where the kingdom must decide how fairly the benefits of productive land are distributed among those who work it, claim it, and rely upon it.
