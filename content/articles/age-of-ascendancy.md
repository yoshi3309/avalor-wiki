## After the Blood King

The period historians call the **Age of Ascendancy** began after [[The Misfits]] defeated [[Cid the Blood King]]. Their victory ended a long deception about Avalor's founding age and helped restore the name of [[Jaznaar]]. The kingdom's place among other powers changed as knowledge of its champions spread.

The name entered the language of historians and chroniclers. No surviving royal proclamation records its adoption as a formal division of the calendar.

## Prestige and responsibility

King Gradvum's court expanded diplomatic relationships while trade and commerce grew. These successes were real, but their rewards were not equally distributed. Ports and established routes often benefited sooner than distant communities.

The celebrated companions themselves assumed different duties. [[Lavitz Shawe]] became **Lord Marshal**, inheriting the vacancy left by the death of his predecessor at Stanleyville. [[Tel'Lora Ebbenflow]] became **High Wizard**, holding a new eighth seat on [[The Aegis]] rather than merely inheriting one of the council's earlier seven. [[Heureux]] taught and preserved history, ultimately establishing his academy in [[Taveras]], while Sven's activities grew less visible and [[Amara]] followed the call of the natural world.

The period's significance lies not simply in rising influence, but in the questions prosperity has brought: how to honor the truths newly recovered, how to protect the people who built the realm, and how to ensure that the promise of unity reaches beyond the citadel.
## A peace shaped by remembrance

The return of Queen Melmiri was a victory for the living, but it came with a duty to the dead. **[[Anna, Lowkee's Keeper|Anna]]**, the noncombatant companion who had traveled with the Misfits and borne the [[Emerald of Armanti]], belonged to the remembered fellowship even though she held no royal office and fought no celebrated duel. The companions' story could not be told without Fo'Gurn, who had belonged to the original company, or [[Jack of Stanleyville]], the child whose spirit had traveled beside them. Memorials for the slain Lord Marshal and the others lost along the way belonged to the same difficult public reckoning as the restoration of Jaznaar's name.

The misdeeds of Aldus Greystone III had endured partly because a kingdom learned to repeat a false accusation against a drow defender. Correcting that record required more than removing the word *Betrayer*. It required the institutions entrusted with history to ask how an error so useful to the powerful had survived for generations.

## A fellowship beyond the royal court

The five celebrated companions did not remain an adventuring company at court for the next twenty years. Lavitz accepted the Marshal's office, Tel'Lora the High Wizard's work, Heureux a period of court service before founding his academy, Amara a temporary responsibility to the royal library before returning to nature's roads, and Sven a promise of royal assistance rather than a permanent court appointment. Their paths divided as duties, loves, scholarship, and distant roads drew them outward. That separation should not be mistaken for a quarrel or an ending to their loyalty.

The Age of Ascendancy is remembered for renewed prestige and expanding possibility. It must also be judged by whether the kingdom turned those opportunities into protection, fair governance, and a more truthful understanding of its own past.
