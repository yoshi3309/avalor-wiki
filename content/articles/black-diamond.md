## The name behind the guarded gates

The **Black Diamond** was more than a band of smugglers and more than an order of forbidden mages. By **2020 AF**, it had become a formidable power within **[[Blackwater Bay]]**, able to protect shipments, constrain portions of the city's trade, fund excavations, and send armed agents after those who crossed it.

To travelers far from the harbor, its name was sometimes carried in rumor: an invisible broker of dangerous knowledge, an underworld patron, a collector of artifacts that respectable institutions would refuse to touch. The more extravagant stories cannot all be verified. But in Blackwater Bay, its power was visible in the ordinary workings of commerce. Guards examined papers at merchant checkpoints, captains waited upon controlled schedules, and cargo passed under the eyes of people answerable to the Diamond.

The network's strength rested upon an arrangement that made each part more effective than it could have been alone. **Commerce supplied money and access. Criminal influence supplied leverage and protection. Occult research gave the enterprise its most dangerous pursuits.**

The organization did not control every street in Blackwater, and its influence should not be mistaken for legitimate sovereignty over the city. Yet within its guarded district, it could make the routine movement of people and goods serve purposes few outsiders understood.

## An organization built to endure

The Diamond's better-attested modern strength grew under the direction of **[[Esogoth]]**, a calculating organizer whose authority extended from commercial matters to arcane expeditions and armed recovery efforts. He proved able to bring unlike interests into working alignment.

Not everyone associated with the Diamond occupied the same place in it. A merchant might care about reliable access to a port. A guard might care about wages and orders. A smuggler might value a powerful patron. An occult practitioner might want access to knowledge forbidden elsewhere. Their differing motives made the organization difficult to confront as a single, uniform body.

At its center, influential members could coordinate these interests. At its outer edges, hired workers and commercial intermediaries might know only the immediate terms of their involvement. Employment by a Diamond-controlled operation did not necessarily mean knowledge of its hidden research, nor did it prove that every sailor or warehouse worker was an initiate.

Behind the visible guards and merchants, the Diamond's more secretive interests were harder for outsiders to trace. Its reach was not the same as personal allegiance: employment in a Diamond-controlled operation did not make every worker a party to its occult pursuits.

## The three pillars of power

### Commerce: wealth and access

The Diamond drew resources from shipping, the movement of goods, commercial interests, and transactions that blurred the line between open trade and smuggling. Legitimate-looking operations could move cargo without drawing attention to the more perilous interests they supported.

Commercial infrastructure also opened doors. A merchant vessel could reach distant harbors, hire transport, acquire supplies and negotiate passage where an armed expedition would provoke immediate suspicion. Manifests, passes, insignia, and scheduled deliveries became instruments of access as well as accounting.

There was no need for every transaction to be illegal. The ability to conduct ordinary business was part of what made the network effective.

### Criminal influence: enforcement and protection

The Diamond maintained armed personnel, exercised pressure through underworld connections, and could enforce commercial demands in districts where ordinary civic authority was inconsistent. In Blackwater Bay, its checkpoints and patrols made that influence tangible.

Discipline was as important as violence. Guards who believed a prison transfer had received proper authorization might obey a convincing order; merchants with the right documentation could pass an inspected gate without a fight. The network relied upon its own routines and upon the confidence that others would accept them.

The same reliance could be exploited. During the **2020 AF** affair, infiltrators used uniforms and false authority to remove a prisoner from a Diamond excavation before passing through its merchant district under legitimate commercial cover.

### Occult research: the forbidden pursuit

The organization's most consequential operations concerned ancient remains, relics, and forms of magic whose dangers ordinary merchants could scarcely evaluate.

Its researchers and blood-magic practitioners sought access to extraordinary powers, supporting excavation sites and elaborate bindings placed upon dangerous objects. Their work required specialized knowledge and costly protection, which the organization's commercial and criminal interests could supply.

The full aims of those investigations were not known to the people whose labor, trade, or suffering supported them. A cargo manifest might describe a shipment without revealing why the object it carried mattered to the people who arranged its departure.

## The guarded quarter of Blackwater Bay

Within Blackwater Bay, the Diamond controlled a district where commerce operated beneath a disciplined system of permits and inspection.

One important approach passed through the **merchant gate**. Traders could present documents, appropriate garments, insignia and cargo papers to satisfy guards who had been trained to respect a familiar commercial process. The organization therefore appeared, to some visitors, almost like a conventional port authority.

That appearance concealed an essential difference. Its soldiers did not derive their authority from the Avalorian Crown, and the network's private interests extended into activities no ordinary merchant charter would justify.

The **Artifact's Rest** served travelers, sailors and harbor workers within the district. Its reputation as a meeting place did not make it the same establishment as the Burntwood Tavern, which belonged to [[Ambrose Burntwood]] and Aelianos in [[Port Orange]].

**Pier Seven** was among the most consequential locations in the guarded harbor. It was there that the Diamond prepared an enormous magical object for departure during the events of 2020 AF.

## The price of forbidden research

The Diamond's excavation operations reached into ancient structures and partially forgotten sites. Their purposes extended well beyond the collection of valuable stone.

The smuggler **[[Corvin Voss]]** became a prisoner at one such outpost after learning that people were being mistreated in the organization's blood-magic experiments. The captive **[[minor-characters#stavros-the-pale-man|Stavros]]**, called the Pale Man, possessed knowledge of the bindings placed upon the enormous artifact later transported toward Pier Seven.

Their experiences expose the human cost behind the Diamond's resources and order. To some of its practitioners, living people became expendable in the pursuit of forbidden power. Those crimes cannot be excused as ordinary hazards of difficult scholarship.

Much of that cruelty remained concealed from the merchants, sailors, and hired guards whose ordinary labor sustained the organization.

## Esogoth and the great shipment

In **2020 AF**, **[[Esogoth]]** directed the preparation of an immense object for shipment aboard a vessel at **[[blackwater-bay#pier-seven|Pier Seven]]**. The cargo was secured too thoroughly and was too large for an ordinary thief to carry away.

The object became the focus of the covert expedition later chronicled as **[[Escape from Blackwater Bay]]**. The five operatives sent by Lord Marshal [[Lavitz Shawe]] reached Blackwater with the help of companions they met along the way, including Corvin and the Tide-Touched kobold [[Skrik, the Tide Blessed|Skrik]].

When the operatives discovered that they could not remove the cargo itself, Corvin proposed stealing the ship.

They did so by boarding from the water and exploiting the assumptions of its working crew. Esogoth appeared at the pier with soldiers and blood mages, attempting to prevent the departure. Despite his intervention, and despite the pursuit that followed through the treacherous **Teeth**, the vessel escaped.

The object later caused the ship to rise into the air. The Diamond deployed aerial **Retrievers**, and **[[minor-villains-and-adversaries#gladios-and-shadowthorn|Gladios]]**, riding the wyvern **Shadowthorn**, attempted to take the sea elf [[Naeris Thorne]] alive.

These efforts failed. Gladios escaped, but the company carried its damaged vessel to Renada. Only after the return did the crew discover that the immense artifact had vanished from its restraints.

The artifact's disappearance left those who had carried it to Renada with no certain answer about where it had gone.

## A city begins to resist

The loss of the vessel did not instantly destroy the Black Diamond. It did, however, demonstrate that its elaborate controls and armed power could be overcome.

Reports from the period around the affair described burning warehouses, attacks upon tax collectors, missing patrols, and neighborhoods that began refusing the organization access. Broken black-diamond symbols appeared upon walls. For residents accustomed to the Diamond's strength, the story of the impossible theft was evidence that resistance might be survivable.

No single leader of the unrest has been confirmed. The city's discontent should not be compressed into a unified rebellion when the known events show separate streets and groups acting under different pressures.

Nor is there evidence that every subsequent disturbance arose because of the stolen ship. Some unrest developed alongside the mission rather than solely after it.

The network's grip was weakened in parts of Blackwater Bay, not conclusively broken everywhere.

## A power larger than its name

The Black Diamond is significant not simply because it obstructed a covert Avalorian operation, but because of the machinery it built around forbidden power. Its interests could turn ordinary trade into concealment, administrative order into control, and specialized research into a reason to sacrifice the vulnerable.

Its full origins, internal membership and ultimate ambitions remain incompletely understood outside the circles that direct it. What can be established is the considerable reach it possessed by 2020 AF and the limits exposed when one vessel departed without its permission.

The organization survived the failure at Pier Seven, though its standing was challenged. Whether the resulting fractures would grow, or whether its leaders would restore their authority, remained unresolved.
