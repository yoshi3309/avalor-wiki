## A city in two halves

**Stannisport** occupies both banks of the **River Juce**. The **Heroes Bridge** joins its halves, carrying people and goods across a river that would otherwise divide an exceptionally busy port.

Boats unload cargo from the south and from the routes connected to [[East Port]]. On shore, merchants redistribute those goods toward other parts of Avalor. Stannisport's importance lies not merely in receiving cargo, but in sending it onward.

The bridge is as much a part of everyday life as the quays. Markets, visitors, and neighbors rely upon the crossing to keep the city working as one.

## A city of trade and faith

Stannisport is home to **[[The Guilds of Avalor#the-clerics-guild|the Silver Forge]]**, the Clerics Guild that took shape with the unification of Avalor in **1 AF**. Earlier fellowships of clerics had served different communities; the new kingdom gave them a common home. The guild welcomes differing faiths, placing **empathy** alongside wisdom and strength in its understanding of service.

Its three disciplines—the **Healing Hand**, **Guiding Light**, and **Shield of Mercy**—reflect healing, counsel, and protection rather than separate churches. Their work suits a port where dockworkers, travelers, traders, and families arrive with needs that do not depend upon their deity or station.

**[[Petris]]** studied among the Silver Forge's healers and later returned to teach. **[[Aldus Greystone III]]** also studied its restorative arts, although his later crimes were neither its teaching nor its legacy. The guild remains distinct from the [[The Great Cathedral|Great Cathedral]] in Renada and the [[The Cathedral Paladins|Cathedral Paladins]] at [[Heaven's Heart]].

The city is often mentioned as a measure of commercial importance. The growth of [[Taveras]], the great artistic city on the **River Rhys**, has led some to imagine that Stannisport's position among the kingdom's leading cities might one day be challenged. Their identities remain different: Taveras is famed for performance and bardic study, while Stannisport's **River Juce**, **Heroes Bridge**, and Silver Forge shape the rhythms of its quays.

Farther along Avalor's wider trading world, [[East Port]] serves the eastern navy and [[Tarnapolus]] receives southern commerce on **Lake Noch**. Those settlements and their waterways have distinct histories; Stannisport's importance rests on the bridge and river that have made its own two halves work together.
