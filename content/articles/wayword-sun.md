## A light amid dangerous country

**Wayword Sun** stands within the [[Dead Marshes]], a landscape of changing paths, difficult waters, and dangers that ordinary patrols cannot always answer. It is remembered not for royal splendor, but for an institution founded where protection was especially hard to obtain.

The settlement's name is closely associated with [[Faelynn]], the wood elf ranger called the Night Mistress and remembered among [[The Six]]. Her skill in the wilderness later became the foundation for work that endured beyond the adventures of the founding fellowship.

## Named for a sunrise

In her youth, Faelynn watched the morning sun reach the east-facing homes of her woodland village. That same sunrise ended the fiend's enchantment that had forced her to destroy the chieftess and undo the wards protecting her people.

She eventually defeated the fiend and, after traveling with the heroes remembered as [[The Six]], returned to the work of protecting others. In the marshes she found an abandoned shelter and named it **Wayword Sun**, holding close the memory of the morning that had restored her freedom.

What began as one ranger's place of refuge drew other hunters. Together they founded [[The Hunters Guild]], making the shelter a base from which to defend travelers and communities who could not easily summon a royal garrison.

## The hunters' work

The guild's duties extend beyond confronting beasts. Its members must recognize dangers, understand unstable terrain, guide travelers, and judge when intervention is needed. Their protection can mean most to people whose appeals might never reach [[Renada]] or a distant royal garrison.

Travelers' accounts also praise the **Leaking Bucket Inn** as a welcome refuge in the wider marshes. Whether its roads meet the routes through Wayword Sun is unclear from surviving descriptions of the region.

## An institution that endures

The Dead Marshes were harmed during the founding wars and again in the aftermath of the Black Tower catastrophe. The dangers did not end when those events passed into history. Neither did the need for people willing to face them.

Wayword Sun preserves an important truth about Avalor: its protection has never belonged solely to kings and soldiers. Some of the kingdom's most necessary guardians work beyond its walls.

## The present leadership

The Hunters Guild continues today under [[Malikeah]]. The endurance of its work is part of Wayword Sun's present character, not merely a memory of Faelynn's founding.
