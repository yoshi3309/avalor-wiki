## Older than the first king

**Old Renada** preserves a simple truth that can disappear beneath the lion banners: the city existed before the kingdom whose capital it became.

When Rynhold Frozenroar and the other northern rulers negotiated their union, Renada was not an empty site waiting to receive a citadel. Families already lived there. Routes of exchange had developed, and streets followed the needs of a community older than the royal court.

## Streets that survived a change of purpose

The district retains irregular streets, older workshops, family dwellings, and small courts among more recent construction. Their continued presence does not establish that every building has stood since the founding. It does demonstrate that the city accumulated its history rather than replacing it entirely when the crown arrived.

The approach descending from [[Crownrise]] toward Old Renada passes from the ceremonial world of royal administration into streets whose arrangement owes less to the requirements of a procession.

## The choice of a capital

Rynhold did not make Kroubia's established seat the capital of his newly united kingdom. By choosing Renada, which had already possessed its own community and connections, he signaled the formation of a political union rather than the simple expansion of his former realm.

That decision brought opportunity, construction, and new inhabitants. The surviving royal accounts preserve less of the negotiations and disruptions experienced by the settlement's older households. A history concerned only with the first citadel will therefore overlook much of what made the city possible.

## Inheritance without uniformity

Old Renada's people are not relics of a vanished age. Its streets continue to support work, residence, and daily exchange in the modern capital.

The district reminds visitors that a kingdom may be founded on a particular date without its cities, customs, and communities beginning their lives upon the same morning.
